Indeed pricing starts at $0 for organic job posts and scales to $5.00+ per click for Sponsored Jobs in competitive markets. Employers who lean on Indeed as their primary recruiting channel hit three distinct cost tiers, and the gaps between them catch most teams off guard. With 32.34% of the job board market according to [6sense](https://6sense.com/tech/job-board/indeed-market-share) (2025), Indeed ranks as the largest job board globally, though that dominance means steep competition for ad placement.

This guide breaks down every cost layer: the Indeed sponsored jobs pricing model (CPC auction), the Resume database ($120-$300/mo), the Professional platform ($400/mo), and the hidden minimums that quietly inflate your bill. We also weigh those costs against ZipRecruiter, LinkedIn, and AI sourcing alternatives. If you're [evaluating Indeed alternatives](/blog/indeed-alternatives/) alongside other recruiting software, this guide covers the pricing context you need to compare total costs.

> **TL;DR:**
>
> - **Sponsored Jobs run on a CPC auction.** Clicks range from $0.10 in rural markets to $5.00+ for competitive tech roles, with a $5/day minimum and a $25/job floor enforced since 2023.
> - **Resume database access is a separate cost.** Standard is $120/mo for 30 contacts and Professional is $300/mo for 100 contacts ([ShiftNow](https://www.shiftnow.com/blog/how-much-does-indeed-cost), 2025).
> - **Monthly spend scales with company size.** Micro-businesses pay $150-$300, midsize firms $600-$1,200, and staffing agencies $5,000+ per month ([ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-indeed-job-posting-cost/), 2026).
> - **Pay-per-application is dead.** Indeed killed its 2021 PPA model on December 18, 2023 after surprise billing complaints ([SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/indeed-pay-per-application-pricing-ends-dec-18)).
> - **Pin is a flat-rate alternative for sourcing.** [Pin](https://www.pin.com/) starts at $100/mo and searches 850M+ profiles without per-contact caps.

## Indeed Pricing for Employers: What It Costs in 2026

Across its product range, Indeed pricing for employers spans $0 to $400+/month depending on which products you use. Three distinct tiers define the structure. Free organic posts come first, then Sponsored Jobs on a CPC auction, and finally premium add-ons like Resume search and the Professional platform that layer on monthly subscription costs.

Here's what each layer costs:

| Product | Cost | What You Get | Billing Model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free Job Posts | $0 | Up to 3 posts/month, 30 days each, organic placement only | Free |
| Sponsored Jobs | $0.10-$5.00+/click | Priority placement, CPC auction, $5-$499/day budget | Pay-per-click |
| Indeed Resume (Standard) | $120/mo or $1,150/yr | 30 candidate contacts/month, unlimited search | Subscription |
| Indeed Resume (Professional) | $300/mo or $2,880/yr | 100 contacts/month, batch messaging, SMS, team sharing | Subscription |
| Indeed Professional Platform | $400/mo | Unlimited posts, advanced reporting, resume access, priority support | Subscription |

Sources: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/161381/Indeed/pricing/) (2025), [ShiftNow](https://www.shiftnow.com/blog/how-much-does-indeed-cost) (2025), [ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-indeed-job-posting-cost/) (2026)

Free tier access sounds appealing, but it functions largely as a marketing tool. Organic postings get buried within hours by newer listings, and staffing agencies aren't eligible for free posts at all. Most employers who stick with Indeed end up sponsoring jobs within their first week.

Meanwhile, Pin's proactive sourcing delivers pre-qualified candidates they're actually interviewing. After six months, most customers report cutting their Indeed spend by 70-80%, keeping the free tier only for local hourly roles where inbound volume genuinely helps. Those who've made the full switch average 90% reductions in total recruiting spend, per our 2026 user survey. ## How Do Indeed Sponsored Jobs Work?

As Indeed's core revenue driver, Sponsored Jobs are what most employers end up paying for. Running on a CPC auction, the Indeed Sponsored Jobs pricing model is straightforward: set a daily budget, and pay each time a job seeker clicks your listing. Placement is determined by your bid versus competing employer budgets.

According to [ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-indeed-job-posting-cost/) (2026) and [ShiftNow](https://www.shiftnow.com/blog/how-much-does-indeed-cost) (2025), CPC rates vary dramatically based on role type, location, and competition:

| Category | Typical CPC | Example Roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low competition (rural/warehouse) | $0.10-$0.50 | Warehouse worker, general labor, retail associate |
| Average roles | $0.97-$2.71 | Administrative assistant, customer service, sales rep |
| Competitive roles (tech/metro) | $3.00-$5.00+ | Software engineer in SF, product manager in NYC |

Location is the biggest cost multiplier. CPC rates in metro areas can run up to 300% higher than rural markets for the same job title, according to [ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-indeed-job-posting-cost/) (2026). January hiring surges add another 15-20% premium as employers compete for attention during the busiest hiring month.

Budget controls are straightforward - you set a minimum of $5/day and a maximum of $499/day. But the real floor is higher than it appears. Since June 2023, Indeed has enforced a $25 minimum budget per job posting. And as of July 1, 2025, that minimum applies strictly to each individual job - you can't spread a single budget across multiple postings anymore, according to [Job Board Doctor](https://www.jobboarddoctor.com/2025/05/23/indeed_healthy_budget_updates/) (May 2025).

What does this look like in practice? Tech staffing agencies sponsoring 10 roles in a metro market can easily spend $18,000+ per month on clicks alone - before accounting for resume database access or platform fees.

## What Happened to Indeed's Pay-Per-Application Model?

If you've heard that Indeed charges per application instead of per click, that's outdated. Back in late 2021, Indeed launched a pay-per-application (PPA) model, positioning it as a more cost-efficient alternative where employers only paid for candidates who actually applied, ranging from $15 to $50 per application.

Rollout backfired quickly. Under PPA, any application not rejected within 72 hours incurred a charge - surprise bills ran into the thousands. According to [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/indeed-pay-per-application-pricing-ends-dec-18) (December 2023), Indeed officially discontinued the PPA model on December 18, 2023, reverting to the CPC auction as its standard billing method.

That PPA experiment lasted roughly two years. Older guides still reference it, and employers who left Indeed during the PPA era may not realize the billing model has changed back. Teams weighing Indeed against LinkedIn should also compare [LinkedIn job posting pricing](https://www.pin.com/blog/linkedin-job-posting-pricing/), which operates on a different model - daily budgets with algorithmic distribution rather than a CPC auction. Before committing to paid promotion, teams exploring LinkedIn's no-cost option can read our guide to [free job posting on LinkedIn](/blog/how-to-post-jobs-on-linkedin-for-free/). It covers all five limits LinkedIn does not advertise on its post-a-job page.

## How Much Does Indeed Resume Cost?

Access to Indeed's resume database costs $120 to $300 per month, billed on top of any job posting spend rather than bundled with Sponsored Jobs. As a standalone subscription, it taps a candidate database of 635M+ verified job seeker profiles, according to [Expanded Ramblings](https://expandedramblings.com/index.php/indeed-statistics-facts/) (March 2026).

According to [ShiftNow](https://www.shiftnow.com/blog/how-much-does-indeed-cost) (2025), here's what each tier costs:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Contacts/Month | Key Features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free Trial | $0 (2 weeks) | - | 5 | Unlimited search, contact templates |
| Standard | $120/mo | $1,150/yr | 30 | Unlimited search, automated exports |
| Professional | $300/mo | $2,880/yr | 100 | Batch messaging (50 at once), SMS/phone outreach, team sharing, advanced filters |

Contact limits are the pain point. At the Standard tier, 30 contacts per month costs $4 per candidate reach-out. Fine for filling one role at a time - it falls apart for recruiters running multiple searches simultaneously. A single engineering search might burn through all 30 contacts in a week.

Professional tier math improves to $3 per contact, but $300/mo for 100 contacts still limits active recruiting campaigns. Compare that to AI sourcing platforms that search across larger databases without per-contact limits on their base plans. ## What Are the Hidden Costs of Indeed?

Indeed has at least five cost drivers that don't appear on any pricing page. Per-job budget floors, CPC volatility, and applicant quality issues each quietly inflate your true cost-per-hire beyond what the CPC rates and subscription fees suggest. Here are the ones that catch employers most often:

### The $25/Job Budget Floor

Since June 2023, every Sponsored Job requires a minimum $25 budget. That might sound small, but the July 2025 enforcement change made it stricter: each new job added to a programmatic campaign must independently meet the $25 minimum, per [Job Board Doctor](https://www.jobboarddoctor.com/2025/05/23/indeed_healthy_budget_updates/) (May 2025). High-volume employers who previously spread thin budgets across dozens of postings now pay significantly more.

### CPC Volatility

Unlike subscription-based platforms with fixed monthly costs, Indeed's CPC model produces unpredictable bills. A $600/mo budget in March might need to be $900/mo in January when hiring demand peaks and CPC rates spike 15-20%. Forecasting monthly spend is a persistent challenge, especially across multiple roles and markets.

### Low Applicant Quality

This isn't a pricing cost, but it's a cost-of-hire multiplier. Employer reviews consistently cite applicant quality as Indeed's biggest weakness. Some hiring managers report that 97-98% of applicants fail basic qualification screens, which means your cost-per-qualified-applicant is far higher than the CPC suggests.

### Glassdoor Consolidation

Glassdoor no longer sells job postings independently. Every paid post now routes through Indeed. Companies that once diversified across both platforms face a single-vendor dependency, which strips their ability to A/B test channels or negotiate rates.

### Indeed Apply Lock-In

Employers sending large volumes of jobs through Indeed are required to use the JobSync API for application routing. Opting out limits you to just 3 free posts per month, effectively penalizing employers who want to maintain their own applicant tracking workflows.

## Is Indeed Worth the Cost for Recruiters?

Whether Indeed is worth the cost depends on what you're recruiting for. High-volume hourly hiring usually runs $150-$300/mo and fills roles quickly. For specialist or technical positions, cost-per-qualified-applicant often exceeds $500+ because most clicks come from unqualified candidates.

Against three common recruiting needs, it consistently underperforms:

**Passive candidate sourcing.** Indeed's resume database holds 635M+ profiles, but you're limited to 30-100 contacts per month depending on your plan. You can't automate outreach, and the database is filled with active job seekers - not the passive candidates who are typically the strongest hires.

**Specialist or technical roles.** When you need a senior DevOps engineer with Kubernetes experience or a compliance officer with fintech background, job postings attract unqualified applicants in volume. Employers report that up to 97-98% of Indeed applicants fail basic qualification screens - an expensive filter when you're paying per click.

**Budget-conscious teams.** The CPC model's unpredictability makes budgeting difficult. A role that costs $300 to fill in a rural market might cost $3,000 in San Francisco. And the $25/job minimum, enforced per-posting since July 2025, adds up fast for teams with multiple open positions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to post a job on Indeed?

Posting a basic job on Indeed is free - you get up to 3 organic posts per month. Sponsored Jobs, which get priority visibility, cost $0.10-$5.00+ per click with a $5/day minimum budget. Most employers spend $150-$1,200/mo depending on hiring volume and market competition, according to [ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-indeed-job-posting-cost/) (2026).

### Does Indeed still charge per application?

No. Indeed discontinued its pay-per-application (PPA) model on December 18, 2023, reverting to cost-per-click billing, according to [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/indeed-pay-per-application-pricing-ends-dec-18). Employers now pay each time a job seeker clicks on their Sponsored Job listing, not when they submit an application.

### How much does Indeed Resume cost?

Indeed Resume subscriptions cost $120/mo (Standard, 30 contacts) or $300/mo (Professional, 100 contacts), per [ShiftNow](https://www.shiftnow.com/blog/how-much-does-indeed-cost) (2025). Annual billing reduces costs to $1,150/yr and $2,880/yr respectively. A free 2-week trial includes 5 candidate contacts.

### Is Indeed or ZipRecruiter cheaper for employers?

Indeed can be cheaper for low-volume hiring thanks to its free tier and CPC model starting at $5/day. ZipRecruiter starts at ~$399/mo per job slot with a subscription model - see our [ZipRecruiter pricing breakdown](https://www.pin.com/blog/ziprecruiter-pricing/) for the full tier details. However, Indeed's costs scale unpredictably - high-volume employers in competitive markets often spend more on Indeed than they would on ZipRecruiter's fixed plans. For a full comparison, see our [ZipRecruiter vs Indeed guide](https://www.pin.com/blog/ziprecruiter-vs-indeed/).

### What's a more cost-effective alternative to Indeed for sourcing?

AI sourcing platforms offer a different model entirely - they proactively find and contact candidates instead of waiting for applications. Pin starts at $100/mo with access to 850M+ profiles, automated multi-channel outreach, and 5x better response rates than industry averages. Unlike Indeed's $120-$300/mo resume database that limits you to 30-100 contacts, AI sourcing tools search without per-contact caps.

### Is Indeed still free for employers?

Yes, but with major limitations. Free accounts allow up to 3 organic job posts per month, each lasting 30 days. Organic listings typically get buried within hours by newer sponsored jobs, so free posts generate minimal visibility in competitive markets. Staffing agencies aren't eligible for free posts at all. Most employers using Indeed for serious hiring end up sponsoring jobs within their first week on the platform.

### Are there any hidden fees associated with using Indeed?

Yes - at least five. The $25/job budget floor (enforced per-posting since July 2025) catches high-volume employers off guard. CPC volatility means monthly bills can spike 15-20% during peak hiring periods like January. Indeed's Glassdoor acquisition eliminated cross-platform pricing power for employers. The JobSync API requirement for high-volume employers restricts ATS flexibility. And poor applicant quality inflates cost-per-hire well beyond the stated CPC rate - some hiring managers report 97-98% of applicants failing basic qualification screens.

### How does Indeed's pricing compare to other job boards?

Among [major job boards](/blog/best-job-posting-sites/), Indeed sits in the middle on price but is the least predictable. ZipRecruiter runs roughly $399/mo per slot on a fixed subscription, and LinkedIn charges $7-$10/day per promoted post. Indeed's CPC auction is the wildcard: it can swing from $150/mo for low-volume hourly roles to $5,000+/mo for staffing agencies in competitive metros. The trade-off is simple: Indeed's free tier and pay-per-click floor make it the cheapest place to start, yet its costs scale the fastest as hiring volume and role competitiveness climb.

### What is the average cost-per-click on Indeed by industry?

CPC rates track role competitiveness more than industry label, though the pattern holds across sectors. Warehouse, retail, and general-labor roles average $0.10-$0.50 per click, while administrative, customer service, and sales roles run $0.97-$2.71. Competitive tech, healthcare, and finance roles in major metros reach $3.00-$5.00+, according to [ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-indeed-job-posting-cost/) (2026). Because metro markets add up to a 300% premium over rural areas for the same title, location often moves your cost-per-click more than the industry does.

### Is there an Indeed API and what does it cost?

Unlike many SaaS tools, Indeed does not publish standalone API pricing. Programmatic job distribution and application routing run through partners such as JobSync, and high-volume employers are required to use that API path rather than free posting. No per-call API fee is published; the real cost is the underlying Sponsored Jobs CPC spend the API distributes, since opting out caps you at just 3 free posts per month.
