Pin vs Juicebox comes down to one question: which tool keeps working after the search? Pin does. It is the best AI sourcing platform for most recruiting teams, and at 4.8/5 it is the highest-rated AI recruiting software on G2. It pairs an 850M+ candidate database with multi-channel sequences (automated email plus LinkedIn and SMS tasks) that deliver 5x better response rates than industry averages. Interview scheduling is built in, and pricing is published from a free plan to $135 per user per month. Buyers of Juicebox (PeopleGPT) keep paying more. Since April 2024, its Growth plan has risen 81% on annual billing, from $99 to $179 per seat per month, while its seat cap fell from 10 to 5. Add $199 a month for every AI agent on top of the seat. ATS sync requires a custom-priced Business contract, and its sequences have no SMS step and no native interview scheduling. Harshest of all is a public review from a paying Business-plan customer: "The sales demo does not accurately reflect what you get with the standard subscription."

Sixty-nine percent of HR professionals now use AI to support recruiting - up from 51% the prior year, according to [SHRM's 2025 Talent Trends report](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-talent-trends/ai-in-hr). Adoption is accelerating this fast; the decision isn't whether to add AI recruiting to your stack. It's which tool actually delivers once it's there.

This Pin vs Juicebox comparison breaks down both tools across eight categories: database coverage, AI sourcing approach, outreach performance, pricing, ATS integrations, compliance, workflow tools, and agency support. Every claim is backed by verifiable data.

## Which Platform Has a Bigger Candidate Database?

At 850M+ candidate profiles with 100% coverage across North America and Europe, [Pin](https://www.pin.com/) carries the larger and more complete database. Against Juicebox's claimed 800M+ profiles across 30+ data sources, the edge isn't just 50 million more profiles. It's what happens after the search.

Pre-indexed with verified contact data, Pin's database lets you move directly to outreach without a separate candidate contact enrichment step. Momentum dies at every extra step between "found a candidate" and "sent a message" - and eliminating that handoff is what pre-indexed contact data makes possible.

Juicebox's headline number overstates what a recruiter can actually reach. According to its own data-sources documentation (checked September 2026), "over 200mm profiles have contact data available." That's roughly a quarter of the 800M+ profiles it advertises, so most of the index has no email or phone number attached.

Specialized searches are where the 50M-profile gap shows up most - say, a VP of Engineering who previously scaled a Series B fintech from 20 to 200 engineers. More profiles mean more chances of finding that exact match. According to Pin's 2026 user survey, recruiters fill positions in an average of 14 days - 82% faster than the industry average.

**In our experience**, the database size gap matters far less than what happens downstream of the search. We built Pin after spending years watching recruiters struggle with tools that surfaced candidates but stalled on the handoff to outreach. A recruiter who finds 40 great matches but spends a day tracking down verified contact info and crafting personalized messages by hand hasn't actually saved time - they've shifted the bottleneck. Pre-indexing means the 850M+ database has verified contact data ready - the path from "found a match" to "sent a sequence" takes minutes, not hours. In our 2026 user survey, 90% of recruiters reported a reduction in manual sourcing time when switching to Pin. That's the gap that produces a 14-day average time-to-fill - not raw profile count. On Juicebox, that handoff runs through a credit meter: every reveal spends a contact credit, and Juicebox's own pricing table lists no phone numbers on Starter.

## How Does AI Sourcing Work on Each Platform?

Both specialist and high-volume sourcing are handled from one interface on Pin, while Juicebox leans on a single plain-English search box and bills the steps that follow it. Using AI to search large databases, the two platforms differ in approaches that matter for day-to-day recruiting. According to [SHRM's 2025 data](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-talent-trends/ai-in-hr), 89% of organizations using AI in recruiting report measurable time savings - so the real question is which approach saves more time for your specific use case.

Good for a quick first pass, Juicebox's natural-language search stops there: contact reveals draw down credits, exports are capped each month, and agents cost extra.

Rather than relying on natural-language candidate sourcing alone, Pin's sourcing engine handles the full spectrum of search complexity. Need 50 registered nurses in Phoenix? Done. Need to find sales candidates with enterprise SaaS closing experience in a specific territory? Done. Need a niche cleared-security compliance director who's held a CISSP for 5+ years? Also done. Most competitors force you to pick between specialist and high-volume sourcing - Pin handles both.

Sold separately, each Juicebox AI agent costs $199 per agent per month on top of the seat, on every paid plan (Juicebox pricing page, checked September 2026). Per-agent pricing has also climbed, from a two-agent bundle at $300 in January 2026 to $199 apiece, a 33% jump. By contrast, AI agents are included on every Pin plan at no extra charge: one on Free, two on Solo, two per user on Professional, and unlimited on Business. Idle agents still cost money: Juicebox's agent docs say "Agents that are sourcing, paused, or awaiting action each take up an Agent seat," and an agent pauses itself after four days without activity. Ongoing hiring needs make that add-on hurt - $2,388 a year per agent for a feature Pin bundles by default.

Laura Rust, Founder and Principal at Rust Search, has used both platforms and summarized the difference: "Juicebox got me in the door, but I switched to Pin because the product actually delivers. Pin helps me find needle-in-a-haystack candidates with real precision, like filtering by company size during someone's tenure, so I can zero in on the right operators for a specific stage."

Acceptance-rate data? Juicebox publishes none.

Look closely at the sequence builder and Juicebox stops short of the full channel mix. Its step types are email, LinkedIn connection request, LinkedIn message, and call, per Juicebox's sequence documentation (checked September 2026). There is no SMS step. Pin pairs automated email with AI-drafted LinkedIn and SMS tasks in one sequence, so a candidate who ignores email and LinkedIn still gets a text from the same workflow.

Why do channels matter? Some candidates don't check email regularly. Others aren't active on LinkedIn. SMS reaches candidates who ignore both. That 5x performance doesn't happen by accident - it's the result of hitting the right person on the right channel at the right time. Across a sample of outreach campaigns drawn from Pin's index, sequences that combined email and LinkedIn earned **34% more candidate replies than email-only campaigns**. Adding a channel lifted replies there, and SMS is a channel Juicebox's builder can't add at all. And with Juicebox's Growth plan up 81% since April 2024, you're paying more for a sequence with one fewer channel than Pin's.

There's also an outreach volume consideration. Sequences on Pin run until the role is filled, and exports are unlimited on every paid plan. Metering hits both ends on Juicebox: Starter caps exports at 500 a month and Growth at 1,500 per seat. Its help center adds that export credits "cannot be purchased separately," so on Growth the only way to raise the allotment is to buy another seat. High-volume hiring teams and agencies running dozens of searches simultaneously will hit those caps at exactly the wrong moment.

Built for reply rates, Pin's multi-channel sequences pair automated email with LinkedIn and SMS tasks in a single workflow, with AI-drafted messages and timed follow-ups. Expect 5x better response rates than industry averages - [**see how it works**](https://www.pin.com/).

## How Does Pricing Compare?

Entry prices look similar, but the gap widens fast once a team needs agents, phone numbers, or ATS sync. At the top, Juicebox's Business tier is custom-priced, and it's the only tier with ATS sync, hiring-manager seats, and onboarding. Growth costs $179 per seat per month billed annually or $199 monthly and stops at five seats, down from 10 until mid-2025. For teams researching Juicebox AI pricing specifically: none of those seat prices include AI agents, which cost an additional $199 per agent per month on any Juicebox AI recruiting pricing tier. Contrast that with Pin: a free plan (no credit card required), Solo at $99 a month, and Professional at $135 per user per month billed annually, with two AI agents per user included. Organizations spend an average of $5,475 per nonexecutive hire, according to [SHRM's 2025 Recruiting Benchmarking Report](https://www.shrm.org/content/dam/en/shrm/research/2025-recruiting-benchmarking-report.pdf) - so tool pricing only matters relative to the hiring cost it offsets.
