## What Should You Prioritize in an Interview Note-Taking Tool?

Three factors separate recruiting-grade note-takers from generic meeting tools: native ATS connectivity, recruiting-specific summaries, and compliance certifications. Consistency, not just convenience, is what's at stake. Structured interviews are the most predictive hiring method, with a mean validity of .42, the highest of any selection method ([Sackett et al., 2023](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/industrial-and-organizational-psychology/article/structured-interviews-moving-beyond-mean-validity/7CB1F7C86CB0D15328B3F07AD5F964E2)). That validity swings by roughly ±.24, though, depending on how consistently each interview is documented and scored. Note-takers that capture every answer the same way are how you keep a structured process consistent. Of the 10 tools reviewed here, only 3 connect natively to major ATSes - so that's the first filter to apply. Most AI note-takers were designed for sales teams or general business meetings, and the gap shows up in how they structure summaries, what systems they connect to, and how they handle candidate-facing scenarios. When evaluating an AI meeting assistant for recruiting specifically, rather than general meetings, that ATS filter narrows the field significantly. Here's what matters most:

-   **Native ATS integration:** Can the tool push notes and scorecards directly into Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, or Workday? "Zapier-only" integrations add friction and break more often than you'd expect. Native connections save minutes per interview.
-   **Recruiting-specific summaries:** Generic meeting tools list action items. Recruiting-specific tools generate structured candidate evaluations, skills assessments, and scorecard-ready formats built for each stage of the interview process.
-   **Bot-free recording option:** Some candidates react poorly to seeing "AI Notetaker" join the call. Tools with bot-free recording use browser extensions or system audio capture instead - a meaningful candidate experience difference, especially for senior roles.
-   **Compliance and security:** SOC 2 Type II, GDPR compliance, and recording consent management aren't optional when capturing candidate conversations. Verify before you buy.
-   **Pricing model:** Some tools charge per user, others by minutes or conversations. High-volume recruiters conducting 15+ interviews per week can hit caps fast on minute-based plans.
-   **Multi-language support:** Hiring globally? You need transcription accuracy beyond English. Coverage varies wildly - from 3 languages (Otter.ai) to 100+ (Fireflies.ai).

Talking to our customers, the teams that get the most from AI note-takers have already automated sourcing and outreach upstream. When recruiters aren't spending five or six hours daily on manual sourcing, they run sharper interviews - and note-taking tools deliver stronger returns because fewer poor-fit candidates enter the pipeline to begin with. High-performing Pin teams follow a consistent pattern: Pin handles sourcing from 850M+ profiles and automated outreach, a dedicated note-taker captures each interview, and the recruiter's attention shifts from admin to actual decisions. Pin's 2026 user survey shows these teams run 35% fewer interviews per hire. Pin's AI matching surfaces better-fit candidates early, so note-takers log fewer conversations overall - but every conversation logged moves a hire forward. The bottleneck, in most teams we talk to, isn't interview documentation. Getting the right candidates - those who actually fit a role's requirements - into the pipeline is the harder step.

## Which AI Note-Taking Tools Are Built Specifically for Recruiting?

### 1\. Fireflies.ai - Best Value With Native ATS Integration

![Fireflies.ai homepage](/images/urlbox/fireflies-ai-0663124c.webp)

Fireflies.ai bridges the gap between general-purpose meeting tools and recruiting-specific platforms. It records, transcribes, and summarizes interviews across Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet - and offers native Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby integrations that most general tools lack entirely. Talk-time analysis and sentiment scoring add a lightweight interview intelligence layer.

**Key features:**

-   Native Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby sync
-   100+ language transcription for global hiring teams
-   "AskFred" AI assistant for searching across past interviews
-   Speaker-labeled transcripts with topic detection

**Pricing:** Free plan: 800 min storage. Paid tiers run $10/user/mo (Pro), $19/user/mo (Business), and $39/user/mo (Enterprise).

**Worth knowing:** A visible bot joins every call, which some candidates notice. Higher tiers run an AI credit system that can add costs beyond the base subscription. Before your first recorded interview, configure privacy controls - default auto-share settings are aggressive.

### 2\. Metaview - Good for Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby Teams, at $60 a Seat

![Metaview homepage](/images/urlbox/metaview-ai-8aa48733.webp)

Metaview structures its summaries around candidate evaluation (skills demonstrated, concerns flagged, talking points covered) rather than generic meeting recaps. It now costs more than most tools here, though, and it's no longer just a notetaker.

**Key features:**

-   Native Greenhouse integration with one-click scorecard autofill
-   Native Lever and Ashby sync
-   Stage-specific summary templates (phone screen vs. technical vs. final round)
-   Cross-interview candidate comparison

**Pricing:** Free tier: 25 conversations/mo. Pro costs $60/user/mo, billed monthly only. Metaview dropped its $20/user/mo Core plan in late 2024, and the $50 annual Pro option listed in March 2026 is gone. Enterprise is custom pricing.

**Recent:** Metaview has pivoted toward sourcing. It launched an AI Sourcing agent in fall 2025, outreach sequences in February 2026, and an AI Screening agent in September 2026, each billed separately. Its $100 sourcing plan now stops at 200 profiles a month, and our [Pin vs Metaview comparison](/blog/pin-vs-metaview/) shows how it stacks up against a sourcing-first platform.

**Worth knowing:** Higher per-seat cost than general-purpose tools. Metaview's privacy page says "your compliance responsibilities are covered," but its Terms of Service (checked September 2026) make you "solely responsible" for recording notices and consent. Recordings, video included, stay for two years by default on AWS servers in the UK. Inaccuracy was the most-tagged con in a [June 2025 snapshot of Metaview's G2 page](https://web.archive.org/web/20250604013459/https://www.g2.com/products/metaview/reviews). One reviewer there said it "mixes up languages" on German interviews, and another said the bot struggles when a speaker has an accent. On [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/metaview/reviews), one reviewer put it plainly: accuracy is "good but not infallible." Another Product Hunt reviewer (July 2026) faults the missing API and the lack of bulk access to historical transcripts. Mobile app recording isn't available.

### 3\. BrightHire - Best for Enterprise Teams on Workday

![BrightHire homepage](/images/urlbox/brighthire-ai-3c8743a5-2.webp)

Unlike the other tools here, BrightHire covers full interview intelligence, not just documentation. Structured interview planning, real-time guidance prompts, one-click scorecard submission, and equity/bias reporting are all part of the platform. It's the only option with native Workday integration - a major differentiator for enterprise teams locked into that ecosystem.

**Key features:**

-   Deepest native ATS integrations (Greenhouse, Workday, Lever)
-   Real-time interviewer guidance during live calls
-   Equity analytics for identifying interview bias patterns
-   BrightHire Screen - async AI screening for first-round interviews

**Pricing:** Custom (enterprise sales required). Self-serve access and a free tier aren't offered.

**Results:** An internal BrightHire study of 25,000+ candidates showed a 28% increase in pipeline efficiency, 27% fewer interviews per hire, and 19% reduction in candidate withdrawals. Those are self-reported vendor metrics, but they're based on a large enough sample to be directionally useful. Transparent pricing and self-serve access are absent, which makes BrightHire inaccessible for smaller organizations. Teams looking for BrightHire alternatives with self-serve access and transparent pricing should look at Fireflies.ai first, which offers native Greenhouse and Lever integration without enterprise sales requirements.

### 4\. Fathom - Best Free Tier for Individual Recruiters

![Fathom homepage](/images/urlbox/fathom-video-730f8d51.webp)

Fathom's free plan includes unlimited recordings and transcripts - the most generous offer in this category. AI summaries are capped at 5 per month on the free tier, which limits high-volume use but works well for independent recruiters or small teams testing the waters.

**Key features:**

-   Unlimited free recordings and transcripts
-   Summaries generated in under 30 seconds
-   Instant video clip sharing for hiring manager reviews
-   SOC 2 Type II certified

**Pricing:** Free (unlimited recordings, 5 AI summaries/mo). Premium: $19/user/mo. Team: $29/user/mo.

**Worth knowing:** ATS connectivity with Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby isn't available - Fathom connects to HubSpot and Salesforce instead. Manual copy-paste or Zapier workarounds are required for ATS workflows. Summary templates are general-purpose, not recruiting-specific.

### 5\. Carv - Best for Recruiting Agencies

![Carv homepage](/images/urlbox/carv-com-0c0b2a71.webp)

Carv is the only tool on this list designed specifically for staffing and recruiting agencies. It auto-generates not just interview notes but polished candidate presentations for hiring managers - the kind of write-ups agencies spend hours creating manually. Handles [virtual meetings](/blog/virtual-meeting-platforms-recruiters/), phone screens, and in-person recordings.

**Key features:**

-   Auto-generated candidate presentations and evaluation profiles
-   Native Bullhorn, Workable, Recruitee, and JobAdder integrations
-   Stage-specific templates for different interview rounds
-   ISO and SOC 2 Type II certified

**Pricing:** 5 meetings free. Recruiter Pro: $60/user/mo (annual). Agency plans: custom.

**Worth knowing:** Expensive for individual recruiters at $60/user/mo. Smaller user base means fewer independent reviews to evaluate. Fewer mainstream ATS integrations compared to Fireflies or Metaview.

## Which General Meeting Tools Work Well for Recruiting Interviews?

Though they weren't built for recruiting, these tools handle interview recording and transcription well enough to deserve a look - especially if you also record internal syncs, client calls, and other meetings beyond candidate interviews.

### 6\. tl;dv - Best for Sharing Interview Clips With Hiring Managers

![tl;dv homepage](/images/urlbox/tldv-io-204c1ca9.webp)

Instead of asking a hiring manager to watch a 45-minute interview recording, tl;dv lets you share a 90-second clip of the candidate answering the most important question. The tool generates AI summaries and timestamps that make clipping fast and easy.

**Key features:** Unlimited free recordings, video clip moments, 30+ language transcription, "multi-meeting intelligence" for cross-interview analysis.

**Pricing:** Free plan: unlimited recordings with 10 AI notes/mo. Paid tiers run $18/user/mo (Pro) and $35/user/mo (Business).

**Worth knowing:** ATS connectivity requires Zapier - native sync isn't available. A visible bot joins every call. Some users report accuracy issues with technical conversations.

### 7\. Otter.ai - Best Bot-Free Option on a Budget

![Otter.ai homepage](/images/urlbox/otter-ai-a520308b.webp)

Otter.ai offers a rare combination: bot-free recording (via desktop app or Chrome extension) at one of the lowest price points in the category. Real-time collaborative transcription works particularly well for panel interviews where multiple interviewers need to highlight and tag sections simultaneously. Teams running distributed hiring processes will find it especially useful for [remote interviews](https://www.pin.com/blog/remote-interview-questions/) where recording quality and real-time collaboration matter most.

**Key features:** Bot-free recording option, real-time collaborative transcription, AI chat for querying past meetings.

**Pricing:** Free plan covers 300 min/mo. Annual subscriptions run ~$8/user/mo (Pro) and ~$20/user/mo (Business).

**Worth knowing:** Language support covers only about 3 options, which limits global hiring use. Minute caps on all plans restrict high-volume schedules. Native ATS connections are absent - Greenhouse is accessible via Zapier only.

### 8\. Fellow - Most Affordable Paid Plan

![Fellow homepage](/images/urlbox/fellow-app-d25d6187.webp)

At $7/user/mo, Fellow offers the lowest entry price on this list. It includes bot-free recording, post-call transcript redaction, and granular sharing permissions - privacy features that matter when handling candidate conversations. SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA compliant.

**Key features:** Bot-free recording, transcript redaction, SOC 2 + GDPR + HIPAA compliance, Slack huddle recording support.

**Pricing:** Free plan: 5 AI notes and recordings. Annual plans from $7/user/mo (Team), $15/user/mo (Business), $25/user/mo (Enterprise).

**Worth knowing:** ATS sync, scorecard automation, and recruiting-specific templates are all absent. A strong fit for teams that need an affordable, compliant note-taker without deep recruiting workflows.

### 9\. Bluedot - Best Invisible Recording for Candidate Experience

![Bluedot homepage](/images/urlbox/bluedot-so-3480423c.webp)

Bluedot is a Chrome extension that captures audio at the OS level - no bot appears in the call's participant list. Completely invisible recording. It works for Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, phone calls, and even in-person meetings, making it the most versatile option for candidate-friendly recording.

**Key features:** Invisible recording (zero participant-list presence), works for in-person and phone interviews, custom AI note templates.

**Pricing:** Free tier: 5 lifetime recordings (not monthly). Annual pricing runs $14/user/mo (Basic), $20/user/mo (Pro), $32/user/mo (Business).

**Worth knowing:** Very limited free tier (5 lifetime recordings compared to monthly allowances elsewhere). Chrome-only - Firefox and Safari aren't supported. Native ATS sync isn't included.

### 10\. Avoma - Best for Interview Quality Analytics

![Avoma homepage](/images/urlbox/avoma-com-020b75e2.webp)

Avoma layers coaching insights and engagement analytics on top of standard transcription. Speaker engagement metrics, topic detection, and talk-time ratios help identify which interviewers are most effective - useful for companies running structured interview training programs.

**Key features:** Coaching analytics and engagement metrics, speaker-level talk-time analysis, topic tagging, cross-interview comparison.

**Pricing:** 14-day free trial (no permanent free plan). AI Meeting Assistant: $19/user/mo. Conversation Intelligence: $69/user/mo. Revenue Intelligence: $99/user/mo.

**Worth knowing:** No native ATS integration (CRM-focused: Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho). Designed for sales teams, so interview summaries aren't recruiter-specific. Pricing climbs quickly for the analytics features that differentiate it from cheaper alternatives.
