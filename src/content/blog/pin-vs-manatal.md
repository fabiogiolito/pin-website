In any Pin vs Manatal comparison, Pin is the stronger AI recruiting platform for teams that need to find and engage passive candidates. It searches 850M+ proprietary profiles, runs multi-channel outreach sequences (automated email plus LinkedIn and SMS tasks) with 5x better response rates than industry averages, schedules interviews automatically, and offers a free plan with paid plans from $99/mo. Manatal is a well-built applicant tracking system and CRM built for managing candidates already in your pipeline. Expect a clean UI, resume parsing, job board distribution to 2,500+ channels, and per-user pricing starting at $15/user/month.

According to [SHRM's State of AI in HR 2026](https://www.shrm.org/executive-network/insights/state-of-ai-hr-2026-5-critical-insights-chros), 39% of organizations have implemented AI in their HR functions, and recruiting leads every HR function in adoption. Two distinct camps are emerging among those tools: platforms that find candidates for you, and platforms that organize candidates you already have. Pin and Manatal sit on opposite sides of that line. Anyone evaluating a broader set of [candidate sourcing software](/blog/candidate-sourcing-software/) options can use our platform comparison, which scores eight tools on database depth, AI capabilities, and pricing.

This Pin vs Manatal comparison breaks down both platforms across nine categories: core approach, candidate database, sourcing, outreach, scheduling, pricing, integrations, compliance, and agency support.

>
> - **Different jobs entirely.** Pin is a proactive AI sourcing engine that finds passive candidates and runs outreach. Manatal is an ATS/CRM that organizes candidates already in your pipeline.

Manatal offers email plus two-way SMS in four countries, with 200 to 5,000 mass-email credits a month depending on plan.

> - **Manatal genuinely wins on the ATS core.** Job board distribution to 2,500+ channels, any-language resume parsing, branded career pages, and an AI Interviewer in 11 languages are real strengths if inbound applications are your main channel.

## What's the Core Difference Between Pin and Manatal?

As a sourcing-first platform, Pin is built around finding candidates who aren't looking. As an ATS-first platform, Manatal is built around organizing ones who are. Every feature each tool offers flows from that single architectural distinction. Pin's 2026 user survey spans 2,000+ organizations and 20,000+ users. Among them, time-to-hire drops 82% compared to traditional methods. Architecture drives that gap, not recruiter effort. Recruiting is also where HR teams adopt AI first: at 27%, it leads every HR function in [SHRM's 2026 AI in HR research](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/ai-hr-2026-from-hype-to-measured-human-centered-impact).

Starting before you have any candidates, Pin's AI scans 850M+ profiles to find matches the moment you describe a role. Then it runs outreach sequences that mix automated email with LinkedIn and SMS tasks, and it schedules interviews. From "zero candidates" to "interview booked," the entire workflow runs without leaving the platform.

Manatal's workflow begins after candidates arrive. You post a job to 2,500+ boards, candidates apply, and Manatal tracks them through your pipeline. Its AI recommendation engine scores and ranks candidates already in your database against job descriptions. Valuable, certainly - but it's a fundamentally different problem than finding passive candidates who aren't looking at job boards.

Manatal was founded in January 2019 by Jeremy Fichet and Yassine Bel Mamoun, with offices in Bangkok and Singapore. Sequoia India's Surge program backed it with $5.1 million in seed funding in February 2022, according to [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/recruitment-saas-platform-manatal-raises-us5-1-million-in-funding-to-transform-hiring-globally-301479179.html), and the company now says 10,000 recruitment teams in 135+ countries use it. Built by the team that previously created and sold Interseller to Greenhouse, Pin brings years of recruiting-tool engineering DNA to a purpose-built AI sourcing platform. In December 2024, Pin announced a $3M seed round led by Expa Ventures.

Having built Pin after years working on Interseller, we've watched how recruiting teams divide based on one variable: where their candidates come from. Teams that source mostly from inbound applications - job boards, referrals, careers pages - often find Manatal well-suited to their workflow. Its ATS core is clean, the resume parsing is reliable, and the per-user pricing scales predictably at low headcount. Trouble starts the moment a team needs to build pipeline from scratch. In Pin's 2026 user survey, recruiters reported a 90% reduction in manual sourcing time and filled positions in an average of 14 days. Workflows that depend on an ATS-first tool typically can't compress timelines that way. One question keeps coming up from teams evaluating both platforms: "Do I have candidates to track, or do I need to find them first?" Usually, that answer makes the decision clear.

Practically speaking: if your team's bottleneck is "we don't have enough qualified candidates," Pin solves that. If your bottleneck is "we have applicants but our pipeline is disorganized," Manatal addresses that.

## Which Platform Has a Bigger Candidate Database?

850M+ candidate profiles are indexed in Pin's database, with 100% coverage across North America and Europe. Fully proprietary - Pin owns and maintains the data directly - search results, filters, and candidate contact information are all available without third-party dependencies.

Approximately 700M profiles are accessible through Manatal's "Sourcing Hub," but with an important caveat: the data comes from a third-party provider, not from Manatal's own infrastructure. Each search uses one credit and returns up to 100 profiles, according to Manatal's support documentation. Professional and Enterprise plans include 10 searches a month (roughly 1,000 profiles), while Enterprise Plus ($55/user/month) raises that to 300 searches.

Database ownership matters because it affects data freshness, search depth, and enrichment quality. Pin's proprietary database means the platform controls how often profiles are updated, how granular the filters are, and how quickly new profiles get added. Third-party databases introduce a layer of dependency - if the data provider has stale records or incomplete coverage in certain industries, the ATS inherits those gaps.

## How Does Outreach Compare Between Pin and Manatal?

Pin's multi-channel outreach sequences deliver 5x better response rates than industry averages. Email steps send automatically, with timing, personalization, and follow-ups handled by the platform; LinkedIn and SMS steps arrive as tasks with an AI-drafted message the recruiter sends. No outreach response rates are published by Manatal.

Email is Manatal's main outreach channel, with native two-way SMS available in the US, Canada, Australia, and the UK. The platform handles triggered email actions (e.g., send a template when a candidate moves to a specific pipeline stage) and mass email campaigns to candidates already in your database. Mass-email credits are capped by plan: 200 a month on Professional, 1,000 on Enterprise, and 5,000 on Enterprise Plus, per Manatal's support documentation. There's no LinkedIn step in Manatal's workflows.

Follow-ups are where most replies come from. Across 180,000+ outreach sequences that earned a reply on Pin, 62% of candidates answered only after a follow-up touch, not the first message. A team that sends one email and waits misses most of the passive talent it reached.

Multi-channel outreach consistently outperforms single-channel, and that gap matters. When a candidate ignores an email, a LinkedIn touch or SMS follow-up creates another chance to connect. Pin builds those steps into the same sequence as the automated emails, so the recruiter sees each LinkedIn or SMS task on the right day instead of tracking it in a spreadsheet. With Manatal, LinkedIn touches happen outside the platform.

An 83% candidate acceptance rate - the highest in the industry, meaning 83% of candidates Pin recommends get accepted into customers' hiring pipelines - confirms that the outreach isn't just reaching people, it's reaching the right people. High response rates paired with that acceptance rate indicate sourcing precision, not just volume.

Sequences that combine email, LinkedIn, and SMS touches give Pin users 5x better response rates than industry averages - [**try it free**](https://www.pin.com/).

## How Does Interview Scheduling Differ?

Native automated interview scheduling is built into Pin. Once a candidate responds positively to outreach, the platform handles the back-and-forth of finding a time, syncs with your calendar, and sends confirmation. No separate tool required. The entire flow from sourcing to scheduled interview runs in one system.

Manatal's support docs describe no native candidate self-scheduling links. Instead, automations can email a booking link from a tool like Calendly. Manatal's own March 2026 blog notes that Calendly connects through Zapier. That's a two-tool dependency, and every extra hop is a potential failure point. Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 sync is built in, and activities can generate Google Meet or Teams links.

At scale, the difference compounds. According to [SHRM's 2025 benchmarking data](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-recruiting-benchmarking), over half of organizations have recruiters managing roughly 20 requisitions each. Multiply that by the number of candidates per req who need scheduling, and manual or Zapier-dependent workflows eat hours that could go toward higher-value activities. Hours disappear fast.

## What Does Manatal Do Better Than Pin?

Among inbound-heavy teams, Manatal is a genuinely strong ATS/CRM. Reviewers on [Capterra](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/181145/manatal) rate it 4.6/5 across 142 reviews, consistently praising its clean interface, fast setup, and ease of use. When the core need is organizing an inbound hiring pipeline, the platform has earned its reputation.

Here's where Manatal genuinely delivers:

-   **Job board distribution** - Post to 2,500+ free and premium channels (Indeed, Monster, ZipRecruiter, LinkedIn, Google for Jobs) from a single dashboard. Pin doesn't include job board posting because it focuses on finding passive candidates, not waiting for active ones to apply.
-   **Resume parsing** - Parses resumes in any language, with the interface available in English, Spanish, French, and German. Bulk upload supported. The parser extracts skills, work history, education, and certifications automatically.
-   **Two-way SMS** - Native text messaging with candidates in the US, Canada, Australia, and the UK.
-   **AI candidate recommendations** - Manatal's recommendation engine scores candidates in your internal database against job descriptions with visible reasoning. It works well for ranking applicants you already have.
-   **Branded career pages** - Custom career pages with subdomain support, compatible with WordPress, Wix, and Squarespace.
-   **AI Interviewer, Notetaker, and MCP Server** - Launched in 2025, the AI Interviewer runs asynchronous video screens in 11 languages (a paid add-on on every plan), the AI Notetaker transcribes live interviews, and the MCP Server connects ATS data to Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot Studio, and Gemini CLI on Enterprise Plus.

What's the common thread? Organizing, scoring, and processing candidates already in your system is where Manatal excels. If most of your hires come from inbound applications and job board responses, Manatal covers that workflow at a low price point. That gap shows when you need to proactively find candidates who aren't applying to your postings.

## What About Integrations and Compliance?

SOC 2 Type 2 certification - an independent audit of a vendor's data security and availability controls - is now table stakes, and both vendors state it. Pin's public Trust Center at trust.pin.com lists its controls and subprocessors, covering encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, network security, and authentication, with the report available on request. Manatal's security page states SOC 2 Type II certification and US data storage. For enterprise procurement teams and regulated industries, ask either vendor for the report during evaluation.

Dozens of integrations are listed in Manatal's directory, including Google Suite, Microsoft 365, Indeed, LinkedIn (job posting), Zapier, Mailchimp, and several assessment platforms. API access - critical for custom workflows and deep ATS integration - requires the Enterprise Plus tier at $55/user/month. That means teams on the Professional ($15) or Enterprise ($35) plans can't build custom integrations or connect Manatal to tools not covered by native connectors.

Pin connects to 120+ ATS platforms, so sourced candidates flow straight into the system your team already uses. ATS sync is an add-on on the Solo and Professional plans and included on Business, according to [Pin's pricing page](https://www.pin.com/pricing/).

On AI bias prevention, Pin excludes candidate names, gender, and protected characteristics from its AI entirely. No exceptions. Regular team reviews of AI outputs and third-party fairness audits run on an ongoing basis. No detailed bias prevention methodology is published by Manatal, though its AI recommendation engine scores candidates based on skills and experience data.
