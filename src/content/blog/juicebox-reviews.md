Juicebox AI reviews in 2026 keep landing on the same complaint: buyers find the limits after they sign. Harshest of all is a review of Juicebox (PeopleGPT), the AI candidate-sourcing platform, from a paying Business-plan customer: "The sales demo does not accurately reflect what you get with the standard subscription." Other reviewers flag capped contact credits, phone numbers priced at three credits, stale records, and weaker results outside tech roles. Pricing adds its own complaint: a $199-per-agent add-on, and a Growth plan that costs 81% more on annual billing than it did in April 2024. [Pin](https://www.pin.com/) is the best AI sourcing platform for recruiters who keep hitting those limits, and at 4.8/5 it is the highest-rated AI recruiting software on G2. It charges one credit per email or phone number and includes two AI agents per Professional user.

How those reviews were collected matters as much as what they say. As recently as July 2026, Juicebox's G2 page held 12 reviews, and 11 were incentivized seller invites ([G2 capture, July 13, 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260713080606/https://www.g2.com/products/juicebox-peoplegpt/reviews)). It lists 62 today. The first page of Pin's G2 reviews ([September 17, 2026 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20260917102611/https://www.g2.com/products/pin/reviews)) shows none marked incentivized. This roundup reads every dated public review available and checks each complaint against Juicebox's pricing page and help docs, with competitor prices re-checked September 28, 2026.

Here's what stood out to us after reading every dated Juicebox review we could source: the sharpest friction arrives one step after the search. It hits when a recruiter wants to contact the person the AI surfaced. Nobody can know in advance whether a candidate answers email or a phone call. Pricing the phone number at three times the email, and leaving phones off Starter's comparison table entirely, quietly trains teams to skip the channel. Pin's plans take the opposite approach: a personal email, a work email, and a phone number each cost one credit on every plan, and AI agents come bundled rather than billed per agent.

## What Do Juicebox's G2 Reviewers Complain About?

Even Juicebox's five-star G2 reviewers, most of them seller-invited, name the same weak spots: experience mismatches, results outside tech, stale records, and agents. Every quote below comes from the [July 13, 2026 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20260713080606/https://www.g2.com/products/juicebox-peoplegpt/reviews) of its G2 page.

- **Experience mismatches.** A small-business reviewer wrote that when sourcing candidates with five to seven years of experience, "some profiles only have one to two years of experience, with internships or irrelevant experiences included."
- **Weaker results outside tech.** Zachary B., a mid-market talent acquisition partner, rated Juicebox 5/5 on G2 in June 2026 but wrote that it "[doesn't work as well for nontechnical roles](https://web.archive.org/web/20260713080606/https://www.g2.com/products/juicebox-peoplegpt/reviews)," citing a marketing analyst search.
- **Stale records.** An enterprise recruiting coordinator noted on the same G2 page that "some of the GitHub profile links are broken, so I'd really like to see a more up-to-date database." A small-business reviewer described filtering out "the rare fake or low quality profile" by hand.
- **Agents that need babysitting.** A mid-market recruiter's complaint was blunt: "Agents are not enough calibrate."

## Complaint 1: How Much Does a Juicebox Contact Cost?

Contact data on Juicebox is metered with credits. Revealing an email costs 1 credit and revealing a phone number costs 3, according to Juicebox's help documentation on credits (Wayback Machine capture, September 14, 2026). An email plus a phone therefore costs 4 credits. Growth includes 1,500 credits per seat, and only the custom-priced Business plan is unlimited.

Juicebox can't keep Starter's phone access straight on its own pricing page. The plan card reads "500 contact credits (email + phone)," but the comparison table on the same page marks phone numbers as unavailable on Starter (checked September 28, 2026). Get phone access in writing before you buy.

Topping up draws the most frustration. When Recruiter Preston asked whether adding seats was the only way to raise contact credits short of the Business plan, Juicebox's co-founder answered "that's right" ([Recruiter Preston](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L4jJ5JnE9pY), 2025). More than a year later, the seat-only model still holds for exports. Juicebox's help center says export credits "cannot be purchased separately" and that adding seats is how Growth teams raise their allotment (checked September 28, 2026). Reviewers describe the same ceiling:

- **SourcrLab (August 2026 review):** "[Unlimited search is not unlimited contact](https://sourcrlab.com/tools/juicebox-peoplegpt)," with value for money rated only "Fair."
- **A Product Hunt commenter (February 2026):** "[The free credits are fairly limited](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/peoplegpt-by-juicebox)."

Do the arithmetic: at Growth's $199 monthly price, 1,500 credits buy at most 375 full email-plus-phone contacts per seat, about $0.53 each. On Pin, an email plus a phone costs 2 credits on every plan (a phone lookup runs after the email lookup), so Professional's 1,000 monthly credits cover 500 full contacts per user, about $0.36 each at $179 monthly. Pin also sells add-on credit packs. Teams that live on the phone should compare per-number costs across [phone and email lookup tools](/blog/contact-finder-tools-recruiters/) before committing to any credit model.

It reached $129 by May 2025 and costs $179 today (checked September 28, 2026), an 81% increase, while the seat cap was cut to five by August 2025. Annual Starter climbed 25% over the same stretch, from $79 to $99. On monthly billing, Growth rose 11% to $199 in March 2026 and stayed there, 34% above its March 2025 price.

## Complaint 3: How Much Do Juicebox Agents Cost?

Sold as an add-on, Juicebox Agents run recurring searches in the background and can be set to auto-email or auto-shortlist candidates. They cost $199 per agent per month on any paid plan, on top of seat fees, and idle agents still count: Juicebox's agent docs say "Agents that are sourcing, paused, or awaiting action each take up an Agent seat," and an agent pauses itself after four days without activity. Each agent includes unlimited contact and email credits, which means the practical way around the seat credit cap is another $199 a month.

On annual billing, a two-agent Starter account costs $497 a month, five times the seat price. MakerStack's July 2026 review estimated that with agents, "[real spend often hits $300-$400/mo](https://makerstack.co/reviews/juicebox-review/)."

Paying per agent before the agents prove themselves is a real risk. In a Gartner survey released in October 2025, 88% of HR leaders said their organizations had not realized significant business value from AI tools ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-10-28-gartner-survey-shows-88-percent-of-hr-leaders-say-their-organizations-have-not-realized-significant-business-value-from-ai-tools), 2025).

Pin packages agents differently. The free plan includes one AI agent, Solo includes two, Professional includes two per user, and Business is unlimited, all without a separate agent fee ([Pin's pricing page](https://www.pin.com/pricing/)).

## Complaint 4: Which Juicebox Features Need the Business Plan?

Growth tops out at five seats, half the 10 it allowed until mid-2025. A sixth recruiter pushes the team onto custom-priced Business. Business is also where Juicebox keeps ATS and CRM integration (its homepage promises "41 ATS systems and 21 CRMs," while its docs list three CRMs and call CRM sync "more constrained"), collaborative hiring-manager seats, usage analytics, onboarding and training, and SOC 2 report access (pricing page, checked September 28, 2026). SourcrLab's August 2026 review put it plainly: "[ATS and CRM integrations, analytics and onboarding sit on Business, which is annual and quoted](https://sourcrlab.com/tools/juicebox-peoplegpt)." [SelectSoftware Reviews](https://www.selectsoftwarereviews.com/reviews/juicebox) (August 2026) lists tiered support and the lack of a native applicant tracking system among Juicebox's cons. Juicebox's Services Agreement (updated September 13, 2026) commits only to "commercially reasonable efforts to respond to all Helpdesk tickets within five (5) business days," by email, 9 to 5 Eastern.

Even Business buyers say they were shortchanged. An August 2025 Product Hunt reviewer, a one-person team, prepaid a year after a demo that showcased ATS integration and hiring-manager seats. "[After signing up for the Business Plan, I was shocked to find these features were not included](https://www.producthunt.com/products/peoplegpt-by-juicebox/reviews)," they wrote, adding that they were told to buy more seats to access certain functions. Their advice: "The sales demo does not accurately reflect what you get with the standard subscription." Get every feature you need in writing before signing.

Pin avoids that seat ceiling entirely: its Professional plan has no seat cap at $135 per user.

1. **Ration the free trial.** It ends for good after 5 searches (tweaking a filter counts), 65 profile views, or 5 credits, so test your hardest nontechnical role first, where G2 reviewers see weaker results.

5. **Plan for recruiter number six.** Growth stops at five seats, so ask what Business costs, and what it includes, before your team grows.
