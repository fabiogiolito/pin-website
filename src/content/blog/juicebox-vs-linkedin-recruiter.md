# Juicebox vs LinkedIn Recruiter: AI Sourcing Compared in 2026

Juicebox charges up to $199 per seat per month, plus $199 for every AI agent, for natural-language search across 800M+ profiles. Meanwhile, LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate runs $900 per seat per month ($10,800/seat/yr list, 3-seat minimum) for access to LinkedIn's 1.3 billion members, and its Hiring Assistant agent costs extra. Most recruiting teams weighing juicebox vs linkedin recruiter won't pick either as the best answer in 2026. Instead, they pick [Pin](https://www.pin.com/), rated 4.8/5 on G2 and the highest-rated AI recruiting software on G2. It pairs 850M+ multi-source profiles and plans from $99/mo with a native agentic workflow spanning sourcing, outreach, and scheduling in one platform.

This guide walks through the 2026 version of each tool, pricing deltas, AI feature parity, integration depth, and where each one falls short for real recruiting functions. Every data point is sourced. Organizations running LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate at $32,400+/yr, or weighing a startup that has lifted its Growth seat price 81% since April 2024, should read to the end. The trade-offs aren't what vendor marketing implies.

## What Does Juicebox Do in 2026, and What Costs Extra?

Type a prompt like "senior backend engineer in Austin who's worked at a Series B startup and published on Rust" into Juicebox and the tool returns a ranked list of matching profiles. At the core is PeopleGPT, an AI layer built on top of an 800M+ profile index aggregated from 30+ public data sources. Behind the scenes, the system evaluates up to 5,000 profiles per search in real time, according to Juicebox's own product page.

Funding has moved fast. In September 2025, a $30M Series A led by Sequoia came at $10M+ ARR and 2,500+ paying customers ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/25/juicebox-raises-30m-from-sequoia-to-revolutionize-hiring-with-llm-powered-search/), 2025). An $80M Series B at an $850M valuation followed in March 2026 ([Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/juicebox-80-million-at-850-million-valuation-raised-for-ai-recruiting-platform/), 2026). Named customers include Cognition, Ramp, Perplexity, OpenAI, Anyscale, and Quora, a list skewed heavily toward tech-native buyers. Founders David Paffenholz and Ishan Gupta built the company through YC's Summer 2022 batch.

Where the marketing softens is on feature coverage. Only Business-plan accounts get ATS and CRM sync, and the "21 CRMs" on Juicebox's homepage shrink to three (Salesforce, HubSpot, and Affinity) in its own help docs. Sequences pair automated email with LinkedIn connection-request and InMail tasks, and there is no SMS channel or native interview scheduler. Exports are metered on every plan, ATS pushes included, and Starter's pricing table lists no phone numbers at all. Agents, the platform's autonomous workflow layer, add $199/mo per agent on top of any seat subscription, and a paused agent still takes up a paid Agent seat, per Juicebox's Agents help doc. Anyone expecting ChatGPT-speed search to come with ChatGPT-speed contact velocity should reset expectations.

## How Does LinkedIn Recruiter Work in 2026: Lite vs Corporate?

LinkedIn Recruiter isn't one product. It's three. And the gap between tiers drives most of the cost and capability decision.

At the entry tier, Recruiter Lite targets solo recruiters and small shops at $170/mo. Above that, Recruiter Corporate is the $900-$1,080 per seat per month enterprise plan most in-house recruiters and agencies actually want. Recruiter Professional Services (RPS) is the agency-specific variant. For anyone running juicebox vs linkedin recruiter math, the enterprise plan is the real choice, since Lite is missing most of what makes the platform useful for serious sourcing.

### Recruiter Lite ($1,680-$2,040/yr single seat)

Lite gives 30 InMails per month (max 120 accumulation), 20+ search filters, and visibility restricted to 1st/2nd/3rd-degree connections only ([Dover](https://www.dover.com/blog/linkedin-recruiter-lite-vs-professional), 2026). Out-of-network profiles appear anonymized: no name, no employer. Notably missing from Lite: ATS integration, shared workspace, team pipeline. Additional seats cost $2,670/yr each with a 5-seat maximum. For any organization doing any volume of outbound sourcing to candidates they don't already know, Lite's network walls make it functionally useless.

### Recruiter Corporate ($10,800-$12,960/seat/yr estimated, 3-seat minimum)

The enterprise plan is where LinkedIn's real sourcing power lives. This tier opens up full network access to all 1.3 billion members, 40+ search filters, and 150 InMails per license per month with rollover. Shared projects and candidate pipelines ship standard.

ATS integration covers Bullhorn, Greenhouse, Jobvite, Workday, Lever, iCIMS, and others (LinkedIn Talent Solutions ATS partner directory, 2025). At 3 seats, the annual floor is $32,400. A 10-person recruiting function pays roughly $108,000/yr before add-ons.

Across thousands of verified LinkedIn purchases, Vendr puts the median all-product contract at $38,103/yr, with a range from $8,128 to $159,491 ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/linkedin), 2026). Buyers negotiate an average of 13% savings, but the floor is still well above any Juicebox plan.

### Hiring Assistant (paid add-on)

Here's the catch most comparison articles miss: Hiring Assistant is a paid add-on layered on top of Recruiter Corporate or RPS+, and LinkedIn hasn't publicly disclosed its price. Anyone who assumes "Corporate includes the AI" is in for a surcharge discussion. Globally, LinkedIn's AI agent rolled out in September 2025 after a charter program with 500+ companies, and its headline outcomes, such as 4+ hours saved per role, are LinkedIn's own charter-program figures ([HR Brew](https://www.hr-brew.com/stories/2025/09/03/linkedin-s-first-agent-hiring-assistant-expands-availability-globally-after-first-year), 2025). It also works only inside LinkedIn's network, so it can't surface anyone LinkedIn doesn't already hold.

### How much does Juicebox AI cost in 2026?

Two of the three seat prices are published; the third is quoted. Growth runs $199/seat/mo monthly, or $179 annually, for up to five seats. Starter runs $119/seat/mo month-to-month, or $99/seat/mo billed annually, for a single seat with no phone numbers. Business is custom-priced, and it's the tier where ATS and CRM sync and SOC 2 report access live. Agents add $199 per agent per month on any paid plan.

Mostly, the price path has run one way. Growth cost $99 per seat on annual billing, with up to 10 seats, in April 2024 ([Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20240414102601/https://juicebox.ai/pricing)). Today's $179 is an 81% increase, the seat cap is down to five, and annual Starter has risen from $79 to $99. Between January and late March 2026, monthly Starter rose from $119 to $139, monthly Growth from $179 to $199, and agents went from $300 for two to $199 each. July 21, 2026 brought Starter back down to $119, with 500 contact and 500 export credits, while Growth kept its March increase and moved to 1,500 credits of each ([UsagePricing](https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/juicebox), 2026). Anyone working from a quote written before that date is reading the wrong numbers.

Two things don't change with the plan: the export meter and the agent surcharge. A team running two agents pays $4,776/yr on top of seats, and paused agents still count.

## How Do They Compare on AI Search, Outreach, and Integrations?

Capability-wise, Juicebox and LinkedIn's Hiring Assistant are closer than the pricing gap suggests, though they solve different problems. Running as a semantic search layer on a scraped multi-source index, Juicebox takes a complex query and returns a ranked profile list.

Hiring Assistant is different: an autonomous agent that lives inside LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate, writes InMails, surfaces candidates, and waits for recruiter approval. Different workflows. Different operational risks.

### Data breadth

Across 30+ public data sources (LinkedIn, GitHub, Stack Overflow, and others), Juicebox aggregates broadly. By contrast, LinkedIn Recruiter holds the canonical LinkedIn dataset alone: 1.3 billion self-maintained profiles, but only LinkedIn. That distinction matters for engineering, research, and security-clearance roles where GitHub, patents, and academic publications hold signal that LinkedIn profiles don't show.

### Outreach channels

Channel coverage is where both tools come up short. Juicebox sequences pair automated email with LinkedIn connection-request and InMail tasks, but there is no SMS step at all. LinkedIn Recruiter gets InMail, capped at 150 per seat per month on Corporate, plus email only through ATS sync. Neither reaches a candidate by text.

Channel choice moves replies more than message volume does. Across a sample of 5,200,000+ recruiter outreach messages sent through Pin, LinkedIn messages earned candidate replies about 3x more often than email. That gap held across 200+ organizations running both channels. So any cap on the LinkedIn touch costs replies, which is why the 150-InMail cap on Recruiter Corporate bites at volume.

### Integrations

On integrations, Juicebox reserves ATS and CRM sync for the custom Business plan, while LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate connects to a short list of major ATSes (Bullhorn, Greenhouse, Jobvite, Workday, Lever, Oracle, Avature, iCIMS, ClearCompany) and Lite connects to none. One recent policy change to flag: starting October 1, 2025, LinkedIn stopped accepting new Apply with LinkedIn (AWLI) partners ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/talent/recruiter-system-connect?view=li-lts-2026-03), 2025). Newer ATSes are locked out of that particular integration path.

## How Does Juicebox Work With LinkedIn?

Juicebox reads LinkedIn as one of 30+ public sources feeding its 800M+ profile index, but it is not a LinkedIn product and holds no LinkedIn partnership. That distinction is what most searches for Juicebox LinkedIn are really asking about. No Recruiter seat is required to run it, and no LinkedIn contract governs what it returns.

Two touchpoints connect the two in daily use. Sequences can carry a LinkedIn connection request or an InMail as a recruiter task. Reply detection works by parsing LinkedIn's notification emails in the connected mailbox, so InMail responses land back in the sequence.

For teams already paying for Recruiter Corporate, the overlap question matters more than the feature list. Where Juicebox AI LinkedIn coverage helps is reach, across GitHub, publications, and other sources LinkedIn doesn't index. It adds no InMail credits. And it doesn't remove the need for a seat when InMail is the primary channel.

## Where Does Each Platform Fall Short?

Every tool has tradeoffs. These are the ones that matter for juicebox vs linkedin recruiter buyers.

### Juicebox's gaps

- **Prices moved up.** Growth rose 81% on annual billing since April 2024, from $99 to $179 per seat, while its seat cap fell from 10 to 5. Every AI agent adds $199/mo on top, and paused agents still hold a paid seat.
- **ATS and CRM sync require Business.** Every ATS and CRM connection sits on the custom-priced Business plan, and the homepage's "21 CRMs" are three in Juicebox's own docs.
- **Onboarding and admin seats are Business-only too.** Juicebox's pricing table reserves onboarding and training, admin seats, and usage analytics for Business, while Pin includes onboarding from Solo and admin seats on Professional.
- **No SMS step and no interview scheduler.** LinkedIn connection requests and InMails are available as sequence tasks, but nothing reaches a candidate by text, and booking an interview means pasting your own link.
- **Exports and phones are metered hard.** Every plan caps exports, ATS pushes included, topping out at 2,250 per seat a month on Business. Starter's pricing table lists no phone numbers, and Growth charges 3 credits per phone, so an email plus a phone costs 4 credits.
- **The contract promises five business days.** Juicebox's Services Agreement (updated September 13, 2026) commits only to "commercially reasonable efforts to respond to all Helpdesk tickets within five (5) business days," by email. Its 24-hour promise covers Growth and Business, leaving Starter and Free with no guaranteed response time.
- **The free plan is a trial.** It ends for good after 5 searches (tweaking a filter counts), 65 profile views, or 5 one-off credits, per Juicebox's help docs.
- **The demo can oversell the plan.** One paying Business-plan customer on [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/peoplegpt-by-juicebox/reviews) wrote that ATS integration and hiring-manager seats shown in the demo were not included, and planned to cancel.

### LinkedIn Recruiter's gaps

- **Pricing cliffs kill smaller organizations.** Below 3 seats, the enterprise plan isn't available at all. Lite's out-of-network anonymization makes broad sourcing nearly useless.
- **The $32,400/yr floor is real.** Above 3 seats, the annual cost is $32,400+ before InMail overages, Talent Insights, or Hiring Assistant.
- **Siloed data.** Every search stops at LinkedIn's own network, with no GitHub, patent, or publication signal.
- **The InMail cap still bites at scale.** 150 InMails/seat/month on Corporate with rollover means 1,800 touches per seat per year before $10 overage credits.
- **Bundle creep.** Talent Insights is a separate product, from £9,400/yr for one seat (LinkedIn UK price list). Hiring Assistant is a paid add-on on top of Corporate. Neither is included in the base seat.

Those cost pressures, combined with AI-native alternatives that didn't exist three years ago, explain the growing [reasons teams are replacing LinkedIn Recruiter](/blog/leaving-linkedin-recruiter/).
