## Why Combine Your ATS and CRM Into One Platform?

Recruiting teams that consolidate their ATS and CRM into a single platform eliminate the most common workflow killer: context-switching between systems. SHRM's [2025 benchmarking report](https://www.shrm.org/about/press-room/shrm-releases-2025-benchmarking-reports--how-does-your-organizat) puts the average nonexecutive cost-per-hire at $5,475, and its [2026 benchmarking](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/recruiting-benchmarking) puts median nonexecutive time-to-fill at 39 days. Much of that cost comes from administrative overhead: duplicate data entry, broken integrations, and candidates who fall through the cracks between systems.

Consolidation isn't slowing down in recruiting tech. Three major acquisitions reshaped the landscape in 2025 alone. [SAP completed its SmartRecruiters acquisition](https://news.sap.com/2025/09/sap-completes-smartrecruiters-acquisition/) in September, [Workday closed its Paradox deal](https://newsroom.workday.com/2025-10-01-Workday-Completes-Acquisition-of-Paradox) in October, and iCIMS acquired Apli. Every deal pointed the same direction: standalone tools are merging into broader platforms.

What specific problems does consolidation solve?

-   **No more duplicate candidate records.** When your ATS and CRM share one database, a sourced candidate who later applies doesn't become two separate entries. One profile tracks every touchpoint from first outreach to signed offer.
-   **Shorter time-to-fill.** Handoff gaps between sourcing (CRM) and tracking (ATS) disappear. Candidates move from "nurtured passive talent" to "active applicant" inside the same workflow.
-   **Lower software spend.** Two platform subscriptions, two implementation projects, and two vendor relationships collapse into one.
-   **Better reporting.** Full-funnel analytics from sourced to hired become possible when the data lives in one system. You can actually measure which sourcing channels produce hires, not just which channels produce applicants.

Not every "all-in-one" platform delivers on all four. Some started as an ATS and bolted on a CRM tab. Others built CRM-first and added applicant tracking as an afterthought. Whether you're CRM-heavy or ATS-heavy, our [ATS software guide](https://www.pin.com/blog/ats-software-guide/) covers what to look for in each category. That difference shows up in how well candidate data flows between the two functions, and that's what we'll evaluate below.

## How Did We Evaluate These Platforms?

Choosing among the top ATS platforms for consolidated recruiting means going beyond feature claims. We assessed each platform across five dimensions that matter most when consolidating ATS and CRM into one system:

-   **ATS depth:** Job posting, resume parsing, interview scheduling, compliance reporting, and offer management. Does the ATS side feel like a full product or a checkbox feature?
-   **CRM depth:** Talent pooling, nurture sequences, passive candidate tracking, and relationship scoring. Can you actually run long-term candidate engagement from this platform?
-   **Sourcing capabilities:** Does the platform help you find candidates, or only manage the ones you've already found? Native sourcing separates the strongest platforms from the rest.
-   **Pricing transparency:** Published pricing, clear per-user or per-company models, and honest add-on disclosure. If we can't find the price, that's a yellow flag.
-   **Integration ecosystem:** Connections to HRIS, job boards, calendar tools, background check providers, and communication channels (email, LinkedIn, SMS).

Every platform below claims to offer ATS and CRM in one product. Some actually do. Others added a "talent pool" tab and called it a CRM. We'll be specific about what each one delivers and where the gaps are. Our dedicated ranking covers the [best applicant tracking systems](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-applicant-tracking-systems/) as a standalone category.

## 8 All-in-One Recruiting Platforms: Full Breakdown

### 1\. Pin - AI-Native Recruiting That Replaces ATS, CRM, and Sourcing Tools

![Screenshot of the Pin AI recruiting platform homepage](/images/urlbox/pin-com-934154a1-1.webp)

Pin isn't a traditional ATS with a CRM bolted on. It's an AI-native recruiting platform that collapses three separate tool categories (sourcing, outreach, and pipeline management) into a single workflow. Instead of waiting for candidates to apply, Pin proactively finds them by scanning 850M+ candidate profiles with AI that matches recruiter-level precision.

Where traditional all-in-one platforms manage the middle and bottom of your recruiting funnel, Pin starts at the very top. Searching across the largest candidate database in the industry, with 100% coverage in North America and Europe, Pin's AI surfaces candidates who match your requirements. Its sequences send email automatically and queue LinkedIn and SMS steps as recruiter tasks, delivering 5x better response rates than industry averages. 83% of candidates Pin recommends are accepted into hiring pipelines.

Interview scheduling runs on automated back-and-forth and calendar syncing, plus a shared multi-channel team inbox keeps the entire recruiting team coordinated. Pipeline management happens in a [visual recruiting CRM](https://www.pin.com/features/crm/) that runs from first touch to signed offer, with AI alerts for candidates going stale.

Agencies get multi-client management from a single account through Pin, with no dashboard switching needed. Recruiters can also [source on the go](/blog/mobile-recruiting-apps/) via the Chrome extension. And Pin is SOC 2 Type 2 certified with strict bias elimination guardrails at every step.

> "Absolutely Money maker for Recruiters... in 6 months i can directly attribute over $250k in revenue to Pin." - Rich Rosen, Executive Recruiter at Cornerstone Search

**Pricing:** Free plan (no credit card required), Solo at $99/mo billed annually, Professional at $135 per user per month billed annually ($179 month-to-month), and custom Business pricing with unlimited seats. ATS integration is an add-on on Solo and Professional and included on Business. Full details are on the [Pin pricing page](https://www.pin.com/pricing/).

**Where it fits:** Teams that want to stop paying for three separate tools (sourcing, outreach, pipeline management) and consolidate into one AI-driven platform. Works for both in-house TA teams and recruiting agencies.

**What to know:** Pin isn't a traditional ATS. It doesn't process inbound job applications or generate EEOC compliance reports the way Greenhouse or Workable would. Built for proactive recruiting, not inbound processing. High-inbound teams may still want a lightweight ATS alongside Pin for compliance tracking. Ashby is a popular pairing, and our rundown of the [best Ashby integrations](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-ashby-integrations/) shows how teams connect Pin's sourcing engine to Ashby's structured hiring workflows.

### 2\. Gem - Talent CRM and ATS Built for Relationship-Driven Recruiting

![Screenshot of the Gem talent CRM and ATS platform homepage](/images/urlbox/gem-com-7937b4f2.webp)

[Gem](https://www.gem.com/) is one of the most complete all-in-one platforms in this category, pairing a category-leading talent CRM with a native applicant tracking system, AI sourcing, scheduling, and full-funnel analytics. Most tools on this list started as an ATS and bolted on a CRM tab. Gem went the other way: it built its reputation on candidate relationship management, then launched its own ATS in 2023 so both functions share one data model.

Gem's CRM depth is its calling card: talent pools, multi-step nurture campaigns, engagement scoring, and automated follow-ups that keep passive candidates warm over months. On the sourcing side, an AI Sourcing Agent searches a pool Gem puts at 800M+ profiles alongside your own ATS and CRM data. Two-way integrations with Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and Workday mean teams can adopt Gem's CRM and sourcing even while keeping another ATS as their system of record. Security-wise, Gem holds a SOC 2 Type II report.

**Pricing:** Custom quote on annual contracts, priced by company headcount, for both the "Gem + your ATS" and all-in-one plans. Startups under 30 employees can get six months free through Gem's startup program, then a discount.

**Where it fits:** Mid-market and enterprise in-house talent teams that want applicant tracking, a genuine talent CRM, and nurture automation in one system rather than three.

**What to know:** Pricing is sales-led, so most teams need a demo to get numbers. Teams that want self-serve pricing, a free plan, or 100% profile coverage across North America and Europe often pair Gem with a dedicated sourcing engine like Pin.

### 3\. Workable - Mid-Market ATS With Built-In Sourcing and CRM

![Screenshot of the Workable ATS and CRM platform homepage](/images/urlbox/workable-com-3fd4f950.webp)

Workable is one of the few mid-market platforms that genuinely combines applicant tracking with sourcing and [candidate relationship management](/blog/candidate-relationship-management/) in one product. Its AI sourcing scans public profiles and recommends candidates for open roles, and the newer Workable Agent can source, screen, and qualify applicants. With the talent CRM database, you can tag and nurture passive candidates over time. Not as deep as a dedicated sourcing tool, but more than most ATS platforms offer.

Solid ATS coverage includes job posting to 200+ boards, customizable hiring pipelines, structured interview kits, two-way calendar sync with self-scheduling, and offer management. Background checks run through partner integrations.

**Pricing:** Standard at $299/mo, Premier at $599/mo, and Enterprise at $719/mo for companies with 1-20 employees, billed annually and scaling with headcount, per [Capterra's Workable pricing page](https://www.capterra.com/p/130175/Workable/pricing/). Workable no longer lists its old $149 Starter plan. On Standard, add-ons for texting ($89/mo), video interviews ($109/mo), and assessments ($59/mo) push the real cost to about $556/mo. See our [best recruiting software comparison](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-recruiting-software/) for alternatives in this price range.

**Where it fits:** Growing companies (50-500 employees) that want applicant tracking, sourcing, and CRM in one dashboard without enterprise pricing.

**What to know:** Workable's sourcing AI works from public profile data rather than a proprietary candidate database. Next to dedicated recruitment CRMs, Workable's CRM is basic: you can tag and pool candidates, but nurture sequences and engagement scoring are thin. With the Starter plan gone, the entry price has roughly doubled.

### 4\. Bullhorn - Agency-Standard Recruiting Platform With Deep Back-Office Integration

![Screenshot of the Bullhorn recruiting platform homepage](/images/urlbox/bullhorn-com-9c28abd9.webp)

Bullhorn has been the default platform for staffing agencies for over a decade. It combines applicant tracking with a full recruitment CRM, plus back-office features like billing, credentialing, and time/expense management that agency-specific platforms need. If your staffing firm runs on spreadsheets and manual invoicing, Bullhorn is designed to replace all of it.

Bullhorn's CRM side is genuinely strong: relationship tracking, deal pipeline management, activity logging, and client-facing reporting. Bullhorn's marketplace lists 300+ partners, including 110+ VMS integrations, plus job boards, onboarding tools, and payroll systems. Large agencies with complex workflows will find it essential.

**Pricing:** Bullhorn now publishes its entry tiers: Starter at $99/user/mo and Core at $165/user/mo, both with free implementation. Pro, Max, and the enterprise bundles are quote-only. Bullhorn Automation (built on Herefish, which Bullhorn acquired in 2020) adds nurture sequences. At Starter pricing, a 10-recruiter agency pays at least $11,880 a year before upgrades.

**Where it fits:** Staffing and recruiting agencies (25+ recruiters) that need CRM, ATS, billing, and compliance under one vendor. Bullhorn's integration ecosystem is built specifically for the agency workflow of managing multiple clients simultaneously.

**What to know:** Bullhorn scores 3.7/5 for value for money on [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/140531/Bullhorn-Recruiting-Software/), its lowest category score. Compared to newer platforms, the interface feels dated. Built-in interview scheduling and omnichannel outreach sit in the higher Max tier. Despite the CRM strength, Bullhorn's AI matching works on candidates you already have, so most agencies still add a sourcing tool to find new ones.

### 5\. Manatal - Budget-Friendly Recruiting Platform With AI Candidate Scoring

![Screenshot of the Manatal ATS and CRM platform homepage](/images/urlbox/manatal-com-1c116aa9.webp)

Manatal offers one of the lowest entry points for a combined ATS and CRM platform: $15/user/month on annual billing. It includes AI-powered candidate scoring, a CRM pipeline with customizable stages, and social media profile enrichment that pulls LinkedIn and other public data into candidate records. For small teams watching every dollar, it's hard to beat on price.

Manatal's ATS handles job posting to 2,500+ channels, resume parsing, collaborative hiring with scorecards, auto-scheduling with Google and Outlook calendars, and compliance tracking. Within the CRM module, you can create talent pools, tag candidates by skills or availability, and run basic drip campaigns. Manatal's AI recommendations suggest candidates from your existing database for new roles.

**Pricing:** Professional at $15/user/mo (annual billing, $19 monthly), Enterprise at $35/user/mo, Enterprise Plus at $55/user/mo. On the $15 plan, active jobs cap at 15, and open API access requires the top tier. There's no free plan, though a 14-day free trial is available.

**Where it fits:** Small recruiting teams (1-10 users) and boutique agencies that need both ATS and CRM functionality without spending $200+/mo per person.

**What to know:** Manatal's CRM is functional but shallow. Nurture sequences are basic, engagement scoring is limited, and there's no built-in sourcing beyond social media enrichment. That $15/user/mo price is attractive until you realize the Professional plan restricts you to 15 active jobs, so most growing teams will need the $35 tier quickly.

### 6\. SmartRecruiters (SAP) - Enterprise Recruiting Suite Post-Acquisition

![Screenshot of the SmartRecruiters enterprise recruiting platform homepage](/images/urlbox/smartrecruiters-com-5fa43179.webp)

SmartRecruiters was one of the strongest standalone enterprise ATS/CRM platforms before SAP acquired it in September 2025, and SAP says it will stay a standalone product that also connects to non-SAP HR systems. SAP is replacing its older SuccessFactors Recruiting module with SmartRecruiters, giving those customers three to five years to migrate, [per CIO.com](https://www.cio.com/article/4068172/sap-sets-timeline-to-replace-successfactors-recruiting-module-with-smartrecruiters.html). By March 2026, the first phase of native SuccessFactors integration had shipped.

SmartRecruiters' CRM includes SmartCRM for building talent communities, automated nurture campaigns, candidate engagement scoring, and event management. Configurable workflows, approval chains, candidate self-scheduling, AI-powered job matching, and multi-language support make up the ATS side. Enterprise buyers get a genuine all-in-one who want ATS and CRM without stitching together two vendors.

**Pricing:** Quote-only. [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/smartrecruiters) puts the median contract at $33,507/yr, with deals ranging from about $15,250 to $51,561 (February 2026), and frames pricing per recruiter seat.

**Where it fits:** Enterprise organizations (1,000+ employees), especially those already in the SAP ecosystem, that want recruiting tied into their broader HR platform.

**What to know:** The product's roadmap now runs through SAP, with integration work prioritized for SuccessFactors customers. If you're outside the SAP ecosystem, ask how non-SAP integrations will be supported and get pricing locks in writing before signing. Alternative enterprise platforms are covered in our [recruiting tech stack guide](https://www.pin.com/blog/build-recruiting-tech-stack-2026/).

### 7\. Zoho Recruit - Free Tier With CRM and ATS for Budget Teams

![Screenshot of the Zoho Recruit ATS and CRM platform homepage](/images/urlbox/zoho-com-b5cec245.webp)

Zoho Recruit offers two distinct editions, Corporate HR and Staffing Agency, both combining ATS and CRM functionality. On the Corporate side, a Free plan makes Zoho Recruit one of the only combined platforms with a genuine $0 entry point, though it allows one active job per recruiter license. Existing Zoho customers (Zoho CRM, Zoho People, Zoho Mail) get the most from it through native integrations.

Job posting, resume parsing, interview scheduling, and offer management are covered on the ATS side. Talent pools, client management (agency edition), and basic candidate engagement tracking make up the CRM side. On the Enterprise plan, Zoho's Blueprint feature automates multi-step workflows.

**Pricing:** The Staffing Agency edition runs $25, $50, and $75 per user per month billed annually ($30, $60, and $90 monthly) for Standard, Professional, and Enterprise, per [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/125768/Zoho-Recruit/pricing/). Corporate HR buyers get Free, Standard ($25/user/mo annual), and Enterprise (about $50/user/mo annual) plans, with active-job limits per license.

**Where it fits:** Teams already using Zoho's ecosystem who want recruiting tools that integrate natively. Solo recruiters testing the waters will find the free tier genuinely useful.

**What to know:** Zoho Recruit's CRM is serviceable but not deep. Engagement scoring is basic, nurture sequences are manual, and there's no sourcing capability, so you'll still need a separate tool to find candidates. Per-user pricing gets expensive fast at 5+ users; flat-rate tools may be cheaper for larger teams.

### 8\. JazzHR - Affordable SMB ATS With Basic CRM Features

![Screenshot of the JazzHR applicant tracking system homepage](/images/urlbox/jazzhr-com-9b95551e.webp)

JazzHR targets small businesses that need applicant tracking without enterprise complexity or pricing. Basic CRM-like features are included: candidate tags, talent pools, and activity tracking. Calling it a full CRM is a stretch versus the modern [recruiting CRM features checklist](/blog/recruiting-crm-features-checklist/) most teams are buying against in 2026. Workflow simplicity is JazzHR's real strength: post jobs, collect applications, move candidates through stages, make offers. No unnecessary bells.

JazzHR handles posting to over a dozen free job boards, syndication to paid boards, resume parsing, and candidate scoring, with interview scheduling on the Plus plan and above. Saving and tagging candidates for future roles is as close to CRM functionality as JazzHR gets.

**Pricing:** Hero at $99/mo, Plus at $325/mo, and Pro at $499/mo on monthly billing, per [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/87750/JazzHR/pricing/). Annual plans run $1,000, $3,480, and $5,508 per year. Hero starts at three job postings a month, while Plus supports up to 200 active jobs. Offers and e-signatures are a paid add-on.

**Where it fits:** Small businesses (5-50 employees) with low hiring volume (under 10 roles/year) that want a simple, affordable ATS with basic candidate pooling.

**What to know:** JazzHR's "CRM" is really just a talent pool feature, with no nurture sequences, engagement scoring, or relationship management tools. Hero's posting limit makes it impractical for any team hiring more than a few roles at once. Ownership-wise, JazzHR sits inside the Employ brand alongside Lever and Jobvite. Sourcing capabilities are absent altogether.

## How Do You Integrate ATS and CRM Software?

If you'd rather keep your current ATS and CRM systems than consolidate, there are three practical ways to connect them. Which one fits depends on how much engineering time you have and how many tools are in the stack.

-   **Native connectors.** Most ATS CRM software ships prebuilt integrations you switch on in settings, such as Gem's two-way sync with Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby. These are the fastest to set up but only cover the pairings the vendor chose to build.
-   **Direct API or iPaaS.** Tools like Zapier or Workato move records between systems and fire on events such as a stage change. They're flexible, but someone has to own field mapping and fix breakages.
-   **Unified APIs.** Services such as [Merge](https://www.merge.dev/categories/ats-recruiting-api) expose one normalized API across 70+ ATSs, which suits teams building internal tools or connecting many systems at once.

Whichever route you choose, map candidate IDs first so a sourced lead and a later applicant resolve to the same record. That's the step most integrations skip, and it's why only 39% of organizations in HR.com's study call their HR tools usefully integrated.

## Key Takeaways

-   **Most teams should consolidate.** Running separate ATS and CRM tools creates data silos, integration headaches, and inflated costs. The trend is clearly toward unified platforms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is ATS and CRM software?

ATS and CRM software are the two core recruiting systems: an ATS tracks people who apply, and a CRM nurtures people who haven't. Applicant tracking system (ATS) software manages active job applications: collecting resumes, moving candidates through interview stages, and generating offer letters. Candidate relationship management (CRM) software builds relationships with passive candidates before they apply, keeping them engaged through talent pools and nurture campaigns. Combined CRM and ATS software handles both in one system, so a sourced candidate flows into applicant tracking without manual transfers or duplicate records. Pin combines a recruiting CRM, outreach, and AI sourcing across 850M+ profiles, with a free plan to start.

### What is the difference between an ATS and a recruiting CRM?

An ATS manages candidates who've already applied, tracking them from application through offer. A recruiting CRM manages relationships with passive candidates who haven't applied yet. In short, the ATS starts at the application and the CRM starts before it. Combined platforms keep both in one candidate record, which removes duplicate data entry and handoff gaps.
