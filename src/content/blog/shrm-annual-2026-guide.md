## How Does SHRM 2026 Actually Look on the Ground?

SHRM26 in Orlando is the flagship event in SHRM's 2026 conference portfolio. It sits one tier above the spring SHRM Talent Conference (which already wrapped April 19-22 in Dallas) and the AI+HI Project conference (SHRM's standalone AI and human intelligence event in San Francisco). At 9800 International Drive, the Orange County Convention Center West Building sits roughly 11 miles and a 20-minute drive from Orlando International Airport (MCO).

Five days of programming run Monday through Friday, with the main conference body concentrated June 16-19. Pre-conference workshops start Monday afternoon at 1:00 PM and continue Tuesday morning through 12:00 PM, billed separately from the main pass. From there, Tuesday's main orientation begins at 12:15 PM, the first breakout block opens at 1:45 PM, and John Maxwell delivers the opening general session at 3:15-4:30 PM. The Expo Opening Reception then runs 4:30-7:00 PM, followed by Tuesday Night Entertainment from 8:00-10:00 PM.

### What Happens Wednesday Through Friday?

Wednesday and Thursday concentrate the densest learning. Each morning opens with a 9:00-10:00 AM general session on the Main Stage. After that, multiple breakout rounds run across the 9 tracks. Lunch service runs 11:00 AM-1:00 PM both days. Wednesday's expo hall stays open 8:30 AM-4:30 PM; Thursday's hours run 8:30 AM-3:00 PM.

Thursday night closes with **SHRM26 After Hours at Disney's Hollywood Studios from 8:00-11:00 PM**. Every paid in-person registration includes admission, and shuttle service runs from the SHRM hotel block. Friday is a half-day: Oprah Winfrey takes the Main Stage at 9:00-10:00 AM, final breakouts run through 11:45 AM, and registration closes at noon.

## What's New for the SHRM Conference 2026 vs. SHRM25?

Three changes define the SHRM Annual Conference 2026 versus SHRM25: AI is now a dedicated standalone track, BambooHR steps in as presenting sponsor with CEO Brad Rencher on the main stage, and Disney's Hollywood Studios replaces the typical Thursday-night program. SHRM25 in San Diego drew 20,000+ in-person attendees from 80+ countries, with a Main Stage address from former President Joe Biden, per [SHRM's official press release](https://www.shrm.org/advocacy/shrm25-welcomes-over-20-000-attendees-to-shape-the-future-of-wor). Chicago's 2024 event set the all-time attendance record at roughly 26,000 total, including ~2,200 virtual passholders ([SHRM press release](https://www.shrm.org/about/press-room/record-breaking-shrm-annual-conference---expo--shrm24--kicks-off)). SHRM26 targets that range again, with the in-person page projecting 25,000+ attendees.

Three things are visibly different in 2026:

1. **AI is structurally embedded, not bolted on.** "AI, Data, & Tech" is now its own dedicated track, not a sub-theme inside Talent Management. Future Forward sessions from the WorkplaceTech Accelerator anchor Thursday's expo programming, and several Wednesday breakouts target AI ROI specifically (e.g., "Return on Intelligence: Move AI ROI Beyond Cost to Competitive Advantage").
2. **BambooHR is the presenting sponsor**, with CEO Brad Rencher delivering a general session. Year-over-year, the HR-tech presence at SHRM has scaled noticeably as AI vendors compete for HR mindshare.
3. **Disney Hollywood Studios After Hours replaces typical Thursday-night entertainment.** Park-wide access, included with the standard pass. It is the largest closed-park event SHRM has organized recently.

This AI emphasis maps directly to SHRM's own 2026 research. Per [SHRM's State of AI in HR 2026 report](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/state-of-ai-hr-2026), based on 1,908 HR professionals surveyed, 27% of organizations now report recruiting as the #1 AI use case in HR. Among HR director-level and above, 73% have adopted AI by 2025. SHRM also notes that AI's organizational impact is 5.7x more likely to shift job responsibilities than to displace jobs.

## How Should Talent Acquisition Pros Plan Their Days?

Pin's take: most TA professionals overbook their SHRM schedules and burn out by Wednesday afternoon. The conference is large enough that no one walks the full expo, attends every breakout, and still has the energy to network. A working approach is to pick one anchor session per day, three-to-five exhibitor conversations from the 650+ vendor floor, and one networking event per evening. Treat the rest as optional. Based on what we hear from recruiters who've attended past SHRM events, the highest-ROI conversations almost always happen at the expo or the after-hours events, not in the breakout rooms.

For Tuesday, anchor on the Maxwell keynote and the Expo Opening Reception. That reception is the cleanest networking window of the week because attendees are fresh and the expo is new. On Wednesday, prioritize the AI ROI sessions and any Talent Management & Acquisition breakouts that feature live customer panels. Thursday's Disney Hollywood Studios after-hours event is the social capstone of the week. On Friday, the Oprah keynote is mandatory and registration closes at noon, so plan an early-afternoon flight.

[State of talent acquisition in 2026](/blog/state-of-talent-acquisition-2026/) data should shape what sessions you prioritize. AI sourcing, candidate experience, and compensation transparency are the three TA themes most aligned with what hiring teams actually report struggling with in the current market. These four data points explain why SHRM26's track design weights AI, leadership, and TA the way it does. ## How Many PDCs Can You Earn at SHRM26?

SHRM26 awards 28+ Professional Development Credits toward SHRM-CP and SHRM-SCP recertification, per the [recertification page](https://annual.shrm.org/recertification). Sessions auto-populate in the SHRM certification portal when the email used at registration matches the holder's SHRM membership account. No manual submission required.

For HR practitioners on a 3-year recertification cycle (60 PDC requirement), SHRM26 alone delivers nearly half the cycle's credits in a single week. A practitioner holding a SHRM-CP completes roughly 47% of the full cycle requirement from one SHRM26 registration. Pair it with the spring SHRM Talent Conference or fall AI+HI Project, and one year's conference attendance handles the entire cycle.

PDCs are not awarded for time spent in the expo hall, though Expo Theater sessions (live vendor product demonstrations during expo hours) often count. The certification page is the authoritative source for what does and doesn't count.
