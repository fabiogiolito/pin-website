## Vincere pricing in 2026: the published rate card

**Vincere costs £69 per user per month for the Core CRM/ATS license, plus £25 to £349 per agency per month for a mandatory Smart Pack.** Pricing splits into those two parts: a per-user license, and a per-agency add-on that bundles AI, automation, and analytics. You cannot buy one without the other if you want a working modern recruitment workflow.

| Component | Price (GBP, 2026) | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Core CRM/ATS license** | From £69/user/mo | Job pipelines, candidate database, basic search, email integration, mobile apps |
| **Smart Pack: Base** | £25/agency/mo | Job creation tools, CV scoring, basic analytics, Copilot AI assistant |
| **Smart Pack: Present** | £139/agency/mo | Base features + AI candidate presentations and shortlist summaries |
| **Smart Pack: Automate** | £235/agency/mo | Base features + chatbots + CRM workflow automation |
| **Smart Pack: Elite** | £349/agency/mo | Full AI suite, enhanced analytics storage, third-party data connectors, Pay & Bill, Mid-Office |

Source: [Vincere pricing page](https://www.vincere.io/pricing/) (fetched April 2026).

A few things to flag for buyers:

- **No published USD rates.** Vincere quotes exclusively in GBP, so third-party USD estimates ($85-$100/user/mo) are unverified currency conversions rather than vendor-confirmed pricing.
- **No free trial, no free tier.** Every plan requires a sales call and a signed annual contract.
- **A Smart Pack subscription is mandatory for any AI capability.** Buying the £69 Core license alone gets you no AI, no automation, and no chatbot. Even a 1-seat firm pays the Smart Pack fee on top of the per-user license.
- **Pay & Bill, Mid-Office, and Intelligence are now bundled into Elite.** Since [The Access Group acquired Vincere in January 2022](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/about/news/the-access-group-acquires-vincere/), previously separate modules now sit inside the £349 Elite tier.
- **Branding changed to Access Vincere Evo in 2026.** Vincere rebranded under its parent's naming, and G2, Capterra, and Software Advice all list the vendor as Access Vincere Evo in 2026 ([The Access Group](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/products/vincere/), 2026). Evo is the current generation of the same platform, not a separate SKU, so it carries no separate rate card.

Selecting a Smart Pack, rather than counting seats, is where the real pricing decision sits, because the spread between tiers is considerably wider than the spread between seat counts. Elite costs 14x what Base costs, and the jump buys chatbots, Pay & Bill, and Mid-Office rather than more seats:

## The hidden costs Vincere pricing doesn't show on the rate card

That £69-per-user headline is only the visible part of the contract. Four line items live off the pricing page but show up consistently in Capterra and Spott reviews from 2025 to 2026:

**1. Setup and data migration: $2,000 to $15,000+ (estimated).** While Vincere advertises "complimentary onboarding," third-party analyst [ITQlick](https://www.itqlick.com/vincere/pricing) (Feb 2026) pegs real-world setup at $2,000-$10,000 and training at $1,000-$5,000. Moving your data over runs another $3,000-$15,000 for a 10-user team. No fixed setup-fee schedule has been published by the vendor, so every quote is custom.

**2. Vincere's 6.5% April 2025 price hike.** Verified Vincere customers reported on [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/152350/Vincere/reviews/) (2025) and [Spott](https://spott.io/resources/vincere-a-complete-review-for-agencies) (2026) that Vincere applied a 6.5% across-the-board increase in April 2025, with no documented product upgrade. Buyers signing 2 or 3-year terms in 2026 should assume similar escalators and negotiate a cap upfront.

**3. Vincere data-export fees on contract exit.** One Capterra reviewer in December 2025 reported paying "over €1,000 to receive [my own candidate] data" when leaving. Vincere publishes no corresponding fee schedule. Buyers on annual contracts should ask Vincere for the export-fee policy in writing before signing, because the cost shows up at the worst possible moment (when you're already trying to leave).

**4. The 14-month Vincere exit trap.** Every Vincere agreement renews automatically. To exit at term-end, you must notify Vincere **in writing 3 months before** the renewal date. Miss that notification window and the agreement automatically renews for a further 12 months, so the next available exit point sits 14 months away. Spott reviewers flagged this clause specifically in 2026.

Here's what surprised us when we asked recruiters directly: per-seat pricing quietly assumes your headcount holds still, and it usually doesn't. In Pin's 2026 recruiter survey (250+ respondents), only 50.8% said their team size was unchanged over the previous 12 months. Roughly 35.3% grew and 13.9% resized down. Growth raises the bill mid-term, and contraction leaves you paying for seats you no longer staff, because the lock-in does not flex downward. A flat-account platform absorbs movement in both directions at the same monthly rate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does Vincere cost per month?

Vincere pricing starts at £69 per user per month for the Core CRM/ATS license. On top of that, every plan requires a Smart Pack add-on between £25 and £349 per agency per month, depending on which AI and automation features you need. A 10-seat firm on Core + Elite Smart Pack pays roughly £1,039 per month (~£12,468 per year), before setup, training, or data-migration costs. Pricing is GBP-only on the public site, with no published USD rate card. Third-party USD estimates of $85-$100 per user per month are unverified conversions, not vendor-confirmed figures.

### Does Vincere offer a free trial or free tier?

There is no free trial and no free tier as of April 2026. Every Vincere plan requires a sales call, a demo, and a signed annual contract with a 12-month minimum (1, 2, or 3-year terms available). The lack of a sandbox means buyers cannot benchmark candidate quality, search speed, or workflow fit before committing. For teams that want to try a recruitment platform before signing, Pin is the only AI recruiting platform on this list with a free tier (no credit card required) plus a month-to-month Starter plan at $100/mo.

### What's included in Vincere's Smart Packs?

Smart Packs bundle the AI, automation, and analytics features that aren't included in the £69 Core license. Base (£25/agency/mo) covers job creation, CV scoring, and the Copilot AI assistant. Present (£139) adds AI candidate presentations and shortlist summaries. Automate (£235) adds chatbots and CRM workflow automation. Elite (£349) layers on the full AI suite plus Pay & Bill and Mid-Office modules that were previously sold as separate line items. Smart Packs are billed per-agency, not per-user, so the rate is the same whether you have 1 seat or 50.

### What's the best Vincere alternative for staffing firms?

For teams focused on AI sourcing, outreach, and scheduling, Pin is the best Vincere alternative in 2026. It pairs a free tier with $100-$249/mo flat-account pricing, 850M+ candidate profiles, and 4.8/5 on G2. For firms that need a published per-user rate similar to Vincere's structure, [Crelate](/blog/crelate-pricing/) ($119/user/mo) and Manatal ($15/user/mo annual) are common shortlists. For high-volume staffing with middle-office requirements, [Bullhorn](/blog/bullhorn-pricing/) is the legacy default, though pricing is unpublished and contracts run higher.

### Is Vincere Evo a different product with different pricing?

No, Evo is not a separately priced product. Access Vincere Evo is simply the current branding of the same platform, not a distinct SKU or a premium tier, and Vincere publishes no Evo-specific rate card. The Core CRM/ATS license and the four Smart Packs (Base, Present, Automate, Elite) are the only priced line items. An Evo quote therefore resolves to the same £69/user/mo plus £25-£349/agency/mo structure ([The Access Group](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/products/vincere/), 2026). If a sales rep quotes "Evo" as an upgrade with its own fee, ask which of the four Smart Packs it maps to before signing.

### Why did Vincere increase its prices in April 2025?

In April 2025, Vincere applied a 6.5% across-the-board price hike, reported by multiple Capterra and Spott reviewers in 2025-2026. The vendor did not publicly tie the change to a specific product release or expansion. Buyers signing 2 or 3-year contracts in 2026 should assume similar escalators and negotiate a cap (e.g., CPI + 2%, or a fixed 3% ceiling) at signing. Pin pricing has been flat from 2024 through 2026.
