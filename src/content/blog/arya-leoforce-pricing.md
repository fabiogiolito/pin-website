Arya by Leoforce pricing is now quote-only. Leoforce retired the Arya brand in 2025, discontinued the per-job Arya Pulse plan ($199-$599 per job), and stopped publishing dollar figures for every product it still sells. Its enterprise products last carried public rate cards of $5,000 to $32,000 per month, and there is still no free tier or trial. For recruiting teams budgeting for AI sourcing in 2026, that means the Arya pricing you'll find on review sites describes plans you can no longer buy.

Founded in 2012, the company behind Arya is based in Raleigh, North Carolina, and has about 250 employees according to [Built In](https://builtin.com/company/leoforce). Founder Madhu Modugu is still CEO. Leoforce says it connects to 60+ ATS and CRM systems, including [Bullhorn](/blog/bullhorn-pricing/), [Greenhouse ATS](/blog/greenhouse-pricing/), and iCIMS. Its Greenhouse partner listing still claims 850M+ profiles from 80+ channels. Reviewers are cooler on it, though: [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/recruitment-marketing-platform/leoforce-profile/) users score its functionality 3.8 out of 5.

This guide covers what Leoforce charges today, what the retired Arya plans used to cost, the expenses no rate card mentions, and five alternatives with published prices. Sticker prices matter less than what a full year of hiring actually costs, so we run that math with real role volumes.

> **TL;DR:**
>
> - **Arya pricing is now quote-only.** Leoforce retired the Arya brand in 2025, and its current pricing page lists no dollar amounts.
> - **The old Pulse plan ran $199-$599 per job.** It's discontinued, but review sites still show it, so don't budget from those numbers.
> - **Last published enterprise rates ran $5,000-$32,000/mo.** Convert and Rediscovery were priced for large organizations, not small teams.
> - **No free tier or trial exists.** You can't test Leoforce without a sales process.
> - **Pin publishes every price, starting with a free plan.** Paid plans start at $99/mo with 850M+ profiles, and [SHRM (2026)](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/ai-hr-2026-from-hype-to-measured-human-centered-impact) finds 27% of organizations already use AI in recruiting.

## Arya by Leoforce Pricing: What Each Tier Actually Costs in 2026

Every current Leoforce product is sold by custom quote. According to the company's pricing page, cost depends on the products you choose, hiring volume, role mix, site traffic, region, and integrations. [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/leoforce), which tracks vendor rate cards, and [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/recruitment-marketing-platform/leoforce-profile/) both list Leoforce as custom-quote.

Below is the full lineup, with the last price Leoforce or a third party published for each:

| Product | Pricing Model | Last Published Cost | Status (Sept 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arya Pulse (Self-Service) | Per job | $199/job | Discontinued |
| Arya Pulse (Full-Service) | Per job, managed outreach | $599/job | Discontinued |
| Arya Quantum | Annual subscription | Never disclosed | Discontinued |
| Leoforce Convert | Monthly, by career-site traffic | $8,000-$32,000+/mo | Custom quote |
| Leoforce Rediscovery | Monthly, by database size + open jobs | $5,000-$15,000/mo | Custom quote |
| Leoforce Source | Programmatic job ads | Never disclosed | Custom quote |
| Leoforce Capture | Per qualified applicant | Never disclosed | Custom quote |
| Leoforce Signal | AI sales agent for staffing firms | Never disclosed | Custom quote |

Behind the new model sits an outcome-based pitch: Leoforce's homepage says that if a candidate does not qualify, you do not pay. That shifts risk onto the vendor, which is appealing. The tradeoff is that you can't estimate the Arya cost for your team until a sales rep scopes it.

Role volume is what makes per-job and per-outcome pricing expensive. Pin's data on recruiters who sourced every month from July 2025 through June 2026 shows a median of 11 distinct roles per quarter, roughly 44 a year, and 56% handled 10 or more. At the old $199 Pulse rate, 44 roles came to $8,756 a year. At $599 per role, the same workload cost $26,356.

What did Arya Quantum cost? Nobody outside Leoforce ever knew. Quantum pricing never appeared on Leoforce's site, G2, or TrustRadius, and the plan is now gone. The same opacity carries over to Source, Capture, and Signal, so buyers still enter a full sales process before they can compare costs.

**In our experience** building Pin, pricing opacity is a deliberate sales strategy, and it costs recruiting teams more than money. The team behind Pin also built Interseller, which was acquired by Greenhouse. Among teams evaluating AI sourcing tools, the most common complaint was consistent: they couldn't compare costs before entering a sales funnel. By then, switching costs were real, and their bargaining power was gone.

By contrast, Pin takes the opposite approach. Every plan on the [Pin pricing page](https://www.pin.com/pricing/) is public before you talk to anyone. You get a Free plan, Solo at $99/mo, Professional at $135 per user per month billed annually ($179 month-to-month), and custom Business pricing with unlimited seats. According to Pin's 2026 user survey, 91% of users reduced or eliminated LinkedIn Recruiter spend after switching to Pin. That kind of ROI is only measurable when you know what you're paying from day one.

## Hidden Costs and Pricing Gaps to Watch

Beyond the quoted price, Arya by Leoforce pricing has several cost variables that don't surface until you're deep in the sales process. Contact data quality is the most expensive one. On [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/248233/LeoForce/reviews/), one executive research recruiter wrote that contact info was "probably at least 80% of the time" inaccurate, which means paying for data that doesn't reach real candidates.

Budget for these items beyond the listed price:

### Contact Credits

Ask exactly how contact lookups are metered. Leoforce doesn't publish credit allowances or overage rates, and high sourcing volume can inflate costs well beyond the quote. Get the per-lookup price in writing before you sign.

### ATS Integration Setup

Integration coverage is broad (60+ ATS and CRM systems), but setup isn't instant. [Greenhouse's help center](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360039835752-Arya-by-Leoforce-integration) says configuration can take up to a month after Leoforce receives your credentials, followed by user training. Setup itself may be included, but a month of reduced productivity is a real cost.

### Contract Terms Aren't Published

Minimum commitments, billing frequency, and cancellation terms are all undisclosed. Enterprise products at five-figure monthly price points rarely offer month-to-month billing. Ask specifically about term length, auto-renewal, and exit clauses before signing.

### Onboarding and Training

Trained on data from 16,000+ recruiters, according to [Humancloud's Leoforce profile](https://humancloud.com/profile/leoforce), the platform still scores just 3.8 out of 5 for functionality on Software Advice. Several reviews mention a learning curve. Factor in training time, especially if your team is used to simpler sourcing tools or manual LinkedIn searching.

## Leoforce's Product Rebrand: What Changed in 2025-2026?

In 2025, Leoforce dropped the Arya name and split its offering into five separate products, a lineup we verified on its site in September 2026. Source runs programmatic job ads, Capture delivers pre-screened candidates, Convert is a career-site agent, and Rediscovery re-engages candidates already in your ATS. The newest, Signal, is an AI sales agent that helps staffing firms turn website visitors into client leads. Old goarya.com links now redirect to leoforce.com, and the Pulse product page returns a 404.

What does this mean for buyers? First, Arya AI recruiting as review sites describe it no longer exists. Capterra and Software Advice still list the product as "Leoforce (Arya)," and most Arya by Leoforce review content dates from 2023-2024. Second, the lineup moved away from a self-serve search tool toward managed, outcome-based services.

This repositioning matters for pricing research. Comparing Leoforce to tools like Pin or Workable using older review data? Verify you're evaluating the current lineup, not the 2023 version.

## What Do You Get With Arya by Leoforce?

SHRM's [State of AI in HR report](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/ai-hr-2026-from-hype-to-measured-human-centered-impact) (2026) found that 27% of organizations now use AI in recruiting, the most common AI use case in HR. Within this growing market, Leoforce sells managed recruiting outcomes rather than software your recruiters work in daily. Here's what each product delivered, and what it cost when prices were public.

### Arya Pulse (Discontinued, Was $199-$599/Job)

Pulse delivered AI-matched candidate lists for individual roles. Self-service ($199) provided a ranked shortlist from Leoforce's database, while full-service ($599) added managed outreach by Leoforce's team. Neither tier included ongoing platform access, so your access ended when the job closed.

A 2024 vendor case study with blueStone Staffing reported a 52% reduction in time-to-submit and a 26% increase in weekly submittals per recruiter, per [Humancloud](https://humancloud.com/profile/leoforce). Those are solid numbers, though they reflect a single agency's experience rather than a broad benchmark. Leoforce has since removed that case study from its site.

### Arya Quantum (Discontinued, Never Priced Publicly)

Quantum was the always-on subscription: continuous AI sourcing, candidate scoring, ATS integration, analytics, and team collaboration. Without published pricing, it was impossible to calculate cost per hire before entering the sales funnel. Leoforce no longer sells it.

### Leoforce Convert (Last Published at $8,000-$32,000+/Mo)

This is a career-site agent, not a sourcing tool. Convert chats with visitors on your careers page, pre-screens applicants, and routes qualified candidates into your ATS. The last public rate card, still tracked by [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/leoforce), charged $8,000/mo for up to 100,000 monthly visitors, $17,000 for up to 250,000, and $32,000 for up to 500,000. Busier sites were custom. A midsize company hiring for 5-10 roles per quarter won't justify that spend.

### Leoforce Rediscovery (Last Published at $5,000-$15,000/Mo)

Rediscovery re-engages candidates already in your ATS through automated SMS and email. Its old rate card, per the same tracker, started at $5,000/mo for up to 5 million records and 100 jobs. The top tier reached $15,000/mo for up to 10 million records and 1,000 jobs. It suits staffing agencies sitting on huge candidate backlogs. It's redundant if your ATS already runs re-engagement workflows.

### Leoforce Source, Capture, and Signal (Quote Only)

Source and Capture are the closest successors to Pulse: one buys job-ad reach, the other charges per qualified applicant delivered. Signal targets staffing firms' sales teams rather than recruiters. None of the three has ever had a public price.

## Who Is Arya by Leoforce a Fit For?

According to [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/ai-hr-2026-from-hype-to-measured-human-centered-impact) (2026), 60% of extra-large organizations (5,000+ employees) have implemented AI in HR, compared with about a third of small businesses. That adoption gap matches Leoforce's pricing reality: the platform is built for larger teams with bigger budgets and specific use cases.

Large agencies and enterprise teams find the most value with Leoforce, specifically:

-   **Large staffing agencies** filling 50+ roles per month that want to outsource candidate delivery. The blueStone Staffing case study showed measurable gains at high volume.
-   **Enterprise talent teams** with existing ATS infrastructure who need a supplemental sourcing layer. The 60+ ATS and CRM integrations reduce migration friction, though configuration can take a month.
-   **Companies with massive candidate databases** that want to re-engage past applicants through Rediscovery, last published at $5,000+/mo. If you're sitting on millions of ATS records, automated re-engagement could surface hires without new sourcing spend.

Smaller teams and solo recruiters face specific friction points with Leoforce:

-   **Small to midsize recruiting teams** that need predictable monthly costs. Outcome-based quotes vary with volume, and the last published enterprise rates started at $5,000/mo.
-   **Solo recruiters or small agencies** getting started with AI sourcing. With no free tier or self-serve plan, you're in a sales process before seeing a single candidate match.
-   **Teams that want to run their own searches and sequences.** Leoforce sells managed outcomes rather than a seat your recruiters work in every day.
-   **Recruiters who want to test before buying.** With no free tier, no trial, and a required demo just to learn pricing, the evaluation friction is high. Platforms with transparent pricing and free tiers let you run real searches and measure results before committing any budget.

More options enter this market every quarter. Locking into an enterprise contract without testing alternatives first carries real opportunity cost, especially when tools with lower entry points deliver comparable or broader functionality.
