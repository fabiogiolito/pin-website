Workable pricing runs from $299 per month on the Standard plan to $719 per month on Enterprise for companies with 1-20 employees. Every tier scales with your company's employee headcount rather than recruiter seat count. Gone are the old $149 Starter plan and the pay-per-job option. Unlike most mid-market ATS platforms, Workable actually publishes these rates - a rarity in recruiting software. As a complete Workable ATS pricing review, this guide covers the real cost of each plan, what's included, and where the platform falls short.

But the published number isn't what you'll actually pay. Adding Video interviews+ ($109/mo) and Texting+ ($89/mo) to the Standard plan pushes the real monthly cost to $497, which is 66% above the advertised $299 base, according to [Workable's pricing page](https://www.workable.com/pricing) (October 2026). And those aren't niche extras. They're features most recruiting teams consider essential.

This 2026 Workable ATS pricing guide breaks down all three tiers (Standard, Premier, and Enterprise), maps out add-on costs that inflate your bill, and compares Workable to five ATS alternatives. If you're also evaluating other platforms, our [Greenhouse](https://www.pin.com/blog/greenhouse-pricing/) and [Lever](https://www.pin.com/blog/lever-pricing/) pricing breakdowns cover those in the same detail. Whether you're evaluating Workable for the first time or weighing a renewal, you'll know exactly what to budget for.

> **TL;DR:**
>
> - **Published pricing runs $299-$719/mo.** That covers three tiers (Standard, Premier, Enterprise) for 1-20 employees on annual billing, scaled by company headcount, not seats. The $149 Starter plan is gone.
> - **Add-ons push Standard 66% above the base.** Video interviews+ ($109/mo) and Texting+ ($89/mo) bring the real Standard cost to $497/mo vs. the advertised $299 ([Workable](https://www.workable.com/pricing), 2026).
> - **Headcount cliff effects hit fast.** Crossing from 20 to 21 employees can spike Standard from $299/mo to ~$500/mo, a 67% jump from a single hire.
> - **The AI agent runs on credits.** Every tier includes the 400M+ profile sourcing suite and 3,000 free AI credits, but Workable Agent usage beyond that costs $0.095-$0.12 per credit.
> - **Lower cost, better sourcing elsewhere.** For sourcing-first teams, [Pin](https://www.pin.com/) is the top choice: a free plan, paid plans from $99/mo, 850M+ profiles, and 5x better outreach response rates than industry averages.

## What Does Workable Actually Cost in 2026?

As of October 2026, [Workable's pricing page](https://www.workable.com/pricing) lists three tiers, from $299/month on Standard to $719/month on Enterprise. Pricing is based on total employee headcount, not the number of recruiters using the tool. Older guides still cite Starter ($149/mo) and Pay-per-Job ($99/job/mo), but neither is offered anymore.

See [BambooHR Pricing 2026: Plans, Features, and Limitations](https://www.pin.com/blog/bamboohr-pricing/) for a comparable mid-market ATS breakdown.

Each plan's base cost breaks down as follows:

| Plan | Monthly (Annual Billing) | Annual Total | Month-to-Month | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $299/mo | $3,588/yr | Available (annual saves 20%) | Small teams, steady hiring |
| Premier | $599/mo | $7,188/yr | Annual only | Teams that need video, texting, and assessments bundled |
| Enterprise | $719/mo | $8,628/yr | Annual only | Larger organizations |

On annual billing, that works out to $3,588/yr on Standard, $7,188/yr on Premier, and $8,628/yr on Enterprise. Every figure here is the baseline Workable ATS pricing before add-ons or headcount scaling enter the picture.

Those are the base rates for companies with 1-20 employees. Costs scale sharply once you cross headcount thresholds:

Underneath the headline rates, a few details of Workable's pricing model matter:

-   **Employee-based scaling.** Your price increases as your company grows, even if your hiring volume stays flat. Crossing from 20 to 21 employees triggers a significant jump.
-   **Annual billing saves 20%.** Workable's page labels annual billing "save 20%," and Standard is the only tier you can pay month-to-month.
-   **Premier and Enterprise are annual-only.** No month-to-month option on the top two tiers. You commit to 12 months before you can fully evaluate the platform at scale.
-   **15-day free trial available.** Workable offers a free trial with Standard-tier features and no credit card required (Workable Agent excluded) - one advantage over competitors that require paid contracts from day one.

Headcount-based pricing creates a cliff effect that catches growing companies off guard. A 20-person startup on Standard pays $299/mo ($3,588/yr). Hire one more person and the same plan jumps to roughly $500/mo ($6,000/yr) - a 67% increase triggered by a single hire. Timing your Workable contract renewal carefully matters when you're approaching a headcount threshold.

## Workable Plans: Feature-by-Feature Breakdown

Across three tiers, Workable locks meaningful capabilities behind higher plans. Understanding these Workable ATS features and their pricing reveals both the product's strengths and its limitations. Standard is the entry point and already includes the sourcing suite, while Premier and Enterprise mostly bundle the add-ons Standard sells separately.

| Feature | Standard | Premier | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Job Posting & Distribution | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Candidate Pipeline | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Interview Scheduling | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Sourcing Suite (400M+ profiles) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| E-Signatures | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Video Interviews | ⚠️ $109/mo add-on | ✅ Included | ✅ Included |
| SMS/Texting | ⚠️ $89/mo add-on | ✅ Included | ✅ Included |
| Assessments | ⚠️ $59/mo add-on | ✅ Included | ✅ Included |
| Performance & Engagement | ⚠️ $39/mo add-on | ✅ Included | ✅ Included |
| Workable Agent (AI) | ⚠️ Add-on, credit-based | ⚠️ Add-on, credit-based | ⚠️ Add-on, credit-based |

Nothing separates Workable's plan tiers more clearly than the add-on bundle. Standard includes the sourcing suite but sells video, texting, assessments, and performance tools separately. Premier and Enterprise include all four, so teams that need two or more of them on Standard end up paying close to Premier's price anyway.

Video interviews, texting, and assessments are the other major pain point. Most recruiters consider these table stakes, yet Workable treats them as premium add-ons on Standard. Only Premier and Enterprise bundle them in the base price. That distinction matters more than it looks - we'll break down the real cost impact in the next section.

### Which Plan Should You Pick?

**Standard ($299/mo)** is the entry point now that Starter is gone. Core ATS, the sourcing suite, e-signatures, and 3,000 free AI credits are included, and it's the only tier with month-to-month billing. Add Video interviews+ and Texting+ and the effective price climbs to $497/mo, which most buyers don't anticipate.

**Premier ($599/mo)** bundles video, texting, assessments, referrals, and performance tools. Annual commitment required. If you'd spend $450+ on Standard with add-ons anyway, Premier's flat rate starts looking reasonable.

**Enterprise ($719/mo)** is the top published tier, billed annually only. Positioned for larger organizations, it's worth confirming exactly what Enterprise adds over Premier with Workable's sales team before you budget for it.

## What Hidden Fees Does Workable Charge?

Four types of costs accrue beyond Workable's published base price: add-on features (video interviews, SMS, assessments), headcount threshold jumps, annual renewal increases, and Premier's annual-only billing requirement. Together, these can push the real Workable cost 66% or more above what's advertised.

Credit where it's due: Workable publishes its base prices, unlike [Greenhouse's pricing](https://www.pin.com/blog/greenhouse-pricing/) and [Lever](https://www.pin.com/blog/lever-pricing/), which require a sales call. Publishing a price, though, doesn't guarantee a transparent total cost. Several expenses only become clear once you're evaluating the platform seriously - these are the Workable pricing pros and cons that buyer reviews flag most often.

### 2\. Headcount Threshold Jumps

Growing from 20 to 21 employees on the Standard plan triggers a 67% price jump, according to [SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/resources/how-much-does-workable-cost) (2025). Employee count brackets drive pricing, not a smooth per-person rate - that's a $201/mo increase triggered by a single hire, with no additional features or users included.

### 3\. Annual Renewal Increases

Users report 8-9% annual price increases at renewal, according to buyer feedback compiled by [OutSail](https://www.outsail.co/post/workable-reviews---pricing-pros-cons-and-user-feedback) (2025). Those who push back typically negotiate increases down to 3-5%. Over a three-year contract on Standard:

-   Year 1: $3,588 ($299/mo)
-   Year 2 (at 8%): $3,875
-   Year 3 (at 8%): $4,185
-   **Total: $11,648 vs. $10,764 at flat pricing**

### Where Workable Falls Short

Three limitations consistently appear in user reviews across [OutSail](https://www.outsail.co/post/workable-reviews---pricing-pros-cons-and-user-feedback) and [ITQlick](https://www.itqlick.com/workable/pricing):

-   **Headcount scaling.** The employee-count pricing model penalizes growth. A 50-person company on Standard pays roughly $500/mo for the same features a 20-person company gets at $299/mo.

All of this adds up to a solid entry-level ATS for small teams with modest hiring needs. As your team grows or your sourcing demands increase, though, the cost-to-capability ratio deteriorates.
