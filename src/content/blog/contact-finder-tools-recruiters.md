Contact data ages faster after job changes than most recruiters realize - and mobile numbers are especially volatile. B2B contact data decays at roughly 2.1% per month overall, but numbers go stale at roughly 18% annually, according to the [DMA's 2025 data quality research](https://dma.org.uk/). The churn isn't only about number portability. As of January 2024, 22% of U.S. workers had been with their employer a year or less, per the [Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/tenure.nr0.htm). That means roughly one in five contacts in any database has already switched jobs in the past year. Meanwhile, poor data quality reduces productivity by up to 30% across go-to-market teams, per Gartner's data quality research. Reaching candidates by phone or SMS - not just email - requires a tool that prioritizes verified mobile numbers and direct-dial data, not one that treats phone as an expensive afterthought.

## Why Phone and SMS Contact Data Outperforms Email Alone

Phone and SMS outperform email alone for one reason: they reach passive candidates on channels they still check. A cold recruiting email competes with a flooded work inbox, but a well-timed call or text gets noticed. Outbound sourcing consistently outperforms inbound applications for quality of hire, according to [LinkedIn's Global Talent Trends research](https://www.linkedin.com/business/talent/blog/talent-acquisition/global-recruiting-trends). But outbound only works when you can actually reach people - and for a growing segment of candidates, email isn't the channel that gets a response.

Single-channel cold email reply rates hover in the low single digits for most recruiting teams. Here's the problem: passive candidates - the ones already employed and not actively job hunting - often ignore unfamiliar emails entirely. Their work inbox is flooded. Their personal inbox filters recruiter messages into promotions or spam. But a well-timed phone call or SMS? That gets attention. SMS open rates exceed 90% within the first three minutes of delivery, according to Gartner research. Adding a phone touchpoint to an outreach sequence can double or triple overall response rates compared to email alone. [Gong Labs](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-hidden-power-of-cold-calling-insights-from-300m-calls/) analyzed more than 300 million B2B cold calls. Pairing a call with email nearly doubled the email reply rate - 3.44% versus 1.81% - even when the call never connected live.

This is the core difference between a [single-channel email finder](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-email-finder-tools-recruiters/) and a multi-channel contact enrichment platform. Email finders return an address. Multi-channel platforms return verified mobile numbers for SMS outreach, direct-dial phone numbers for calls, personal and work email addresses, LinkedIn and GitHub profile links, and sometimes personal websites or portfolio URLs. A complete contact picture fuels the multi-channel sequences that actually get candidates to respond. Email-only outreach reaches candidates through one channel when they respond to three or four. The channel you reach them on matters as much as the data itself. **Across outreach tracked through Pin, LinkedIn messages earn a response over 3x as often as email - 16.8% versus 5.0% (Pin outreach data, 2026).** That's why surfacing a verified LinkedIn profile, not just an email address, is now table stakes.

What recruiters tell us: adding a third or fourth contact channel changes response rates fast. But the bigger shift is in candidate quality. The recruiters who respond to multi-channel outreach tend to be more open to new roles than those who click a job ad. According to Pin's 2026 user survey, sequences combining email, LinkedIn messages, and SMS see 5x better response rates than single-channel email outreach. What's less obvious is where those gains come from. They don't come from sending more messages. They come from reaching candidates on a channel they actually check. A text about a relevant role lands differently than another email in a flooded work inbox. Most contact finders stop at the lookup - they return a number and leave you to handle outreach separately. That gap between finding a contact and acting on it is exactly what multi-channel platforms like Pin are designed to eliminate.

## What Should Recruiters Prioritize in a Multi-Channel Contact Finder?

Recruiters should prioritize verified mobile-number quality and the phone-to-email credit ratio above raw database size. Those two factors decide whether you can actually reach candidates by call or text without draining your credit budget. AI adoption in HR nearly doubled from 26% to 43% between 2024 and 2025, according to [SHRM's 2025 Talent Trends report](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-talent-trends/). That surge means more tools competing for recruiter attention - and more confusion about what actually matters. Knowing how to identify multiple contact channels - email, phone, and LinkedIn - for each candidate, then acting on them without stitching together separate tools, is the core challenge these platforms solve. When evaluating multi-channel platforms specifically (not email-only tools), here are the six criteria that separate useful recruiter contact finders from expensive data directories:

-   **Mobile number quality and verification:** The most important differentiator in this category. Does the tool return verified direct-dial mobile numbers, or generic switchboard lines? Verified cell numbers are what you need for SMS outreach and direct calls. Tools that only return office landlines are useless for reaching passive candidates after hours.
-   **Credit costs for phone vs. email:** Most tools charge 3-10x more credits for phone numbers than emails. Apollo.io charges 8x more. Lusha charges 5-10 credits per phone vs. 1 per email. Pin charges 4 credits per phone vs. 2 per email - a 2x ratio that's the most balanced in this category. Compare phone costs specifically, not headline pricing.
-   **Multi-channel coverage depth:** A good multi-channel finder returns mobile phone, work phone, personal email, work email, LinkedIn profile, and GitHub/social links in a single search. Some tools only return one or two of these. Check what "contact data" actually means before committing.
-   **SMS outreach capability:** Finding a number is step one. Can you send SMS sequences from the same platform, or do you need to export the number to a separate tool? Integrated SMS outreach eliminates manual data transfers and keeps your candidate communications in one place.
-   **Database size with phone coverage:** Tools range from 50M to 850M+ profiles, but coverage for phone numbers is always smaller than for email. A tool with 850M+ profiles might have phone data for 40-60% of them. Ask about phone-specific coverage, not just total database size.
-   **Whether the tool complies with phone/SMS regulations:** TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act) in the US, GDPR in the EU/UK, and state-level privacy laws all regulate phone and SMS outreach differently than email. SOC 2 certification and documented compliance matter more when you're handling mobile numbers.

## Which Multi-Channel Contact Finders Include Full Recruiting Workflows?

These five tools go beyond standalone phone or email lookup. They combine multi-channel contact discovery with some form of outreach automation or pipeline management - so you're not exporting phone numbers into one tool and emails into another. According to [SHRM's 2025 research](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-talent-trends/), 43% of HR teams now use AI in recruiting, and many are consolidating their tool stacks in the process. Recruiters who want phone, email, and social contact data feeding directly into outreach sequences should start here.

### 1\. Pin - Multi-Channel Contact Discovery with Built-In SMS, Email, and LinkedIn Outreach

![Screenshot of the Pin AI recruiting platform homepage](/images/urlbox/pin-com-934154a1-1.webp)

Pin is an AI-powered recruiting assistant that handles sourcing, multi-channel contact discovery, outreach, and interview scheduling in one platform. Its 850M+ candidate database covers 100% of North America and Europe. Phone number lookups cost 4 credits ($0.40 each) and email lookups cost 2 credits ($0.20 each), with add-on packs available at 500 credits for $50. That 2x ratio between phone and email costs is the most balanced in this category - compared to 5-10x at Lusha and 8x at Apollo.io.

What sets Pin apart from standalone phone lookup tools: the phone numbers, emails, and social profiles you discover feed directly into automated multi-channel outreach sequences across SMS, email, and LinkedIn. You find a candidate's verified mobile number and send an SMS sequence from the same platform - no exporting, no switching tools, no manual data entry. That integrated workflow delivers 5x better response rates on outreach than industry averages. Pin is SOC 2 Type 2 certified with bias elimination built into every step of the sourcing process.

As recruiter Nick Poloni, President at Cascadia Search Group, put it: "The sourcing data is incredible, scanning 850M+ profiles with recruiter-level precision to uncover perfect-fit candidates I'd never find otherwise. Best of all, the outreach feels genuinely personalized and non-generic, driving sky-high reply rates where candidates even thank me for the thoughtful messages."

**Pricing:** Free tier available (no credit card). Starter at $100/mo. Professional at $149/mo (annual). Business at $249/mo (annual). Month-to-month billing is available, with discounts on annual contracts. Contact credits: 2 per email, 4 per phone number. 500-credit add-on packs for $50.

**Good for:** Recruiters who want to find phone numbers and emails, then send SMS, email, and LinkedIn outreach from the same platform - without stitching together four separate tools.

**Limitation:** If you only need raw phone number data without the recruiting workflow, standalone phone finders like Swordfish AI cost less per lookup.

### 2\. ContactOut - Phone and Social Profile Discovery Across LinkedIn and GitHub

![Screenshot of the ContactOut recruiter contact finder homepage](/images/urlbox/contactout-com-b60d7726-1.webp)

ContactOut is one of the few contact lookup tools designed specifically for recruiters rather than sales teams. Its database covers 300M professionals and 30M companies, with a Chrome extension that works on LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, and GitHub profiles. That GitHub integration surfaces developer contact data - including personal emails, phone numbers, and portfolio links - that other tools miss entirely because developers often keep minimal LinkedIn profiles.

ContactOut offers triple-verified emails and phone numbers, an AI email writer, and contact enrichment across profiles. Access to phone numbers requires the Email + Phone plan ($79/mo annual), which includes unlimited email and phone reveals with 600 exports per month. That pricing structure effectively doubles your monthly cost compared to the email-only plan.

**Pricing:** Free tier (5 emails/day). Email plan at $39/mo (annual). Email + Phone at $79/mo (annual). Enterprise at custom pricing.

**Good for:** Technical recruiters who need phone numbers and social profile links for developers who are hard to reach through email alone.

**Limitation:** Phone data locked behind the $79/mo tier - the $39/mo plan is email-only. Export limits (300-600/month) cap high-volume use. No built-in SMS outreach or scheduling.

### 3\. Lusha - LinkedIn Phone Number Reveals with GDPR Compliance

![Screenshot of the Lusha contact data platform homepage](/images/urlbox/lusha-com-b536362a-1.webp)

Lusha is a LinkedIn-native contact tool with a Chrome extension that reveals phone numbers and email addresses directly on candidate profiles. Its database covers 300M+ business profiles with a claimed 81% accuracy rate. Lusha stands out on compliance - it's GDPR and CCPA compliant, and its Signals API can alert you when candidates change jobs or receive new funding.

Bulk reveal saves time: you can reveal up to 25 contacts at once from Sales Navigator. Costs escalate at the phone reveal step, though. At 5-10 credits per number versus 1 credit per email, a recruiter who needs 50 numbers per week will burn through credits 5-10x faster than one who only needs emails. Lusha acquired Novacy in January 2025 and expanded its database to 300M+ contacts.

**Pricing:** Free tier (40-70 credits/month). Pro at $29.90/mo. Premium at $69.90/mo. Scale at custom pricing. Phone reveals cost 5-10 credits each vs. 1 credit per email - making the effective per-phone cost $1.50-$3.00 depending on plan.

**Good for:** Recruiters who need occasional phone number lookups alongside email reveals directly from LinkedIn profiles, with documented GDPR compliance.

**Limitation:** Phone number reveals consume credits 5-10x faster than email reveals. At volume, Lusha's phone costs exceed Pin's by a wide margin. SMS outreach isn't built in - you'll need a separate tool to reach candidates via text.

### 4\. Apollo.io - Built-In Dialer but Expensive Phone Credits

![Screenshot of the Apollo.io B2B sales intelligence platform homepage](/images/urlbox/apollo-io-99f03835-1.webp)

Apollo.io is a B2B sales intelligence platform that many recruiting teams have adopted for its combination of contact data, email sequencing, and built-in dialer. Its database covers 265M+ contacts across 30M+ companies. The built-in dialer is a genuine advantage for recruiters who work the phones - you can call directly from the platform without a separate tool. Education filters (school, degree, graduation year) are also useful for campus recruiting workflows.

Phone credit costs are where Apollo falls short. Credits cost 8x more for a phone number than for an email. On the Basic plan, that means you'll blow through your monthly allocation fast if phone outreach is central to your workflow. Credits expire monthly with no rollover.

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Basic at $49/mo. Professional at $79/mo. Organization at $119/mo (min. 3 users). Credits expire monthly with no rollover. Phone number lookups cost 8x more credits than emails.

**Good for:** Teams that want a built-in dialer for phone calls alongside email outreach, and don't mind the 8x credit premium for phone numbers.

**Limitation:** Phone credits deplete 8x faster than email credits, making phone-heavy workflows expensive. Credits expire monthly with no rollover. The platform is sales-first, so ATS integrations and recruiter-specific features are secondary. No SMS outreach.

### 5\. SignalHire - Separate Phone-Only Plan for Teams That Prioritize Calls

![Screenshot of the SignalHire contact finder homepage](/images/urlbox/signalhire-com-9a8033cc-1.webp)

SignalHire searches 850M+ profiles and offers something unique in this category: a phone-only pricing plan. At $39/mo (annual), you get phone number lookups without paying for email credits you don't need. Recruiters who already have email addresses from LinkedIn but need mobile numbers for call and SMS outreach will find this structure efficient. All paid plans include unlimited user seats and unlimited exports.

Among the tools on this list, SignalHire is the only one with a built-in visual pipeline tracking board - letting you drag and drop candidates through stages, set deadlines, and flag profiles. Integrations include Salesforce, Zoho Recruit, Pipedrive, JazzHR, and Recruitee.

**Pricing:** Free tier (5-10 credits/month). Phone Only at $39/mo (annual). Emails Only at $57/mo (annual). Pro (both) at $110/mo (annual). All plans include unlimited users and unlimited exports.

**Good for:** Recruiting teams that primarily need phone numbers and want to avoid paying for email credits they won't use. The phone-only plan is the most cost-effective way to get mobile data at scale.

**Limitation:** If you need both phone and email, the Pro plan at $110/mo starts to approach Pin's pricing ($100/mo) without the automated email outreach, SMS tasks, or scheduling. The pipeline tracking is basic compared to dedicated ATS platforms.

## Which Tools Specialize in Phone Numbers, Regional Data, or Niche Channels?

Cell numbers churn at roughly 18% annually per DMA 2025 research - faster than any other contact data type. Enterprise sales intelligence platforms like [ZoomInfo](https://www.pin.com/blog/zoominfo-pricing/) offer massive B2B databases but start at $15,000+/year - overkill for most recruiting teams. That decay rate is why phone-specialist tools matter: these five focus on specific contact channels - verified cell phone numbers, European phone data, real-time mobile lookups, or domain-based discovery. Unlike the full-platform tools above, they solve one problem exceptionally well rather than covering the entire recruiting workflow. They're best used alongside a primary AI candidate sourcing platform to fill gaps in your phone and candidate contact data.

### 6\. Kaspr - European Phone Numbers with GDPR-Compliant Data Sourcing

![Screenshot of the Kaspr phone number finder homepage](/images/urlbox/kaspr-io-9d2c5542.webp)

Kaspr is part of the Cognism group and is built for GDPR-compliant contact discovery with particularly strong European phone number coverage. If you recruit in the EU or UK and need verified mobile numbers for candidates in those regions, Kaspr's data quality for European contacts is notably better than US-centric tools. The Chrome extension works on LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, and Recruiter Lite (on the Business plan).

Credits for phone lookups are limited on lower tiers, so recruiters who need high-volume phone data should plan for the Business plan. GDPR applies to candidate phone data regardless of whether it's a business or personal number - Kaspr was built to comply with that reality from the ground up.

**Pricing:** Free tier (15 B2B email credits, 5 phone credits). Starter at $49/mo. Business at $99/mo (annual, includes Recruiter Lite access). Enterprise at custom pricing.

**Good for:** Recruiters hiring in the EU/UK who need verified European phone numbers from a GDPR-aligned data source.

**Limitation:** Phone credits are severely limited on the free and Starter tiers (5 phone credits/mo on free). North American phone data quality drops noticeably compared to European coverage. No SMS or phone outreach automation - you'll need separate tools to act on the numbers you find.

### 7\. RocketReach - Phone Numbers Require Pro Plan ($75/mo)

![Screenshot of the RocketReach professional database homepage](/images/urlbox/rocketreach-co-129f600b-1.webp)

RocketReach searches 700M+ professional profiles across 60M+ companies with a pay-on-success model - it only charges a credit when a verified contact is found. That's recruiter-friendly for niche roles where many lookups return nothing. However, mobile and direct-dial phone numbers are locked behind the Pro plan at $75/mo (annual). The Essentials plan ($33/mo) only returns email addresses.

**Pricing:** Free tier (5 lookups/month). Essentials at $33/mo (annual) for 1,200 exports/year - email only. Pro at $75/mo (annual) for 6,000 exports/year with mobile and direct phone. Ultimate at $175/mo (annual) for 20,000 exports/year with API and Salesforce sync.

**Good for:** Recruiters who want phone numbers from a large database and are willing to commit to the Pro tier for mobile data access.

**Limitation:** Access to phone numbers requires the Pro plan - the entry-level Essentials plan is email-only. Annual plans don't allow credit top-ups; you must upgrade tiers. SMS and phone outreach tools aren't built in. Not LinkedIn-native like Lusha or ContactOut.

### 8\. Swordfish AI - Cell Phone Number Specialist

![Screenshot of the Swordfish AI cell phone number finder homepage](/images/urlbox/swordfish-ai-a5820ab7.webp)

Swordfish AI is the only tool on this list built primarily around cell phone numbers. While every other tool treats phone data as a premium add-on (3-10x more expensive than email credits), Swordfish makes verified direct-dial mobile numbers its core product. Its Chrome extension, which works across LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter, GitHub, and Stack Overflow, makes it easy to surface cell numbers wherever candidates have public profiles.

**Pricing:** Solo at $99/mo (fair-use limits). Teams at $599/mo (annual) with API, CSV enrichment, and multiple users. Enterprise at custom pricing. No free tier.

**Good for:** Recruiters who depend on phone outreach and need verified cell numbers, not just work emails. If SMS or phone calls are part of your outreach sequence, Swordfish fills a gap that other tools don't.

**Limitation:** No free tier. "Fair use" limits on the Solo plan aren't publicly defined. At Swordfish, the jump from $99/mo Solo to $599/mo Teams is steep. Treat it as a supplement to your sourcing stack, not a replacement.

### 9\. Hunter.io - Email-Only, No Phone or SMS Data

![Screenshot of the Hunter.io email finder homepage](/images/urlbox/hunter-io-8d91f1ee-1.webp)

Hunter.io takes a domain-based approach to contact discovery: enter "acme.com" and it returns all known email addresses at that company along with the email pattern (e.g., first.last format). It's included on this list as a contrast point - Hunter is purely an email finder with zero phone number, SMS, or social profile data. If multi-channel contact data is what you need, Hunter isn't the right tool. For a detailed comparison of email-only tools, see our [email finder tools guide](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-email-finder-tools-recruiters/).

**Pricing:** Free tier (50 credits/month). Starter at $34/mo (annual). Growth at $104/mo (annual). Scale at $209/mo (annual).

**Good for:** Recruiters who only need email addresses and prefer a domain-based search approach. Not relevant for phone or SMS outreach workflows.

**Limitation:** No phone numbers, no mobile data, no social profiles - email addresses only. If your outreach strategy includes phone calls or SMS, Hunter doesn't contribute to that workflow at all.

### 10\. Wiza - Real-Time Phone Numbers at $0.35 Each

![Screenshot of the Wiza real-time contact finder homepage](/images/urlbox/getwiza-com-c4051141.webp)

Wiza claims the highest accuracy in this group: [99% email deliverability](/blog/email-finder-vendor-audit/) with less than 1% bounce rate. Instead of pulling from a cached database, Wiza live-sources contact data from LinkedIn in real time. Freshness extends to phone numbers too. Wiza's phone data is pulled in real time rather than from a database that might be months old, which matters for mobile numbers that churn at 18% per year.

Phone numbers are priced at $0.35 each on the Email plan, or included in the Starter plan's 100-phone-number monthly allocation. Wiza includes one-click bulk export from Sales Navigator and Recruiter Lite, plus a Monitor feature that tracks job changes across your prospect lists.

**Pricing:** Free tier (20 emails, 5 phone numbers/month). Starter at $49/mo (100 emails + 100 phone numbers). Email plan at $83/mo (annual) for unlimited emails with $0.35 per phone number. Pay-as-you-go option at $0.15 per valid email.

**Good for:** Recruiters who need fresh, real-time phone numbers with high accuracy, and who already have a LinkedIn Sales Navigator subscription.

**Limitation:** Requires an active LinkedIn Sales Navigator subscription ($79-$149/mo separately). At $0.35 per phone number, 200 lookups per month adds $70 to your bill. Total cost (Wiza + Sales Navigator) often exceeds $130-$200/mo. No SMS outreach built in.

## How Do You Choose the Right Multi-Channel Contact Finder?

Choosing the right tool depends on which contact channels drive your placements. Recruiters who close candidates primarily through email may find a simpler [email finder tool](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-email-finder-tools-recruiters/) is enough. When phone calls, SMS, or social outreach are part of your workflow, here's how to choose:

**For all-in-one phone, SMS, email, and LinkedIn outreach:** Pin handles sourcing, multi-channel contact discovery, and multi-channel outreach sequences across SMS, email, and LinkedIn in a single platform. Phone lookups cost 4 credits ($0.40) - the most balanced phone-to-email credit ratio in this category. Pin's multi-channel outreach delivers 5x better response rates than industry averages - [start with the free tier](https://www.pin.com/) to test it against your current workflow.

**For verified cell numbers as a primary need:** Swordfish AI is the only tool built around verified mobile numbers. Every other tool treats phone data as a premium add-on; Swordfish makes it the core product. At $99/mo with flat-rate pricing, it's the simplest option for phone-heavy workflows - but separate tools are needed for sourcing and outreach.

**For phone-only pricing without paying for email:** SignalHire's $39/mo phone-only plan is the most cost-effective way to get mobile numbers at scale. Avoid paying for email credits you don't need.

**For European phone numbers with GDPR compliance:** Kaspr's European phone data quality is notably stronger than US-centric tools, and its GDPR-first architecture (backed by Cognism) handles the complexity of EU/UK candidate phone data.

**For real-time phone data freshness:** Wiza's live-sourcing approach means phone numbers are pulled in real time rather than served from a cached database. At $0.35 per phone number, it's more expensive than credit-based tools at volume, but accuracy justifies the cost for high-value roles.

**For developer contact data beyond LinkedIn:** ContactOut's GitHub and Stack Overflow integration surfaces phone numbers and emails for developers who don't maintain LinkedIn profiles. Phone data requires the $79/mo tier.

## Do Contact Finder Tools Comply with GDPR?

Most of these tools claim GDPR compliance, but how thoroughly they document it varies widely - and for phone data specifically, only a handful back it with a Data Processing Agreement and a clear legal basis. Nearly 20 U.S. states have [enacted their own data privacy laws](https://iapp.org/resources/article/us-state-privacy-legislation-tracker/) as of 2025, expanding beyond California's CCPA to cover consent requirements, opt-out rights, and data deletion obligations. GDPR applies to any person's data, business contact or consumer alike. That "B2B email" you found on LinkedIn needs the same legal basis as a consumer's personal email when the candidate is in the EU or UK.

Here's what to check before choosing a tool for cross-border recruiting:

-   **GDPR compliance documentation:** Kaspr (Cognism group) and Lusha offer the most transparent GDPR compliance posture. Ask for a Data Processing Agreement before processing EU/UK candidate data.
-   **SOC 2 certification:** Pin is SOC 2 Type 2 certified with documented encryption, access controls, and network security protocols. Check [trust.pin.com](https://trust.pin.com/) for full compliance details.
-   **Data sourcing transparency:** Understand where the tool gets its contact data. Aggregated public records, web scraping, and user-contributed data all carry different regulatory implications.
-   **Do-not-contact list integration:** Some tools check against national and industry-specific do-not-call lists before surfacing phone numbers. This matters most for phone outreach in regulated industries.

Bottom line: being compliant isn't a checkbox. Recruiting across borders means prioritizing tools that can document their legal basis for processing candidate data, not just ones that claim "GDPR compliant" in their marketing.

### What's the difference between a multi-channel contact finder and an email finder?

An [email finder](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-email-finder-tools-recruiters/) returns only email addresses. A multi-channel contact finder returns verified phone numbers (mobile and direct-dial), email addresses, social profile links (LinkedIn, GitHub), and sometimes personal websites or portfolio URLs. For recruiters running outreach sequences that include phone calls, SMS, and email, a multi-channel platform provides the complete candidate picture that email-only tools miss.

### Why do passive candidates respond better to phone and SMS than email?

Passive candidates - those not actively job hunting - tend to ignore unfamiliar emails because their inboxes are flooded. SMS open rates exceed 90% within the first three minutes of delivery, according to Gartner. A phone call or text from a recruiter about a relevant opportunity gets noticed in a way that one more email in a crowded inbox does not. That's why multi-channel outreach sequences that include phone and SMS consistently outperform email-only approaches.
