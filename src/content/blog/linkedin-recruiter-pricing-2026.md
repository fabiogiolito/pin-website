LinkedIn Recruiter pricing ranges from $1,680 per year for one Recruiter Lite seat to roughly $8,000-$12,000 per seat per year for Recruiter Corporate. Those Corporate figures are list prices from [Vendr's buyer data](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/linkedin) (Feb 2026). Lite is the only plan you can buy online. Corporate and Professional Services are quote-only, and you won't find a rate card for them anywhere on LinkedIn's site.

At the midpoint of that range, a three-person Corporate team runs about $30,000 per year before discounts. InMail top-ups, job promotion, Talent Insights, and the Hiring Assistant add-on push real spend higher. One detail surprises most buyers: in LinkedIn's own UK public-sector price list, a single Talent Insights seat costs more than a Recruiter seat.

This guide breaks down [LinkedIn Recruiter's three plan tiers](/blog/hireez-vs-linkedin-recruiter/) (Lite, Professional Services, and Corporate), maps out the hidden costs that inflate your bill beyond the subscription price, and compares the platform to cheaper sourcing alternatives. Whether you're evaluating LinkedIn Recruiter for the first time or reviewing LinkedIn Recruiter pricing 2026 ahead of renewal, you'll know exactly what to budget for.

> **TL;DR:**
>
> - **Lite has a public price and a free trial.** As of October 2026, Lite is about $170/month on monthly billing or $1,680/year on annual billing, with a one-month free trial for eligible new users.
> - **Corporate is quote-only.** Buyer data puts Recruiter Corporate at $8,000-$12,000 per seat per year in list price, with negotiated discounts of 15-30% ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/linkedin), 2026).
> - **Per-seat, no sharing.** A 10-person Corporate team at $10,000 a seat runs $100,000/yr, and seat sharing is prohibited even when usage is light.
> - **Add-ons can rival the subscription.** Talent Insights starts at £9,400 per seat per year in LinkedIn's UK price list, above the £8,925 Recruiter seat it sits beside.
> - **Pin is the best LinkedIn Recruiter alternative for outbound sourcing.** [Pin](https://www.pin.com/) starts at $99/mo, searches 850M+ profiles, and 91% of its users reduced or eliminated LinkedIn Recruiter spend after switching.

## How Much Is LinkedIn Recruiter in 2026?

How much is LinkedIn Recruiter? Recruiter Lite costs about $170 per month on monthly billing or $1,680 per year on annual billing (roughly $140 a month). Professional Services is quote-only, and Corporate seats list at $8,000-$12,000 per year. Your total LinkedIn recruiting cost is almost always higher once add-ons are counted, so knowing the true LinkedIn Recruiter cost before renewal is what separates a good deal from a bad one.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per seat) | How to buy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter Lite | ~$170 (monthly billing) | $1,680 (~$140/mo) | Self-serve, 1-month free trial |
| Professional Services (RPS) | Quote only | Quote only | LinkedIn sales team |
| Recruiter Corporate | ~$667-$1,000 | $8,000-$12,000 list | LinkedIn sales team, annual contract |

Several things about LinkedIn's pricing model catch buyers off guard:

-   **The free trial only covers Lite.** Recruiter Lite offers a one-month free trial with a credit card on file. It isn't available if you used a Premium free trial in the last 12 months ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a6807901), 2026). Corporate has no trial, so you commit before testing it.
-   **Per-seat pricing.** Each recruiter on your team needs their own license. Seat sharing isn't allowed, so a 10-person Corporate team pays for 10 seats whether or not everyone logs in daily.
-   **Annual contracts on higher tiers.** Lite offers month-to-month billing, but Professional Services and Corporate require annual commitments with auto-renewal.
-   **Volume pricing is real but hidden.** LinkedIn's UK public-sector list drops the Recruiter seat from £8,925 at one or two seats to £6,350 at 251+ seats ([LinkedIn G-Cloud 14 pricing](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/712281/850052488849651-pricing-document-2025-10-20-1430.pdf), revised Oct 2025). Small teams pay the top of the curve.

Renewal terms deserve a closer look too. Take a hypothetical 10% uplift on a $10,000 Corporate seat: it becomes $11,000 in year two and $12,100 in year three. That's $33,100 over three years, or $3,100 more than flat pricing for one license. Across a ten-seat team, the gap passes $30,000, and without a price cap in your contract you absorb it automatically.

We've noticed a consistent pattern across teams that switch to Pin: nearly every recruiter underestimated their all-in LinkedIn Recruiter cost before renewal. Sticker price rarely triggers the evaluation; the all-in gap does, once InMail top-ups, job promotion, and analytics add-ons are tallied. According to Pin's 2026 user survey across 2,000+ organizations and 20,000+ users, 91% of users reduced or eliminated their LinkedIn Recruiter spend after switching. Most weren't dissatisfied with LinkedIn's database. They were unhappy paying per seat for a single-channel tool when multi-source alternatives cost a fraction of the price. When outbound sourcing is the real bottleneck, Pin is the clearest alternative: 850M+ profiles, multi-channel outreach sequences, and plans starting at $99/mo.

| Free Trial | 1 month | - | - |
| InMail Credits/Month | 30 (top-ups up to 70 more) | 100 (140 on RPS+) | 150 per seat |
| Search Filters | 20+ | 40+ | 40+ |
| Network Access | Up to 3rd degree | Full 1B+ network | Full 1B+ network |
