The best job posting sites for employers in 2026 split into three categories: genuinely no-cost options (Wellfound, Handshake Basic, SimplyHired), volume paid leaders (Indeed Sponsored, LinkedIn Promoted, ZipRecruiter), and niche boards by industry. [Pin](https://www.pin.com/) sits alongside this stack as the proactive sourcing layer reaching the 70% of qualified candidates who never browse job boards, with 850M+ profiles and a free tier.

Among the top job boards, niche options include Dice for tech, Snagajob for hourly, Idealist for nonprofits, and Wellfound for venture-backed startups. Each filters applicant pools far better than general boards. Most employers should run two or three.

Why the picks matter now: 68.6% of US employers do most of their hiring through online job boards, per the [iHire 2025 State of Online Recruiting](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ihires-2025-state-of-online-recruiting-report-highlights-ai-boom-skills-gaps--future-of-job-boards-302542238.html) survey of 529 employers. Yet the landscape changed dramatically in late 2025: Indeed capped free hosted posts at 3 per month, Glassdoor folded into Indeed under parent Recruit Holdings, and Greenhouse data shows 18 to 22% of postings on major platforms are classified as ghost jobs. This guide covers verified pricing, free-tier limits, and the policy changes hiring teams need to know.

## Key Takeaways

- **Pin is the best overall hiring platform for employers.** In a Pin survey of 300+ recruiters (April to August 2026), only 13% said their best hire came from a job board or an inbound application. Pin covers the other 87% with proactive sourcing across the largest multi-source candidate database in the industry, automating outreach over email and LinkedIn from $100/mo with a free tier.
- **Genuinely no-cost posting is now rare.** Only Wellfound (startups), Handshake Basic (campus), and SimplyHired offer sustainable free tiers in 2026. Both Indeed and LinkedIn impose severe caps on their no-cost tiers after late-2025 policy changes.
- **Indeed changed the rules in December 2025.** Free hosted posts are capped at 3 per employer per month, and organic visibility was cut from 120 days to 30. As of March 31, 2026, jobs sent via XML feeds without an ATS that supports Indeed Apply receive zero free organic visibility ([Workology](https://workology.com/the-end-of-free-hiring-what-indeeds-changes-mean-for-your-recruiting-strategy/), 2026).
- **Glassdoor is no longer a separate job board.** Recruit Holdings folded job posting into Indeed in late 2025 (1,300 layoffs); Glassdoor remains as an employer-branding and review platform only ([Allwork.space](https://allwork.space/2025/07/recruit-holdings-to-slash-1300-jobs-fold-glassdoor-into-indeed-as-ai-takes-priority/), July 2025).
- **Match the board to the role.** Wellfound for startups, Dice for tech, Handshake for campus, Snagajob for hourly, We Work Remotely for remote-only, Idealist for nonprofits, Ladders for $100K+ roles. Niche boards filter applicant quality far better than general boards.

Talking to our customers across in-house TA teams and staffing agencies, one pattern keeps repeating in 2026. Employers expanding job board reliance are also reporting more unqualified applications, not fewer. Per iHire data, 59.7% of employers still cite "too many unqualified candidates" as a top-three challenge. Yet 31.9% plan to increase job board spend in 2026.

Teams getting clean pipelines pair one or two well-chosen boards with proactive outbound sourcing. In Pin's 2026 user survey across 2,000+ organizations and 20,000+ users (surveyed January through March 2026), the customers running both inbound (job boards plus career site) and outbound (multi-source candidate sourcing) report a 14-day average time-to-fill. That sits well under the [SHRM 2025 national median](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-recruiting-benchmarking) of 44 days. Job board picks below assume that pairing: what to post on, and what to pair it with so good candidates actually reach you.

## The Top Job Boards for Employers in 2026

The top job boards for employers in 2026 are Indeed for raw volume, LinkedIn for white-collar roles, ZipRecruiter for multi-board distribution, and a niche board matched to the role. That four-way split covers most hiring. Where to post jobs is really a question about the role, not the platform.

Here is the short answer for employers deciding where to post a job:

- **Highest applicant volume:** Indeed Sponsored (432.3M monthly visits) and ZipRecruiter for employers wanting one post syndicated across 100+ partner boards.
- **Best platforms for job posting in professional and management roles:** LinkedIn Promoted Jobs, which the company says reaches 3X more qualified applicants than an unpaid listing.
- **Best sites to post jobs at no cost:** Wellfound, Handshake Basic, and SimplyHired, the three genuinely free job posting sites for employers left in 2026.
- **Best place to post jobs by specialty:** Dice for tech, Snagajob for hourly, We Work Remotely for remote-only, Idealist for nonprofits, Ladders for $100K+ roles.
- **Reaching candidates no board covers:** proactive sourcing across the 70% of qualified people who never browse hiring websites for employers at all.

In a Pin in-product survey of 300+ recruiters running from April to August 2026, 13% said their single best hire that quarter came from a job board or an inbound application. The other 87% came from a channel where somebody went out and found the person. That is not an argument against boards. One in eight best hires from a channel with a free tier is a real return, which is exactly why the paid and no-cost options below still earn budget. It is an argument for what sits beside them.

Ranking the best hiring platforms for employers is less useful than matching one to the role in front of you. Two caveats shape every pick below. Free tiers shrank sharply in late 2025, and roughly a fifth of listings on major platforms now read as ghost jobs. Both change the math on which of the top job posting sites actually earn a slot in your budget.

## Best Free Job Posting Sites for Employers in 2026

No-cost posting in 2026 is mostly freemium with caveats. December 2025's policy change capped Indeed's hosted listings at 3 per month and cut organic visibility from 120 days to 30, around the same time Glassdoor's job-posting flow was folded into Indeed. The practical map of "where can I post a job for $0" is shorter than it was a year ago. Six options still deliver meaningful reach without a credit card.

### 1. Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) - Best Free Site for Startup Hiring

![Screenshot of the Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) employer homepage](/images/urlbox/wellfound-com-26fc2f84.webp)

Wellfound's Access plan is genuinely no-cost with unlimited job listings and no time-based expiry, per the [Wellfound pricing page](https://wellfound.com/recruit/pricing). Roughly 10 million startup-ready candidates use the platform, and equity transparency surfaces on every listing - a strong match for pre-seed through Series B companies.

Sourcing add-ons (Promoted Jobs from $200, Recruit Pro at $499/month) are paid. But a small startup posting 3 to 5 roles can run the full no-cost tier indefinitely without restrictions. Outside of tech and growth-stage companies, candidate distribution thins quickly.

### 2. Handshake Basic - Best Free Site for Campus and Entry-Level Hiring

![Screenshot of the Handshake employer homepage](/images/urlbox/joinhandshake-com-100116b2.webp)

Handshake Basic gives employers no-cost, unlimited job listings that distribute across 1,400+ college and university career centers, per the [Handshake employer plans page](https://joinhandshake.com/employers/products/premium/compare/). 2025 Handshake data showed postings declined 15% year-over-year while applications per job rose 30%, and Class of 2025 hiring is projected up 7.3% over Class of 2024.

Campus is competitive, but the no-cost tier is real. The cap is 10 messages per month, which limits proactive outreach but does not affect job visibility. Pro at $450/mo unlocks Promoted Jobs and unlimited messaging.

### 3. SimplyHired - Best Free Site That Syndicates Across 100+ Boards

![Screenshot of the SimplyHired employer homepage](/images/urlbox/web-archive-org-40f16143.webp)

SimplyHired listings publish at no cost and syndicate to 100+ partner job boards automatically, per the [SimplyHired employer page](https://www.simplyhired.com/post-jobs/). Pay-as-you-go kicks in only when you unlock candidate contact information at $9.99 or higher per profile (price varies by experience and demand).

For employers with their own application flow (career site, ATS-managed funnel), SimplyHired functions as a no-cost distribution layer. Teams needing direct outreach via the platform should expect per-contact costs to add up fast above 10 to 15 candidates.

### 4. Indeed - Most Recognized Free Tier (With Major 2026 Caveats)

![Screenshot of the Indeed for Employers homepage](/images/urlbox/indeed-com-bb78a7fc.webp)

Indeed remains the largest job site with 432.3 million monthly visits in March 2026 ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/indeed.com/)), but its no-cost tier was sharply restricted in late 2025. Since December 2025, employers can run only 3 hosted listings per month, with organic visibility cut from 120 days to 30 ([Job Board Doctor](https://www.jobboarddoctor.com/2025/12/12/changes-to-indeed-hosted-jobs/)).

As of March 31, 2026, jobs submitted via XML feeds without an ATS that supports Indeed Apply receive zero free organic visibility ([Workology](https://workology.com/the-end-of-free-hiring-what-indeeds-changes-mean-for-your-recruiting-strategy/)). For meaningful reach in any competitive market, sponsoring is now effectively required. See our [full Indeed pricing breakdown](/blog/indeed-pricing/) for the paid-side numbers.

### 5. LinkedIn - Free Tier for Single Professional Listings

![Screenshot of the LinkedIn Talent Solutions homepage](/images/urlbox/business-linkedin-com-0fa9ed25.webp)

LinkedIn allows one no-cost job post per company page, paused at 14 days or roughly 26 applicants, per the [LinkedIn Help Center](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a519737/differences-between-free-and-promoted-jobs?lang=en). LinkedIn states promoted listings reach 3X more qualified applicants than unpaid posts. The platform now serves more than 1 billion members worldwide, per the [LinkedIn About page](https://about.linkedin.com/), and dominates for white-collar roles.

The no-cost tier is best understood as a single-listing trial rather than a recurring hiring channel. See our [LinkedIn job posting cost breakdown](/blog/linkedin-job-posting-pricing/) for the paid-side math.

### 6. USAJOBS - Best Free Site for Federal Positions

![Screenshot of the USAJOBS federal hiring portal homepage](/images/urlbox/usajobs-gov-4c72ca34.webp)

USAJOBS, operated by the US Office of Personnel Management, is the only place to post federal civilian openings, and it is fully no-cost. Restriction: federal agencies only.

Private employers cannot post here. Contractors pursuing federal work still track openings on USAJOBS to align talent strategies. For any company doing federal contracting, knowing the platform exists matters; for everyone else, it is reference context.

## Best Paid Job Posting Sites for Employers in 2026

Paid posting is worth it when volume, role specialization, or employer brand matter. Per iHire data, 50.3% of employers cite cost as a top-three challenge. The goal is not maximum spend but smart channel selection. Each of the six paid platforms below dominates one use case: volume hiring, professional roles, multi-board distribution, tech, remote-only, and vetted listings.

### 1. Indeed Sponsored - Best Paid Site for Volume Hiring

Indeed for employers runs on a CPC model from $0.10 to $5.00 per click with a $5/day minimum and a $25 floor per job, per the [Indeed pricing page](https://www.indeed.com/hire/o/pricing). For high-volume hiring (warehouse, hourly, retail, customer service), Indeed's 432.3M monthly visits and applicant-volume scale are unmatched.

Budget control is granular (daily caps, pause anytime), and the auto-budget option uses the platform's algorithm to allocate spend across roles. Cost-per-applicant typically lands between $5 and $50 depending on role competitiveness and market.

### 2. LinkedIn Promoted Jobs - Best Paid Site for White-Collar Roles

LinkedIn Promoted Jobs starts at roughly $7 to $10/day with CPC of $1.50 to $4.50, plus annual Job Slots from $200 to $1,000/slot/month. LinkedIn states promoted openings reach 3X more qualified applicants than unpaid listings.

With more than 1 billion members per the [LinkedIn About page](https://about.linkedin.com/), the platform remains the default for mid-to-senior professional and manager roles. Direct recruiter outreach options give it no close peer in the white-collar space.

### 3. ZipRecruiter - Best Paid Site for Multi-Board Distribution

![Screenshot of the ZipRecruiter for Employers homepage](/images/urlbox/web-archive-org-088bc31e.webp)

[ZipRecruiter pricing](/blog/ziprecruiter-pricing/) runs roughly $299 to $899/month per slot (Standard to Pro tiers) with a $24/day daily option, all confirmed through G2 and Capterra reviews; the company directs all pricing to a sales call. ZipRecruiter for employers is built around one thing: automatic distribution to 100+ partner job boards from a single post, plus AI "Invite to Apply" matching that proactively pings candidates.

Small teams managing their first job board strategy trade flexibility for distribution simplicity. For brands with the operational capacity to run channel-level testing themselves, the bundling argument weakens.

### 4. Dice - Best Paid Site for Tech Hiring

![Screenshot of the Dice for Employers homepage](/images/urlbox/dice-com-ab50bf2d.webp)

Dice prices a single 30-day listing at $399, the Starter plan at $499/month (3 slots, 50 candidate views), and Standard at $799/month (unlimited openings, 100 views), per the [Dice pricing page](https://www.dice.com/hiring/pricing). 7 million tech jobs flow through the platform annually, distributed across 3,000+ partner sites.

For tech-specific roles, Dice's filter granularity (by tech stack, certifications, clearance level) leads any general board. Outside tech, coverage thins fast.

### 5. We Work Remotely - Best Paid Site for Remote-Only Roles

![Screenshot of the We Work Remotely homepage](/images/urlbox/weworkremotely-com-a3a920d1.webp)

We Work Remotely charges $299/month per listing for a 30-day post with optional Good/Better/Best upgrade tiers. A $99 GeoLock add-on supports region-restricted hires, per the [We Work Remotely FAQ](https://weworkremotely.com/frequently-asked-questions). 4.5 million monthly visitors focused exclusively on remote work make this the largest dedicated remote-only channel.

Tech, design, marketing, and customer-support roles see the strongest applicant volume; deeply specialized roles (finance, legal, ops leadership) thin out faster.

### 6. FlexJobs - Best Paid Site With Vetted Listings

![Screenshot of the FlexJobs employer homepage](/images/urlbox/flexjobs-com-ac897660.webp)

FlexJobs costs $399/month or $2,999/year for unlimited employer postings and resume database access, per the [FlexJobs pricing page](https://www.flexjobs.com/pricing.aspx). Every employer posting is hand-vetted before it goes live - the strongest anti-ghost-job posture of any board on this list.

Employers prioritizing brand trust with remote candidates can use the vetting badge as a quality signal that competing no-cost boards cannot match. One-off listings are out. The monthly floor sees to that.

## Niche and Specialty Job Boards Worth Knowing

Beyond the twelve boards above, several niche options earn placement when the role profile fits their audience exactly. Monster runs Standard plans at $18/day and Pro at $299/month, using a credits system where unused budget rolls across postings and resume views. The legacy brand still pulls applicant volume in trades, healthcare, and clerical roles. CareerBuilder listings start at $349/month, with pay-for-performance and pay-per-resume models for teams that want to align spend with outcomes. Idealist charges $145 per nonprofit job posting (30 days) and $25 per internship listing, per the [BetterTeam Idealist review](https://www.betterteam.com/idealist) (2026); 1.3 million mission-driven candidates visit monthly.

For senior roles, Ladders restricts the platform to $100K+ openings, gives one no-cost post per month, and prices a single paid listing at $599 (or $539 to $569 in packs). Snagajob runs $89/month Starter and roughly $249/month Growing plans for hourly hiring (restaurants, retail, hospitality), reaching the largest US hourly-worker network. BuiltIn offers a no-cost basic post and complimentary ATS sync for tech employers, with paid Premium Partnerships unlocking promoted jobs and employer-branding placement across location-based tech communities (Chicago, NYC, Austin, Seattle). Craigslist still costs $10 to $75 per listing depending on metro and remains effective for hyper-local trades and hourly roles where applicants prefer a no-friction local search.

Sticker price is a poor guide here. The published monthly range runs from $89 at the low end to $899 at the top. Boards with the widest spread are the ones where the entry tier rarely matches what teams actually pay.

## The Ghost Jobs Problem and How to Avoid Posting One

The Greenhouse 2025 Workforce and Hiring Report (n=4,100+ across the US, UK, Ireland, and Germany) found 18 to 22% of jobs posted on the Greenhouse platform in any given quarter are classified as ghost jobs. 72% of candidates report the role they applied for differed from what was actually offered. CNBC coverage in November 2025 framed ghost postings as "adding another layer of uncertainty to the stalled jobs picture," and the BLS JOLTS gap (7.1M openings, ~4.8M monthly hires) hints at the scale of the problem.

Employers can stay on the right side of this in three ways. First, close listings within seven days of filling the role and do not let evergreen reqs sit live for "pipeline." Second, name a real hiring manager and target start window in the JD. Third, audit any role open more than 60 days for actual recruiting activity.

Vetting helps. Boards that check listings (FlexJobs) or verify companies (Wellfound) reduce candidate skepticism by association, which matters when your employer brand competes for trust against larger names.

### Why is Glassdoor no longer listed as a separate job board?

In July 2025, parent company Recruit Holdings announced it was folding Glassdoor's job-posting operations into Indeed and laying off 1,300 staff, per Allwork.space. Beginning November 18, 2025, new Glassdoor accounts require Indeed login credentials. Glassdoor today functions as an employer-branding and review platform; job posts route through Indeed.

### What is a ghost job and why do they hurt employers?

A ghost job is a listing kept live without active hiring intent. The role may already be filled, the budget may have been pulled, or the opening may exist purely to build "pipeline." Greenhouse 2025 data shows 18 to 22% of openings on its platform are classified this way, and 72% of candidates have applied to roles that differed from what was offered. Ghost listings erode candidate trust and inflate apparent hiring activity in BLS reports.

### Where is the best place to post a job online in 2026?

For most roles the best place to post a job online is one high-volume general board paired with one niche board. Indeed Sponsored and ZipRecruiter deliver the broadest applicant flow, LinkedIn Promoted works best for professional and manager roles, and Wellfound, Handshake Basic, and SimplyHired remain the genuinely no-cost options. Match the second board to the role profile: Dice for tech, Snagajob for hourly, We Work Remotely for remote, Idealist for nonprofits.

### Should employers post on multiple job boards or focus on one?

Most employers in 2026 should run one volume board (Indeed Sponsored or ZipRecruiter), one niche board matched to the role (Dice for tech, Snagajob for hourly, [Handshake for campus](/blog/handshake-pricing/), Wellfound for startups), and one proactive sourcing platform that reaches passive candidates. Single-channel strategies under-perform because 70% of qualified candidates do not actively browse job boards, per LinkedIn Talent Solutions data.
