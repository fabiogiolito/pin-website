### 2. LinkedIn Recruiter System Connect (RSC)

![Screenshot of the LinkedIn Recruiter website homepage](/images/urlbox/business-linkedin-com-e1f94db4.webp)

LinkedIn RSC syncs InMail messages, notes, and candidate profile data between LinkedIn Recruiter and Bullhorn bi-directionally. When a recruiter sends an InMail through LinkedIn, that message and the candidate's profile appear in the Bullhorn record automatically. RSC eliminates the double-entry problem that plagues agencies using both platforms.

One catch: RSC requires an active LinkedIn Recruiter license ($10,800-$15,000+/yr per seat, based on buyer-reported quotes). In fact, 91% of Pin users reduced or eliminated their LinkedIn Recruiter spend after switching.

## What Are the Best Bullhorn Automation Integrations?

Two primary tools make up Bullhorn's automation and engagement ecosystem: Bullhorn Automation (formerly Herefish) for internal workflow triggers from ~$750/month, and Sense for contractor redeployment on custom pricing. Both address different layers of the recruiter workflow - and they complement rather than compete with each other.

### 3. Bullhorn Automation (Formerly Herefish)

![Screenshot of the Bullhorn Automation website homepage](/images/urlbox/bullhorn-com-86e71cbe.webp)

As the platform's native workflow engine, Bullhorn Automation handles all rule-based tasks. Status changes in Bullhorn trigger actions automatically - sending follow-up emails when a submission goes cold, alerting recruiters when a placement is ending, or updating records when a candidate completes onboarding paperwork. Manual busywork stops accumulating with this integration in place. Nothing slips. Pricing reportedly starts at approximately $750/month ([TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/bullhorn-herefish/pricing), 2023), though Bullhorn doesn't publish pricing publicly. Implementation costs range from basic setup to $50,000+ for complex multi-branch configurations. Automation pays for itself at agencies running 50+ active jobs simultaneously by catching the follow-ups that recruiters forget during busy weeks.

Fifty-five percent of firms using AI-powered screening reported improved KPIs by 25% or more ([Bullhorn GRID 2026](https://www.bullhorn.com/news-and-press/press-releases/bullhorn-grid-report-staffing-firms-using-ai-see-stronger-growth-faster-placements/)). Automation handles the repetitive layer - status updates, drip sequences, data hygiene - so recruiters focus on the relationship-driven work that AI can't replicate.

### 4. Sense

![Screenshot of the Sense website homepage](/images/urlbox/sensehq-com-ebd0a683.webp)

Sense specializes in candidate redeployment and reengagement - the revenue staffing agencies leave on the table when they lose touch with previously placed contractors. The company now pitches itself as a broader AI recruiting platform, but redeployment remains its Bullhorn sweet spot. Sense connects to Bullhorn and monitors placement end dates, then triggers automated outreach sequences to bring candidates back into the pipeline before their assignments expire. Redeployment rates directly impact revenue, and Sense addresses a specific and expensive gap for contract staffing firms. A contractor who finishes an assignment and gets placed again through your agency within two weeks is pure margin. Silence costs money. A contractor who goes dark and finds their next gig elsewhere is lost revenue. Custom pricing comes through Sense's sales team.

## Which VoIP Integrations Work Best with Bullhorn?

Choosing a Bullhorn VoIP integration typically comes down to two platforms: CloudCall, built specifically for staffing workflows at $15-$35/user/month, and RingCentral, a broader communications platform with a native Bullhorn connector. Both log calls automatically to candidate records - the question is whether you want a staffing-specific tool or an all-in-one communications suite.

### 5. CloudCall

![Screenshot of the CloudCall website homepage](/images/urlbox/cloudcall-com-bdb9002b.webp)

CloudCall embeds click-to-dial calling, SMS, and call recording directly inside Bullhorn records.
