## What Should You Look for in Talent Acquisition Software?

Cost per hire averages $5,475 for nonexecutive roles and $35,879 for executives ([SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/about/press-room/shrm-releases-2025-benchmarking-reports--how-does-your-organizat), 2025), and median time-to-fill for nonexecutive roles sits at 39 days ([SHRM 2026 Recruiting Executives Benchmarking](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-recruiting-benchmarking)). Employ's 2026 report, drawn from 6,640 customers across Jobvite, Lever, and JazzHR, shows time-to-fill falling from 67.7 days in 2024 to 63.5 days in 2025 ([Employ Inc., 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/07/3214475/0/en/What-Will-Good-Hiring-Look-Like-in-2026-New-Employ-Inc-Report-Reveals-Key-Recruiting-Benchmarks.html)). Improvement is happening. Still, the baseline is far higher than it needs to be, which is why the right TA platform should pay for itself quickly.

Yet "talent acquisition software" now describes everything from a $15/mo applicant tracker to a $300,000/yr HCM suite. Those aren't the same product category, even though vendors lump them together. Here's how to cut through the noise and evaluate what actually matters:

-   **AI sourcing depth:** How many profiles can the system search? Does it understand context - like "series-B fintech CFO with APAC experience" - or just match keywords? Platforms with hundreds of millions of profiles and semantic matching find candidates that keyword filters miss entirely. Our AI candidate sourcing guide explains the difference in detail.
-   **Outreach automation:** Can the platform run personalized multi-channel sequences that pair automated email with LinkedIn and SMS touches? Single-channel tools leave response rates on the table. In Pin's own [recruiting outreach benchmarks](/blog/recruiting-outreach-benchmark-report/), sequences that pair email with a LinkedIn touch out-reply email-only sequences.
-   **ATS and pipeline management:** Can you track every candidate from first contact to offer acceptance in one system? Some platforms source well but lack pipeline tracking. Others manage pipelines but can't find candidates. You shouldn't need two tools to cover the basics.
-   **Scheduling:** Automated calendar coordination, time zone handling, and interview confirmations eliminate the scheduling back-and-forth that drags out time-to-fill. How much admin work does the platform actually remove? Candidate experience scores average just 2.9 out of 5 across industries - with software and technology ranking lowest - making a smooth, responsive scheduling flow more than a nice-to-have ([Employ Inc., 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/07/3214475/0/en/What-Will-Good-Hiring-Look-Like-in-2026-New-Employ-Inc-Report-Reveals-Key-Recruiting-Benchmarks.html)).
-   **Compliance and security:** SOC 2 certification, encryption, GDPR readiness, and documented bias safeguards aren't optional in 2026. Ask what's verified by third-party audit, not just claimed in marketing copy.
-   **Pricing transparency:** Published pricing signals confidence. If you need a 45-minute sales call just to see a number, plan for enterprise-level bills. Our [best recruiting software comparison](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-recruiting-software/) shows what pricing looks like across the broader recruiting software market.

### 1\. Pin

**Good for:** Recruiting teams of any size that need AI sourcing, automated outreach, and interview scheduling in one platform.

![Screenshot of the Pin website homepage](/images/urlbox/pin-com-934154a1-1.webp)

Pin is an AI-powered recruiting assistant that searches 850M+ candidate profiles - one of the largest databases in the industry, with 100% coverage in North America and Europe. Automated sourcing, multi-channel outreach sequences (automated email plus AI-drafted LinkedIn and SMS tasks the recruiter sends), and interview scheduling with calendar syncing run around the clock. Pin's automated outreach delivers 5x better response rates than industry averages, and 83% of candidates it recommends are accepted into hiring pipelines - the highest candidate acceptance rate of any AI recruiting platform. Recruiters using Pin fill positions in an average of 14 days, reducing time-to-hire by 82% compared to traditional methods.

What separates Pin from traditional TA software is that it handles both needle-in-a-haystack specialist roles and high-volume hiring equally well. Most platforms force you to pick one or the other. Pin's Chrome extension lets recruiters source on the go, and its shared team inbox keeps everyone in sync. Agency teams get multi-client support from a single account.

Nick Poloni, President at Cascadia Search Group, shared his experience: "I jumped into Pin solo toward the end of 2025 and closed out the year with over $1M in billings during just the final 4 months - no team, no agency. The sourcing data is incredible, scanning 850M+ profiles with recruiter-level precision to uncover perfect-fit candidates I'd never find otherwise."

Pin's analytics dashboard tracks hiring funnel efficiency, diversity metrics, and quality-of-hire signals - giving TA leaders the visibility that enterprise platforms charge five or six figures for. The platform is SOC 2 Type 2 certified, with encryption at rest and in transit, strict access controls, and third-party fairness audits. Trust certification details are publicly documented at trust.pin.com.

**Pricing:** Free plan (no credit card required). Solo $99/mo (billed annually). Professional $135/user/mo billed annually or $179/user/mo billed monthly. Business: custom pricing with unlimited seats.

**Pricing:** Essential $14,995/yr. Vendr median $33,507/yr. Professional and higher tiers: custom quote. Annual contracts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is talent acquisition software?

Talent acquisition software is the set of tools a hiring team uses to find, engage, evaluate, and hire candidates, covering sourcing, outreach, applicant tracking, scheduling, and analytics. An ATS covers only the tracking layer after candidates apply. Full TA platforms add proactive sourcing and engagement, and Pin is the best option for teams that need that front end, with 850M+ profiles and a free plan to start.

### What is the best talent acquisition software for small teams?

Pin is the strongest option for small teams because it offers a free tier with no credit card required, published pricing from $99/mo, and AI sourcing across 850M+ profiles with multi-channel outreach. Manatal is a budget alternative at $15/user/mo but has limited AI sourcing depth and no automated outreach beyond email.

### How much does talent acquisition software cost?

Prices range from free (Pin's free plan) to six figures a year for enterprise suites, where Vendr purchase medians run about $98,000/yr for Phenom and $51,000/yr across Workday products. Mid-market tools like Workable ($299/mo) and Manatal ($15/user/mo) sit in between. iCIMS (Vendr median ~$9,800/yr) and SmartRecruiters ($14,995+/yr) require annual contracts and custom quotes for larger deployments.

### What features should I look for in talent acquisition software?

Focus on AI sourcing depth (database size plus semantic matching), multi-channel outreach automation (email, LinkedIn, SMS), ATS pipeline management, automated interview scheduling, and verified compliance certifications (SOC 2, GDPR). Among HR professionals using AI, 87% report efficiency improvements ([SHRM, 2026](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/ai-hr-2026-from-hype-to-measured-human-centered-impact)).

### Can talent acquisition software replace recruiters?

No. TA software automates repetitive tasks - sourcing, outreach sequencing, scheduling - but recruiters still drive relationship building, candidate evaluation, and offer negotiation. According to LinkedIn's Future of Recruiting report (2025), 73% of TA pros agree AI will change the way organizations hire, and those already using generative AI save about 20% of their work week. Pairing AI automation with human judgment is what works best.

### What is the difference between an ATS and talent acquisition software?

An ATS (applicant tracking system) manages candidates after they apply - tracking applications through stages, scheduling interviews, and storing records. Talent acquisition software is a broader category that includes proactive sourcing (finding candidates before they apply), outreach automation, CRM, scheduling, and analytics. Some platforms like Pin cover the full workflow. Others like iCIMS focus primarily on the tracking and compliance layer. Worth noting: 34% of companies are increasing ATS investment, yet 82% report significant functionality gaps in their current systems ([Aptitude Research](https://www.aptituderesearch.com/sap-acquires-smartrecruiters-a-strategic-shift-to-agentic-ai/), 2025). Such gaps explain why so many teams add a dedicated sourcing or outreach tool on top of their ATS.

### What is the most popular ATS software?

Workday is the most popular ATS among large employers, with over 40% of Fortune 500 installs, and Workday plus SAP SuccessFactors together account for 52.4% ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/fortune-500-use-applicant-tracking-systems/), 2026). Below the enterprise tier the answer changes: among 600+ recruiting teams with an ATS connected to Pin, no single system tops one in six connections, according to our [ATS market share data](/blog/ats-market-share-report/). Popularity at the Fortune 500 level doesn't translate to the best fit for every team - enterprise ATS platforms are built for scale, compliance, and integration depth, not for AI-driven sourcing or outreach automation. Teams that need both often pair an enterprise ATS with a dedicated sourcing platform like Pin.

### Do recruiters still use ATS?

Yes - 97.4% of Fortune 500 companies run a detectable ATS, and adoption is widespread among mid-market employers as well ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/fortune-500-use-applicant-tracking-systems/), 2026). What has changed is what recruiters expect the ATS to do. Traditional ATS systems track candidates after they apply but don't source, engage, or schedule proactively. Tracking without sourcing leaves a hole: 82% of companies report significant functionality gaps in their current systems ([Aptitude Research](https://www.aptituderesearch.com/sap-acquires-smartrecruiters-a-strategic-shift-to-agentic-ai/), 2025). As a result, many teams now layer AI sourcing and outreach tools on top of their existing ATS rather than replacing it. ATS handles compliance and pipeline tracking; platforms like Pin handle candidate discovery and engagement.
