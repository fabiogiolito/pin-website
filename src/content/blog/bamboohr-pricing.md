BambooHR pricing starts at roughly $10 per employee per month on the Core plan, $17/employee/month on Pro, and $25/employee/month on Elite, based on third-party buyer reports through 2026. Companies with 25 or fewer employees hit a flat $250/month minimum regardless of plan tier - a detail that catches many small business buyers off guard.

No public rate card exists on their website. Getting a quote requires contacting sales, which means two companies of the same size can pay different rates depending on when they signed and what add-ons they selected. According to [OutSail](https://www.outsail.co/post/how-much-does-bamboohr-cost) (2025), the published per-employee-per-month (PEPM) range - sometimes referred to as BambooHR cost per user - for most businesses falls between $12 and $22, decreasing with volume. That tracks with the company's three-tier structure, which BambooHR overhauled in 2024 when it added the Elite plan.

This guide covers all three BambooHR pricing plans 2026: Core, Pro, and Elite. It maps out add-on costs that won't appear on the pricing page, explains recruiting limitations most reviews gloss over, and compares bamboo HR cost to five HRIS and ATS alternatives. Whether researching bamboo HR pricing for the first time or reconsidering after the 2024 restructuring, you'll know exactly what to budget for - including hidden costs that many buyer's guides skip.

> **TL;DR:**
>
> - **Three tiers, per-employee pricing.** BambooHR runs roughly $10/$17/$25 PEPM on Core, Pro, and Elite ([OutSail 2025](https://www.outsail.co/post/how-much-does-bamboohr-cost)), with no public rate card and every deal quoted by sales.
> - **The $250/month floor hits small teams.** Anyone under 25 employees pays at least $250/mo regardless of tier, making the effective per-employee cost roughly $25 at a 10-person startup.
> - **Implementation and add-ons stack up.** Implementation fees add 5-15% of annual contract value, and features like payroll or advanced analytics often sit outside the base plan.
> - **The built-in ATS is a recruiting afterthought.** Active job caps of 5-50 per plan and zero candidate sourcing or outreach automation mean teams doing real hiring outgrow it fast.
> - **For active recruiting, Pin is the best AI sourcing platform.** Gusto, Rippling, and dedicated ATS platforms often beat BambooHR on cost or feature depth depending on team size. For teams where recruiting is the bottleneck, pair a lightweight HRIS with Pin rather than relying on BambooHR's capped ATS.

These are Pro-tier estimates, which shift based on plan choice, negotiation, and which add-ons you bundle. A 100-person company on Core ($10 PEPM) pays about $12,000/yr; on Elite ($25 PEPM), the same headcount costs $30,000/yr - a $18,000 annual gap driven entirely by tier selection.

Several details matter about BambooHR's pricing model:

-   **$250/month floor.** Teams with 25 or fewer employees pay a flat $250/mo regardless of plan tier. That works out to $10/employee/month at exactly 25 people, but $50/employee/month at 5 people. Micro-teams pay a premium.
-   **No public pricing.** Every quote requires a sales call. [SelectSoftware Reviews](https://www.selectsoftwarereviews.com/reviews/bamboohr) notes that users report needing multiple discovery calls before getting a real number.
-   **Per-employee scaling.** Your bill grows as your headcount grows, even if your HR team stays the same size. At 250 employees on Pro, you're looking at $51,000/yr before any add-ons.
-   **Volume discounts exist.** OutSail reports that PEPM rates decrease with higher headcount, dropping to the $12-$14 range for larger organizations.

Platforms like [ADP](https://www.pin.com/blog/adp-recruiting-pricing/) and [Paycor](https://www.pin.com/blog/paycor-pricing/) use similar per-employee models but with different floor thresholds. Teams evaluating bamboo HR cost per employee per month often expect to pay $100/month at 10 employees ($10 PEPM x 10) - but the $250/month minimum floor means they actually pay $250/month from day one. At 10 employees, that's effectively $25/employee, the same rate as Elite. For teams under 25 people, effective bamboo HR cost per employee runs higher than the published PEPM range suggests. Comparing against Gusto ($40/mo + $6 PEPM) makes sense at lower headcounts, where Gusto is significantly cheaper.

**What we're seeing:** Teams that come to Pin after outgrowing BambooHR's ATS almost always share the same story. They started on Core with 5 active job openings, hit that ceiling, upgraded to Pro or Elite - and still couldn't source candidates proactively. According to Pin's 2026 user survey, recruiters at these companies reduce manual sourcing time by 90% after switching to a dedicated AI platform, which also cuts time-to-fill to an average of 14 days. The issue wasn't the HRIS tier. It was using an HR records system as a recruiting engine, a role it was never designed to play. Base pricing looks reasonable for HR operations. Once you add payroll, benefits, and time tracking - then still need a separate sourcing tool - total spend for a mid-size company often reaches $25,000-$40,000 per year. That's the comparison most teams skip before signing. Running a lightweight HRIS alongside a purpose-built sourcing platform consistently delivers stronger recruiting outcomes at a comparable or lower combined cost than a single all-in-one platform that wasn't built for active hiring.

## What Do BambooHR's Core, Pro, and Elite Plans Include?

In 2024, BambooHR added Elite to its existing Core and Pro structure, overhauling its plan lineup per [OutSail](https://www.outsail.co/post/bamboohr-unveils-new-features-and-revamps-pricing-structure) (2024). That restructuring bundled previously separate add-ons into higher tiers, but it also moved some existing customers to lower tiers than they'd been paying for. Here's what each plan includes today:

Between Core and Pro lies the biggest feature gap. Core covers basic HR operations - employee records, time-off tracking, and workflows. Pro adds performance management, employee surveys, and engagement tools; performance management is bundled into the Pro tier at roughly $17 PEPM, covering BambooHR performance management pricing with no separate module fee. Elite adds compensation management, custom dashboards, HR benchmarks, and premium support.

Moving from two tiers to three in 2024, BambooHR added Elite as a premium option while bundling previously separate add-ons into higher tiers ([OutSail](https://www.outsail.co/post/bamboohr-unveils-new-features-and-revamps-pricing-structure), 2024). That restructuring left some customers worse off: long-term users were moved to lower tiers with reduced feature access, while capabilities they'd previously paid for as add-ons became Elite-exclusive. Returning customers, particularly those who signed contracts before 2024, should verify which plan their features map to before renewing.

### Which Plan Should You Pick?

Small teams (under 50 employees) that primarily need digital employee records, time-off tracking, and basic onboarding will find Core covers the essentials at the lowest price point. Performance reviews and engagement surveys are missing, but when those aren't priorities yet, that's fine.

Companies in the 50-150 employee range that want performance management, employee surveys, and a more functional ATS should evaluate Pro. Jumping from 5 active job openings (Core) to 25 (Pro) alone justifies the upgrade for any team doing consistent hiring.

Larger organizations (150+ employees) that need compensation management, custom reporting, and compliance training at scale are Elite's target. The 300+ compliance courses and HR benchmark data suit companies with dedicated HR departments managing complex workforce operations. Without those needs, you're paying a premium for features that won't drive daily value.

### What BambooHR AI Features Are Included by Tier?

Across all three plans, AI features arrived in 2024 - though what you get varies significantly by tier. Core plan users get a basic AI assistant that handles employee questions and generates simple text. Pro upgrades that assistant with expanded capabilities for drafting job descriptions and summarizing employee feedback. Elite unlocks the most advanced tier, including AI-driven analytics, predictive workforce insights, and automated compliance recommendations.

One catch: BambooHR's AI applies entirely to HR operations, not recruiting. No AI candidate sourcing, no AI-powered candidate matching, no AI-driven outreach exists in the platform. Teams evaluating BambooHR for its AI capabilities specifically should understand that the AI manages existing employees - it doesn't find new ones. When AI-powered recruiting is the need, a dedicated tool like [Pin](https://www.pin.com/) fills that gap - scanning 850M+ profiles and automating candidate outreach with 5x better response rates than industry averages.

## What Hidden Fees Does BambooHR Charge?

BambooHR's base PEPM rate understates actual cost by 30-50% once implementation, payroll, benefits, and time tracking fees are added. Each is a separate, unpublished line item that only surfaces after the initial quote. The HR software market hit $17.74 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach $31.89 billion by 2031 at a 10.29% CAGR, per [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/hr-management-software-personnel-and-organization-market) (2025). Vendor pricing complexity scales with that growth. Here's what to budget for beyond the subscription.

### 1\. Implementation and Onboarding

Expect to pay 5-15% of your annual software cost as a BambooHR onboarding fee, according to [OutSail](https://www.outsail.co/post/how-much-does-bamboohr-cost) (2025). On a $50,000 annual contract (roughly 250 employees on Pro), that's $2,500-$7,500 upfront before you've processed a single employee record. Smaller teams spending $10,000/yr still face $500-$1,500 in setup fees. Factor this into your Year 1 budget.

### 2\. BambooHR Payroll Add-On (US Only)

Payroll pricing for BambooHR isn't published anywhere on their website - every quote requires contacting sales. What would you budget for payroll in your stack? You won't know until you call. Payroll processing is a separate add-on with completely unpublished rates, and there's a bigger catch: the module only covers US-based employees. International team members are paid through partner payroll integrations or EOR, not BambooHR Payroll itself. Buyers shopping for a combined HR and payroll solution should factor this into their total budget from the start.

### 3\. Benefits Administration (US Only)

Like payroll, benefits administration is a paid add-on restricted to US employees. One discount exists: 15% off when you purchase both payroll and benefits administration together ([SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/hr/bamboohr/pricing), 2025). That discount still applies to unpublished pricing, so actual savings won't be clear until you negotiate.

### 4\. Time Tracking

Time tracking is a paid add-on here, not a base feature - which is how BambooHR diverges from most competitors. Rippling and Gusto include time tracking in their core plans. With BambooHR, it's an extra line item with undisclosed pricing. For hourly-heavy workforces where time tracking is essential, that turns a "nice to have" into a budget surprise.

### 5\. Employer of Record (via Remote)

Through a partnership with Remote, BambooHR offers EOR services for hiring international employees. Pricing comes from Remote, not BambooHR - it's a third-party cost positioned as a platform add-on. International hiring needs should account for separate EOR costs on top of the BambooHR subscription. For a dedicated comparison, see our guide to the [top EOR service providers](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-eor-providers/).

Given the add-on structure, the sticker price often understates the real BambooHR cost by 30-50%. A mid-size company that needs HR records, payroll, benefits, time tracking, and implementation could easily pay 40-60% more than the base PEPM rate suggests. Always request a fully itemized quote covering every module you'll need - not just the base subscription.

## What Are BambooHR's Recruiting Limitations?

Every plan includes a built-in ATS. The ATS is bundled into the base subscription - no separate BambooHR ATS pricing applies. But "built-in ATS" oversells what the recruiting module actually does, especially for teams with active hiring needs.
