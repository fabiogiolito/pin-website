## What Is a Candidate Sourcing Company?

Candidate sourcing companies find qualified candidates for your open roles before those candidates ever apply. Unlike job boards that wait for inbound applications, sourcing companies actively search for, identify, and engage talent - especially the settled professionals who aren't actively looking. Pin's data puts the median current-role tenure of US professionals at 4.2 years, close to the 4.1-year median the BLS reported in January 2026.

Talent sourcing companies fall into three categories in 2026:

-   **AI sourcing platforms** - Software that automates candidate discovery, matching, and outreach. You run the platform yourself. Lowest cost, fastest deployment.
-   **RPO providers** - Recruitment Process Outsourcing firms that embed dedicated sourcers into your team. They handle the entire sourcing workflow on your behalf. Higher cost, white-glove service.
-   **Staffing firms with sourcing capabilities** - Traditional agencies that maintain candidate databases and source for specific engagements. Typically charge a percentage of the hire's salary.

What's the key distinction? [Sourcing tools](/blog/candidate-sourcing-tools/) are pure software you operate yourself. Sourcing companies - whether AI platforms, RPOs, or staffing firms - deliver candidates as a service, with varying degrees of automation and human involvement. For a deeper look at [active sourcing](/blog/active-sourcing-guide/) methods and when to use them, see our guide.

### What Is Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)?

Recruitment process outsourcing (RPO) is a model where an outside firm takes over part or all of your hiring, from sourcing and screening to scheduling and offers. RPO providers usually bill per hire or a monthly fee per embedded recruiter.

## How to Evaluate a Candidate Sourcing Company

When evaluating a candidate sourcing company or talent sourcing service, five factors drive the decision: database size, what the platform automates, whether pricing is published, how fast candidates arrive, and whether the tool scales. All 10 companies here were scored against those factors. Market context matters too. From 2024 to 2025, AI use in HR tasks jumped from 26% to 43%, according to a survey of 2,040 HR professionals ([SHRM 2025 Talent Trends](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-talent-trends/ai-in-hr)).

1.  **Database coverage** - How many candidate profiles can the company access? Larger databases mean better coverage for niche roles.
2.  **AI and automation capabilities** - Does the company use AI for matching, outreach, and scheduling, or rely primarily on manual sourcers?
3.  **Pricing transparency** - Can you find pricing without a sales call? Are costs predictable or variable?
4.  **Speed to first candidates** - How quickly can you start seeing qualified profiles after signing up?
5.  **Scalability** - Can the company handle both a single niche hire and a 200-person hiring sprint?

### 1\. Pin

![Pin homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/pin-com-934154a1-1.webp)

**What it is:** An AI-powered sourcing platform that searches 850M+ candidate profiles and runs multi-channel outreach sequences across email, LinkedIn, and SMS. Email steps send automatically, while LinkedIn and SMS touches are queued as recruiter tasks with AI-drafted messages. Pin handles the entire top-of-funnel workflow: candidate discovery, personalized multi-channel outreach, team inbox, and interview scheduling.

**Database:** 850M+ profiles with 100% coverage in North America and Europe - one of the largest candidate databases available to recruiters.

**Key results:** Pin's sequences, which pair [automated email outreach](/blog/automated-candidate-outreach/) with LinkedIn and SMS tasks, deliver 5x better response rates than industry averages, and 83% of candidates Pin recommends are accepted into customers' hiring pipelines. Recruiters using Pin fill positions in an average of 14 days, reducing time-to-hire by 82% compared to traditional methods.

> "I jumped into Pin solo toward the end of 2025 and closed out the year with over $1M in billings during just the final 4 months - no team, no agency. The sourcing data is incredible, scanning 850M+ profiles with recruiter-level precision to uncover perfect-fit candidates I'd never find otherwise." - Nick Poloni, President at Cascadia Search Group

**Pricing:** A free-forever plan (no credit card, not a trial), Solo at $99/mo billed annually, Professional at $135 per user per month billed annually ($179 month-to-month), and custom Business pricing. Compare that to RPO providers charging $3,000-$8,000 per hire.

**Compliance:** SOC 2 Type 2 certified. No candidate names, gender, or protected characteristics are fed to the AI matching engine, eliminating sourcing bias at the algorithmic level.

**Best for:** Pin is the best AI sourcing platform for in-house recruiting teams and agencies of any size. It handles both niche specialist roles and high-volume hiring without the overhead of an RPO contract.

[Multi-channel outreach through Pin](https://www.pin.com/) delivers 5x better response rates - start with the free plan to test it on your next open role.

### 2\. LinkedIn Talent Solutions

![LinkedIn Talent Solutions homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/business-linkedin-com-0fa9ed25.webp)

**What it is:** LinkedIn's enterprise recruiting suite with LinkedIn Recruiter, job postings, and talent insights. Access to 1.3B+ members globally.

**Database:** 1.3B+ LinkedIn members, though only active profiles are accessible. Contact details require additional InMail credits.

**Key limitation:** LinkedIn Recruiter is a search-and-InMail tool, not an end-to-end sourcing workflow. There's no automated email sequencing, no SMS, and no built-in interview scheduling. For a full [Pin vs LinkedIn Recruiter comparison](/blog/pin-vs-linkedin-recruiter/) covering pricing, response rates, and automation depth, see our dedicated breakdown.

**Pricing:** LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate lists at roughly $8,000-$12,000 per seat per year ([Vendr, 2026](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/linkedin)). Recruiter Lite runs around $1,680/year billed annually but limits search filters and InMail credits.

**Fits:** Teams embedded in the LinkedIn ecosystem, though you'll need separate tools for email outreach, SMS, and scheduling - all included in Pin.

### 3\. Leoforce (formerly Arya)

![Leoforce homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/leoforce-com-e1efd8f7.webp)

**Platform:** An AI sourcing platform that uses predictive analytics to rank candidate fit, with 60+ ATS and CRM integrations across the recruiting stack. Its products are now sold as Source, Capture, Convert, and Rediscovery.

**Coverage:** Aggregates across job boards, social platforms, and internal databases in real time rather than maintaining a proprietary database.

**How it works:** Leoforce's AI scores candidates against the role, predicts their likelihood of interest, and resurfaces past applicants already sitting in your ATS.

**Cost:** Leoforce retired the Arya brand and its per-job Arya Pulse plan in 2025. Every product is now quote-only, with outcome-based pricing based on hiring volume.

**Ideal for:** Recruiting functions that want AI-driven candidate scoring layered on their ATS, though quote-only, outcome-based pricing makes costs hard to predict before a sales call.

### 4\. Manatal

![Manatal homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/manatal-com-1c116aa9.webp)

**Description:** An AI recruiting platform combining ATS, CRM, and candidate sourcing. Manatal's AI recommends candidates from LinkedIn, job boards, and career pages.

**Talent pool:** Manatal advertises a database of 700M+ candidates (a vendor claim with an undefined footnote), supplemented by profiles from connected job boards and social platforms.

**Standout feature:** Automated resume parsing, social media enrichment, and AI candidate recommendations pulling profile data from LinkedIn and other channels to fill in gaps.

**Plans:** Professional at $15/user/mo, Enterprise at $35/user/mo, and Enterprise Plus at $55/user/mo on annual billing ($19/$39/$59 month-to-month), plus a custom tier and a 14-day free trial.

**Right for:** Budget-conscious hiring managers needing a combined ATS and basic sourcing tool. Sourcing depth is limited compared to dedicated solutions with 850M+ profiles.

### 5\. Paradox (Olivia)

![Paradox (Olivia) homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/paradox-ai-dc511fee.webp)

**About Paradox:** Paradox's AI assistant "Olivia" automates candidate engagement through chatbot-driven screening, scheduling, and follow-ups across text, web, and social channels. [Workday completed its acquisition of Paradox](https://newsroom.workday.com/2025-10-01-Workday-Completes-Acquisition-of-Paradox) on October 1, 2025, so Olivia now sits inside Workday's recruiting suite.

**Reach:** Paradox doesn't maintain a candidate sourcing database. Instead, it engages and screens candidates who enter your pipeline through job postings, career sites, and events.

**Where it shines:** Automated screening conversations, interview scheduling, and multilingual support. Strong for high-volume roles where engaging applicants fast matters more than proactive sourcing.

**Rates:** Quote-only enterprise pricing with no published rates. Expect a custom quote based on hiring volume and modules selected.

**Works best for:** Enterprise organizations in retail, hospitality, or healthcare automating inbound candidate engagement at high volume. Not a fit for outbound sourcing of passive talent.

## Top 5 Outsourced Candidate Sourcing Services (RPO and Staffing)

Outsourced candidate sourcing services from RPO providers and staffing firms deliver sourcing as a managed service, embedding dedicated recruiters into your team - at a much higher cost than AI platforms. Industry research shows [RPO programs](/blog/rpo-guide-recruiters/) reduce recruitment costs by an average of 35%, with optimized programs reaching 40% savings ([RPOA, 2024](https://blog.rpoassociation.org/blog/an-employers-comprehensive-guide-to-rpo-pricing-models)). These five firms are the established players in managed sourcing, but the price reflects the white-glove service.

### 6\. Cielo

![Cielo homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/cielotalent-com-6ed5ec6e.webp)

**What it is:** A global RPO provider that ranked No. 1 on [HRO Today's 2025 Baker's Dozen list for enterprise RPO](https://www.hrotoday.com/news/talent-acquisition/rpo-staffing/2025-bakers-dozen-customer-satisfaction-ratings-rpo/), a customer-satisfaction ranking (Korn Ferry topped the 2026 list). Cielo's CLO.ai, a generative AI tool launched in 2024, supports AI-augmented sourcing.

**Sourcing model:** Dedicated sourcing teams embedded in your organization, supported by proprietary AI tools. Handles everything from job profiling through candidate delivery and offer management.

**Fee structure:** Enterprise RPO contracts typically start at $5,000-$15,000/month per dedicated recruiter, with additional per-hire fees. Minimum engagements usually run $100K+ annually.

**Suited for:** Enterprise organizations hiring 100+ roles annually that want a fully managed sourcing operation, though the cost and 6-12 month implementation timeline makes this impractical for small teams or fast-moving startups.

### 7\. Randstad Sourceright

![Randstad Sourceright homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/randstadsourceright-com-0df57bc2.webp)

**What it is:** The RPO and MSP (Managed Service Provider) arm of Randstad, the world's largest staffing firm (€23.1B in 2025 revenue, per [Randstad's FY2025 results](https://www.randstad.com/press/2026/fy-2025-perform-transform/)). Randstad's enterprise arm was named a Leader in [Everest Group's 2026 RPO PEAK Matrix](https://www.randstadenterprise.com/insights/randstad-enterprise-insights/everest-groups-recruitment-process-outsourcing-peak-matrix/) for the 16th consecutive year. They offer talent sourcing, employer branding, and workforce analytics as part of their managed programs.

**How it works:** Combines dedicated sourcing teams with Randstad's global talent data and AI tools. Their "Talent Intelligence" platform provides market mapping and predictive analytics for sourcing strategy.

**Contract costs:** Custom enterprise pricing based on hiring volume and geographic scope. Typical RPO engagements run $3,000-$8,000 per hire or $5,000-$15,000/month per dedicated recruiter.

**Built for:** Global enterprises that need managed sourcing across multiple regions and languages, though the enterprise-only model and lengthy procurement process puts this out of reach for mid-market organizations or agencies.

### 8\. AMS (Alexander Mann Solutions)

![AMS (Alexander Mann Solutions) homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/web-archive-org-9a25762f.webp)

**What it is:** A global talent acquisition and workforce solutions company that says it has 10,000+ talent experts working across 120+ countries. In October 2025, AMS launched "Next Gen Talent Acquisition" powered by AMS One - their integrated technology and services platform.

**Delivery model:** Full RPO services including dedicated sourcing pods, employer branding, assessment design, and analytics. AMS emphasizes a consultative approach, working as an extension of your internal TA team.

**Billing:** Enterprise RPO pricing, typically $3,000-$8,000 per hire depending on role seniority and volume commitments, with custom quotes required for all engagements.

**Designed for:** Large organizations that need a strategic talent partner rather than just a sourcing vendor, though the enterprise focus and custom pricing model puts AMS out of reach for teams hiring fewer than 50 roles annually.

### 9\. Kforce

![Kforce homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/kforce-com-b2b1ffb5.webp)

**What it is:** A $1.33 billion (2025 revenue) professional staffing firm specializing in technology and finance roles ([Kforce 2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/930420/000093042026000007/kfrc-20251231.htm)). Technology accounts for about 93% of that revenue, making Kforce one of the largest tech sourcing operations in the US.

**Their model:** Kforce's internal recruiters source and screen candidates from their proprietary database and external channels, then present qualified shortlists. They operate on a contingency or contract staffing model rather than RPO.

**Rate structure:** Contingency fees typically 20-25% of the hire's first-year salary for direct placement. Contract staffing uses hourly bill rates with a markup over the candidate's pay rate.

**Recommended for:** Employers hiring technology or finance professionals on a contingency basis. Each extra hire adds another fee. An AI sourcing tool costs the same whether you fill 5 or 50 roles.

### 10\. Robert Half

![Robert Half homepage screenshot](/images/urlbox/roberthalf-com-c783c29f.webp)

**What it is:** One of the world's largest specialized staffing firms with $5.38 billion in 2025 revenue, including $3.43 billion from its Talent Solutions division ([Robert Half Q4 2025 earnings](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/315213/000031521326000003/rhiq42025ex991.htm)). They maintain one of the most extensive candidate networks in accounting, finance, technology, legal, and administrative roles.

**Their approach:** Robert Half's recruiters source from their proprietary database and professional networks. They specialize in both temporary and permanent placements, with a strong bench of pre-vetted candidates ready for rapid deployment.

**Fee model:** Temporary staffing uses hourly markups (typically 40-60% over pay rate). Direct hire fees run 20-30% of first-year salary. No subscription or flat-fee option.

**Works for:** Organizations needing pre-vetted candidates in finance, accounting, or administrative roles quickly. Note that percentage-based pricing makes every hire expensive compared to AI platforms, where monthly costs stay flat regardless of volume. ## How to Choose the Right Sourcing Company for Your Team

Choosing the right candidate sourcing company comes down to three factors: hiring volume, internal TA capacity, and budget. Here's a decision framework based on those three factors:

**Factor 1: Hiring volume.** Filling fewer than 50 roles per year means an RPO contract won't make economic sense - setup costs and minimum commitments eat into the savings. AI platforms scale from one placement to hundreds without changing what you pay. Filling 100+ roles annually across multiple departments is where an RPO's dedicated sourcing team starts to justify the investment.

**Factor 2: Internal TA capacity.** Do you have recruiters who can manage a sourcing tool, or do you need someone else to do the sourcing entirely? With an AI platform, at least one person needs to review candidates and make decisions. RPOs handle everything from [search strategy](/blog/executive-search-strategy/) through candidate delivery. Staffing firms are most hands-off: they present pre-vetted shortlists you just need to interview.

**Factor 3: Budget reality.** Pin's Solo plan costs $1,188 a year, and Professional runs $1,620 per seat per year. An RPO runs $100K-$500K+/year. A staffing firm charges 20-30% per hire. For a team bringing on 20 people at an average $80K salary, that's the difference between roughly $1,200 for a year of Pin Solo versus $320,000-$480,000 (staffing firm). Traditional models rarely win on math unless you genuinely can't allocate any internal time to recruiting.

**Choose an AI sourcing platform if:**

-   You have at least one recruiter who can manage the tool
-   Your budget is under $5,000/month for sourcing
-   You need to fill roles across multiple functions and levels
-   Speed matters - you want candidates within days, not weeks
-   You want predictable pricing more than white-glove service

**Choose an RPO provider if:**

-   You're hiring 100+ roles annually and need dedicated sourcing support
-   Your internal TA team is stretched and can't take on sourcing volume
-   You need managed services across multiple geographies and languages
-   You have budget for $100K+ annually in outsourced recruiting

**Choose a staffing firm if:**

-   You need pre-screened candidates for specific roles quickly
-   You're filling temporary, contract, or project-based positions
-   You don't have internal recruiting capacity at all
-   You're willing to pay 20-30% of salary for the convenience

When choosing a candidate sourcing company in 2026, the math increasingly favors AI platforms for most in-house teams. Test sourcing on a live role with Pin's free plan first. Paid plans start at $99/mo (Solo, billed annually) with 850M+ profiles and multi-channel outreach sequences, the same candidate sourcing services that would cost $5,000-$15,000/month through an RPO provider.

## RPO Pricing Models Explained

RPO providers use four main pricing models: cost-per-hire, monthly management fee, hybrid, and hourly consulting. The model you choose directly determines cost predictability and per-hire economics. Here's how each works, with typical price ranges from RPOA ([RPOA, 2024](https://blog.rpoassociation.org/blog/an-employers-comprehensive-guide-to-rpo-pricing-models)):

**Cost-per-hire:** You pay a fixed fee for each completed hire, typically $3,000-$8,000 for professional roles. Costs are most predictable here, and incentives align around outcomes. Most steady-state RPO programs use this model.

**Monthly management fee:** You pay $5,000-$15,000 per month per dedicated recruiter embedded in your team, regardless of how many hires they make. Consistent volume and a need for dedicated attention make this work. When recruitment slows, though, you're still paying the monthly fee.

**Hybrid model:** A combination of a base monthly fee ($3,000-$5,000/month) plus a reduced per-hire fee ($1,500-$3,000/hire). This model keeps costs predictable while still rewarding performance. Growing in popularity for organizations with variable recruitment volume.

**Hourly consulting:** You pay $75-$150/hour for sourcing support on a project basis. Surge hiring, one-time sourcing projects, and teams needing temporary capacity without a long-term RPO commitment are the typical use cases.

Compare those ranges to AI sourcing platforms: Pin's Professional plan at $135 per user per month (billed annually) includes 850M+ profiles, 1,000 contact lookup credits per user each month, outreach sequences, and interview scheduling. For one recruiter making 10 hires a month, that's under $14 per hire in platform cost - versus $3,000-$8,000 per hire through an RPO.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between a sourcing company and a recruiting agency?

Finding and qualifying candidates is the core of what sourcing companies do - the top-of-funnel work. A [recruiting agency](/blog/how-to-choose-recruiting-agency/) handles the full hiring cycle including screening, interviewing, negotiating offers, and closing. Sourcing companies deliver candidate profiles and initial engagement; recruiting agencies deliver hired employees. Some firms, like RPO providers, offer both.
