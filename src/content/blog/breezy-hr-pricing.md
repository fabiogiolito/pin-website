Last updated: October 7, 2026

Breezy HR pricing starts at $0/month on the Bootstrap free plan, $157/month for Startup, $273/month for Growth, and $439/month for Business on annual billing. Monthly billing runs higher: $189, $329, and $529, respectively. A fifth tier, Pro, requires a custom sales quote. That puts Breezy in the mid-range of ATS pricing - more expensive than [JazzHR](https://www.pin.com/blog/jazzhr-pricing/) but significantly cheaper than enterprise platforms like Greenhouse or Lever.

Founded in 2014 by Darren Bounds and acquired by Learning Technologies Group (LTG) in April 2019, Breezy HR has served 13,000+ companies and processed 15 million candidates across 72 countries, according to [Breezy's company blog](https://breezy.hr/blog/behind-the-deal-why-ltg-bought-breezy-hr). Ratings stand at 4.4/5 on [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/breezy-hr/reviews) (683 reviews) and 4.5/5 on [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/141702/Breezy-HR/reviews/) (1,420 reviews) as of October 2026.

This guide breaks down what each Breezy HR plan includes and maps the add-on costs that aren't obvious from their pricing page. Five alternatives are also compared here, including platforms with AI sourcing that Breezy doesn't offer at any tier.

> **TL;DR:**
>
> - **Five tiers from $0 to $529/mo.** Bootstrap is free but capped at 1 active job, Startup runs $157/mo (annual), Growth $273/mo, Business $439/mo, and Pro requires a custom quote.
> - **Unlimited users at every tier.** Breezy uses flat monthly fees instead of per-seat pricing, which is a real advantage over Greenhouse or Lever for teams with many hiring managers.
> - **Core features are gated high.** Interview scorecards and eSignatures require Growth ($273/mo), and HRIS integrations, offer management, and nurture campaigns live only on Business ($439/mo).
> - **Add-ons inflate the sticker price.** SMS ($41/mo), onboarding ($49/mo), and AI credits ($30 per 100K) stack on top. Growth plus SMS and onboarding is $363/mo, 33% above the headline price.
> - **No native sourcing or outreach.** Breezy covers ATS functions only. For AI sourcing and outreach, [Pin](https://www.pin.com/) pairs automated email with LinkedIn and SMS tasks in one sequence, delivering 5x better response rates with no per-channel add-ons.

Four billing details matter before you sign:

-   **Free tier exists, but barely.** Bootstrap limits you to 1 active position at a time. Candidate data is retained for only 30 days. No email integration, no scheduling, no analytics. It functions as a sandbox, not a working ATS.
-   **Unlimited users on every plan.** Unlike Greenhouse, [Lever](/blog/lever-pricing/), and most enterprise platforms that charge per recruiter seat, Breezy uses flat monthly fees. That's a real advantage for organizations with multiple hiring managers who need visibility into the pipeline.
-   **Annual billing saves 17%.** Without an annual commitment, Startup jumps from $157/mo to $189/mo. Business goes from $439/mo to $529/mo. Month-to-month billing is available on every paid tier, but the savings only come with upfront commitment.
-   **14-day free trial, no credit card.** Breezy's trial unlocks full feature access for 14 days without payment details, and it includes 100,000 Breezy Intelligence AI credits that don't expire.

For teams that need more than applicant tracking - like outbound sourcing, automated outreach, or AI-powered candidate matching - Breezy doesn't cover those functions. A separate sourcing tool would be needed alongside it. When evaluating your full [ATS options for 2026](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-applicant-tracking-systems/), understanding where Breezy HR pricing fits against the full recruiting stack matters before you commit.

## Is the Breezy HR Free Plan Worth Using?

Bootstrap is free forever with no credit card required. That sounds generous until you look at the restrictions. The free tier caps you at 1 active position at a time, retains candidate data for only 30 days, and strips out most of the features that make an ATS functional.

Missing from Bootstrap entirely:

-   No email or calendar integration
-   No interview scheduling
-   No candidate automations or questionnaires
-   No analytics or reporting
-   No EEOC compliance reporting
-   No pipeline customization
-   No mobile app access

What you do get: a branded career page, resume parsing, GDPR compliance, and distribution to 50+ job boards for that single position. If you're a solo founder making your very first hire, the Bootstrap plan works as a proof of concept. For any team hiring more than one role, it's not a real option.

Compare that to other platforms offering free tiers. [Pin's free plan](https://www.pin.com/) includes AI-powered sourcing across 850M+ candidate profiles - not just applicant tracking. That difference matters: Breezy's free tier helps you post a job and wait. An AI sourcing platform actively finds candidates who match your requirements.

## What Are the Biggest Drawbacks of Breezy HR?

Rated 4.4/5 on [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/breezy-hr/reviews) (683 reviews) and 4.5/5 on [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/141702/Breezy-HR/reviews/) (1,420 reviews), Breezy HR earns solid marks overall. Both review platforms surface consistent complaints about feature gating, limited AI capabilities, and add-on costs that inflate the effective price. Six issues come up most often:

-   **Feature gating is aggressive.** Interview scorecards, eSignatures, and multiple pipelines require the Growth plan ($273/mo). HRIS integrations and offer management need Business ($439/mo). Teams often discover mid-implementation that the feature they need is locked behind the next tier up.
-   **No outbound sourcing.** Breezy is a pure inbound ATS. It tracks candidates who apply but doesn't help you find passive candidates. There's no candidate database, no AI matching, and no automated outreach beyond what you build manually.
-   **Add-ons inflate the real price.** SMS texting ($41/mo), onboarding ($49/mo), and AI credits ($30 per 100K) are all separate charges. A Growth plan with SMS and onboarding costs $363/mo - 33% more than the listed price.
-   **Interface feels dated.** Multiple reviewers note that Breezy's UI hasn't kept pace with newer competitors. Navigation can feel clunky, particularly in the reporting and pipeline management sections.
-   **Limited AI capabilities.** Breezy Intelligence offers AI credits for candidate scoring, but it's a basic add-on - not comparable to platforms built around AI from the ground up. There's no semantic search, no AI-driven candidate matching, and no automated outreach personalization.
-   **Dedicated support requires Pro tier.** Only the Pro plan (custom pricing) includes a dedicated account manager. Teams on Business and below rely on standard support channels with no escalation path.

None of these are dealbreakers for teams that purely need applicant tracking. But if your hiring process depends on outbound sourcing or multi-channel outreach, Breezy requires stacking additional tools - and those costs add up.

## Who Should (and Shouldn't) Use Breezy HR?

Breezy HR works well for a specific type of team - primarily inbound-focused hiring operations with many users and moderate hiring volume. With unlimited users on every plan and Breezy HR pricing from $157-$439/mo, it targets mid-market companies that need structured applicant tracking without per-seat cost anxiety. Outbound sourcing and multi-channel candidate engagement are where it falls short.

### Teams That Get the Most Value From Breezy HR

-   **You only need inbound applicant tracking.** Posting jobs, managing applications, and moving candidates through a pipeline - Breezy handles this well from the Startup tier and up.
-   **You have many users but few open roles.** Unlimited users on every plan means a team of 20 hiring managers pays the same as a team of 2. That's rare in the ATS market.
-   **You need built-in video interviewing.** Breezy includes live video and video assessments on paid plans. Most competitors charge extra for this or don't offer it at all.
-   **Budget is under $300/mo for ATS.** The Startup ($157/mo) and Growth ($273/mo) plans deliver reasonable feature sets for small to mid-size teams.

### Teams That Should Skip Breezy HR

-   **You need to source passive candidates.** Breezy has no candidate database and no outbound sourcing. If you're spending hours on LinkedIn searching for candidates manually, Breezy won't change that.
-   **Your team relies on multi-channel outreach.** Email sequences, LinkedIn messages, SMS campaigns - Breezy doesn't automate outbound communication. SMS is a $41/mo add-on even for paid plans.
-   **You're a recruiting agency managing multiple clients.** While Breezy supports multiple pipelines on Growth and above, it wasn't designed for agency workflows. Dedicated recruitment automation platforms handle multi-client operations more naturally.
-   **You want AI-powered candidate matching at scale.** Breezy Intelligence provides basic AI scoring, but it's a credit-based add-on - not a core platform capability. For teams that need AI to search across hundreds of millions of profiles, Breezy doesn't compete in that space.
