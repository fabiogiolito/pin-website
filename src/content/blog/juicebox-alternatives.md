The Juicebox alternatives worth shortlisting in 2026 start with [Pin](https://www.pin.com/), which bundles two AI agents per user into its Professional plan at $135 per user per month, billed annually. Juicebox (PeopleGPT), the AI candidate-sourcing platform, instead bills $199 per agent and 3 credits per phone number, where Pin charges one credit. Pin is the best Juicebox alternative for recruiting teams that want AI sourcing, multi-channel sequences, and interview scheduling on one bill. Its database offers 100% candidate coverage in North America and Europe, and at 4.8/5 it is the highest-rated AI recruiting software on G2.

Juicebox's funding hasn't made it cheaper. It announced an $80 million Series B in March 2026 ([Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/juicebox-80-million-at-850-million-valuation-raised-for-ai-recruiting-platform/), 2026) and raised its monthly Starter and Growth prices that same month. Its Growth plan now costs 81% more on annual billing than in April 2024, and its seat cap has been halved from 10 to 5. Growing teams also run into its plan design: agents cost extra on every tier, the Growth plan stops at five seats, and ATS sync requires a custom Business contract.

This guide ranks eight AI sourcing tools on the things Juicebox users actually switch over: price stability, agent costs, contact data, seat limits, and ATS sync. Metaview, Noon AI, LinkedIn Recruiter, Gem, AmazingHiring, Workable, and Manatal round out the list. Every price below was re-checked on September 28, 2026 against each vendor's live page or latest archived capture, and each capture is dated in the text.

## Why Are Recruiters Leaving Juicebox in 2026?

Rising prices come first. Growth cost $99 per seat per month on annual billing in the April 14, 2024 Wayback Machine capture of Juicebox's pricing page, rose to $129 by May 2025, and costs $179 today, an 81% increase. On monthly billing, Growth went from $179 (January 19, 2026 capture) to $199 (March 23) and stayed there after Starter's July cut ([UsagePricing](https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/activity/juicebox-2026-07-21-starter-price-cut-credits), July 2026). Over the same stretch, the agent add-on went from $300 for two agents to $199 per agent.

Six plan limits start to bite once teams move past a single seat:

- **Agents are a separate bill.** Every paid tier lists Juicebox Agents at $199 per agent per month ([UsagePricing](https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/juicebox), 2026), a 33% jump per agent from January's two-for-$300 bundle. Idle agents still bill: Juicebox's docs say "Agents that are sourcing, paused, or awaiting action each take up an Agent seat."
- **Growth caps at five seats, down from 10.** Growth allowed 10 seats until mid-2025 (archived pricing captures, May and August 2025). Now a sixth recruiter forces a custom-priced Business contract.
- **Phone data costs extra.** Juicebox's own pricing table marks phone numbers as unavailable on Starter and charges 3 credits per phone reveal on Growth (checked September 28, 2026), so an email plus a phone costs 4 credits. Its docs say the way to get more credits is to add a seat. On Pin, the same email-plus-phone lookup costs 2 credits on every plan.
- **ATS sync isn't sold below Business.** Juicebox's help center confirms Starter and Growth export via CSV or PDF only. Its homepage promises "41 ATS systems and 21 CRMs," yet its integration docs list three CRMs and call CRM sync "more constrained."
- **No interview scheduling.** Interview scheduling doesn't appear on any plan in the August 30 capture of the pricing page, so teams bolt on a separate calendar tool.
- **Onboarding sits on Business too.** Juicebox's plan comparison lists onboarding and training, migration assistance, and SOC 2 report access only on Business (checked September 28, 2026). Pin includes onboarding and training from its $99 Solo plan up.

## Which AI Sourcing Tools Come Closest to Replacing Juicebox?

Pin, Metaview, [Noon AI](/blog/juicebox-vs-noon/), and LinkedIn Recruiter are the closest Juicebox AI competitors for agent-driven sourcing. Pin is the best of the four for teams that want agents inside the seat price, while Metaview Pro caps sourcing at 200 profiles per user a month. This group matters because more than half (52%) of talent leaders plan to add autonomous AI agents to their teams in 2026 ([Korn Ferry](https://www.kornferry.com/about-us/press/korn-ferry-research-unveils-top-talent-acquisition-trends-shaping-2026), 2025).

### 1. Pin - Best Overall Juicebox Alternative

Agents, phone lookups, and scheduling, the pieces Juicebox bills separately or skips, come standard on Pin's Professional plan, and at 4.8/5 Pin is the highest-rated AI recruiting software on G2. Drawn from professional networks, GitHub, Stack Overflow, patents, and the open web, its search covers more than 850 million profiles. Professional costs $135 per user per month billed annually or $179 month to month. There's no seat cap, and each seat includes two AI agents and 1,000 contact credits a month, according to [Pin's pricing page](https://www.pin.com/pricing/). A personal email, a work email, and a phone number each cost the same single credit.

From first search to booked interview, the workflow stays in one place. You get AI search, sequences that pair automated email with LinkedIn and SMS tasks, a shared inbox, built-in scheduling, and a recruiting CRM. Pin's 2026 user survey across 2,000+ organizations and 20,000+ users reports an 83% acceptance rate on recommended candidates, the highest in the industry, and 12 hours saved per recruiter each week.

> "I jumped into Pin solo toward the end of 2025 and closed out the year with over $1M in billings during just the final 4 months - no team, no agency. The sourcing data is incredible, scanning 850M+ profiles with recruiter-level precision to uncover perfect-fit candidates I'd never find otherwise. Best of all, the outreach feels genuinely personalized and non-generic, driving sky-high reply rates where candidates even thank me for the thoughtful messages."
> Nick Poloni, President at Cascadia Search Group

**Best for:** Agencies and in-house teams that outgrew one seat and want agents, contact data, and scheduling without add-on math.

**ATS sync:** 120+ integrations, included on Business and available as an add-on on Solo and Professional, so no paid plan is locked out. For the full feature grid, see [how Pin and Juicebox stack up feature by feature](/blog/pin-vs-juicebox/).

### 2. Metaview - Agent Sourcing Priced by the Profile

If per-agent fees are the sticking point, [Metaview prices its sourcing agent](/blog/metaview-alternatives/) into each seat instead. Pro is $100 per user per month for 200 profiles sourced, and Max is $300 for unlimited sourcing. A free tier covers the first 100 profiles, per a September 2, 2026 Wayback Machine capture of Metaview's pricing page.

Contact data is where Metaview's expenses climb considerably. Each email found costs 3 credits and each phone number costs 10. Every workspace gets 500 free credits a month, shared across the team with no rollover (Metaview outreach page, captured June 1, 2026). Top-ups cost $10 per 100 credits, so a phone number works out to about $1 once the free pool runs dry. In August 2026, Metaview bought Reval as it prepares to bring its newer outbound agent, fillmore, to more customers ([Business Wire via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/metaview-acquires-reval-accelerate-shift-130000064.html), 2026). That agent doesn't appear on Metaview's published price list.

**Good for:** Small teams with modest volume that can live under a 200-profile monthly cap.

**Watch out for:** The cap on Pro, the $300 price of uncapped sourcing, and 10-credit phone lookups. Our [Pin vs Metaview breakdown](/blog/pin-vs-metaview/) walks through the plan math.

### 3. Noon AI - One Unlimited Plan, No Published Price

Credit fatigue is Noon AI's entire pitch. It sells a single plan with "no caps, no credits, no per-seat fees," covering unlimited sourcing, email enrichment, agents, and seats (Noon's pricing page, Wayback Machine capture, August 23, 2026).

Budgeting is the catch, because that page states Noon doesn't publish a dollar price and quotes each organization individually. Comparing costs therefore requires a sales call. Its 10x ROI guarantee promises a full refund, but only on annual subscriptions. Teams must also log in on three separate days and open at least one new role or search every week, per Noon's guarantee terms (checked September 28, 2026). Any refund arrives after the 12-month period, based on recruiter time saved that Noon values at $60 an hour. Noon also has no Capterra or Product Hunt listing and no published case studies.

**Good for:** Larger teams that would rather negotiate one flat contract than track usage.

**Watch out for:** No list price, weekly usage conditions on the guarantee, and no independent review listings. The [Pin vs Noon AI comparison](/blog/pin-vs-noon/) covers the feature differences.

### 4. LinkedIn Recruiter - The Incumbent With First-Party Data

Some recruiters go back to the source: LinkedIn Recruiter searches LinkedIn's own member data and includes InMail, which aggregated tools can't offer. That first-party access carries enterprise pricing. Per Vendr, Recruiter Lite lists around $140 to $170 per seat per month billed annually, while higher Recruiter tiers list at $8,000 to $12,000 per seat a year. Across all LinkedIn products, Vendr's median contract is $38,480 a year over 2,367 purchases ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/linkedin), February 2026).

LinkedIn's Hiring Assistant agent expanded globally in September 2025 ([HR Brew](https://www.hr-brew.com/stories/2025/09/03/linkedin-s-first-agent-hiring-assistant-expands-availability-globally-after-first-year), 2025) and sells as a Recruiter add-on. In the UK, the government's G-Cloud rate card lists a Recruiter licence at £8,925 a year for one or two licences, excluding VAT ([Digital Marketplace](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/712281/850052488849651-pricing-document-2025-10-20-1430.pdf), October 2025). That card also offered Hiring Assistant at a promotional £2,079 per licence through June 30, 2026.

**Good for:** Enterprise teams whose hiring runs on InMail and who already hold annual seat contracts.

**Watch out for:** Annual commitments, monthly InMail caps, and data limited to one network. See [our Juicebox vs LinkedIn Recruiter comparison](/blog/juicebox-vs-linkedin-recruiter/) for the head-to-head.

## Which Juicebox Competitors Fit Specialist or ATS-Led Teams?

Gem, AmazingHiring, Workable, and Manatal are Juicebox competitors built for narrower jobs than AI sourcing. Gem is a recruiting CRM that reserves its AI suite for Enterprise, AmazingHiring covers technical roles only, and Workable and Manatal are applicant tracking systems. Two-thirds (66%) of software buyers hit unexpected disruption, regret, or both after a purchase ([Capterra via Business Wire](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/businesses-disappointing-software-purchases-twice-120000209.html), 2025), so matching the tool to the workflow matters more than the feature list.

### 5. Gem - Recruiting CRM With AI on the Top Tier

For teams whose real gap is a CRM rather than a search engine, Gem puts sequencing and pipeline tracking next to search. Its staffing plans start at $99 per user per month billed yearly for Essentials (up to 3 users, 150 email-finding credits each) and $149 for Professional (up to 10 users, 750 credits). Only Enterprise lists the AI suite, covering sourcing, application review, and sequences (Gem's staffing pricing page, Wayback Machine capture, June 11, 2026).

In-house plans are quoted by headcount. Vendr puts Gem's median contract at $25,700 across 233 purchases, with deals from $7,000 to $73,184 ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/gem), February 2026).

**Good for:** Established TA teams that want CRM depth and can budget for Enterprise.

**Watch out for:** The AI sourcing that replaces Juicebox's search sits in the top tier. Our look at [what Gem costs in practice](/blog/gem-pricing/) has the contract ranges.

### 6. AmazingHiring - Sourcing for Technical Roles Only

Engineering-only recruiting teams have a narrower, specialized option. AmazingHiring aggregates profiles from 50+ networks, including GitHub, Stack Overflow, and Kaggle, and scores candidates on technical activity. Its Capterra listing showed a starting price of about $4,800 per user per year as of September 2026.

**Good for:** Tech-only recruiting teams hiring software, data, or ML engineers.

**Watch out for:** It covers technical roles only, so a team that also hires sales or operations still needs a second tool. At about $24,000 a year for five seats, it costs more than every other published-price option here except LinkedIn Recruiter.

### 7. Workable - An ATS With a Sourcing Suite Built In

Because Juicebox has no applicant tracking, some teams would rather run a single system that sources too. Workable's Standard plan costs $299 per month billed annually ($3,588 a year) for companies with 1 to 20 employees (Workable's pricing page, Wayback Machine capture, September 17, 2026). Its candidate sourcing suite includes 400M+ candidate profiles.

**Good for:** Small in-house teams that want job posting, tracking, and light sourcing in one place.

**Watch out for:** Pricing scales with company headcount, not recruiter seats, which fits employers better than agencies. Outbound sourcing is one feature of a broad ATS, not the core product.

### 8. Manatal - Budget ATS for Inbound-Heavy Hiring

Where most hires come from applicants, paying for AI outbound sourcing is overkill. Manatal costs $15, $35, or $55 per user per month billed yearly (Manatal's pricing page, Wayback Machine capture, September 7, 2026). Its entry plan covers up to 15 jobs and 10,000 candidates.

**Good for:** Lean teams that mostly screen and track inbound applicants.

**Watch out for:** It's built to organize applicants, not to search an external profile database, so it doesn't replace Juicebox's discovery.

## How Do You Switch From Juicebox Without Losing Your Pipeline?

Switching from Juicebox takes about a week, and the order matters because monthly-plan credits expire and exports below Business are CSV or PDF only:

1. **Check your renewal terms first.** Business plans are annual only. On annual Starter and Growth plans, the full year of credits is granted upfront, so note your balance before you cancel.
2. **Spend credits on live pipelines.** Monthly credits don't roll over, so reveal contacts for active candidates before the cycle ends.
3. **Export your projects early.** Starter and Growth export via CSV or PDF only, within a monthly cap of 500 exports on Starter and 1,500 per seat on Growth.
4. **Run your three hardest open roles side by side.** A week of parallel searches shows which tool finds candidates your current shortlist misses.
5. **Rebuild sequences with a second channel.** Pair automated email with a LinkedIn or SMS task.
