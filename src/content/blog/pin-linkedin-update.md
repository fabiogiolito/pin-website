Hey, Steve here, CEO of Pin.

By now, you've probably seen that Pin's page and my profile are no longer on LinkedIn. I wanted to share an update directly with you.

## Pin is fully operational

First things first: nothing has changed. The product is fully operational, and our customers are unaffected. We serve more than 2,000 recruiting teams, and growth has actually picked up since.

Pin does not depend on LinkedIn. It never has.

LinkedIn's team raised concerns about our presence on their platform, and we're working with them to resolve it. I'll share more once that's done.

To be clear, LinkedIn doesn't get to determine who's in business or not.

In the meantime, you may hear from our competitors that Pin is shutting down. That's simply not true. If anything, we're just getting started.

## What's coming next

You may have noticed this new HD version of myself in the video. You'll see more of me like this as we announce some of the most ambitious features we've ever built.

The first announcement will be on **September 29** at Ben Mena's virtual summit. <a href="https://recruiters-finish-the-year-strong.heysummit.com/" rel="nofollow">Register for the Finish the Year Strong Summit</a> to join us.

## Thank you for the support

A lot of you reached out this week to check in. Asking if we're okay, telling us you're not going anywhere. I read every one of those.

It meant a lot to me and to the team. So thank you for the support.

Back to work.

Steve<br>
CEO of Pin
