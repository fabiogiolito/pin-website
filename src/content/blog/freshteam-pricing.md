Freshteam pricing started at $0 on the Free plan and scaled to $4/employee/month plus a $169 platform fee on Enterprise - but Freshworks halted all new subscriptions and renewals as of March 7, 2026. Existing customers have until April 2027 to migrate, according to [PeopleMatters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/business/freshworks-to-end-freshteam-hr-product-stop-renewals-from-march-2026-47939) (2025). Gartner Peer Insights now lists it as "Freshteam (Legacy)" in its talent acquisition reviews.

If you're a current Freshteam customer wondering what you're paying for - or an HR team that was considering Freshteam and now needs a Plan B - this guide covers both scenarios. We'll break down every plan and its limits, explain the hidden platform fees that inflated small-team costs, and walk through five Freshteam alternatives worth evaluating before the April 2027 deadline hits.

> **TL;DR:**
>
> - **Freshteam is being sunset.** Freshworks halted new subscriptions and renewals on March 7, 2026, with full shutdown by April 2027 ([PeopleMatters, 2025](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/business/freshworks-to-end-freshteam-hr-product-stop-renewals-from-march-2026-47939)).
> - **Pricing ran $0 to $4 per employee plus platform fees.** Four tiers (Free, Growth, Pro, Enterprise) stacked a $0-$169/month platform fee on top of a per-employee charge.
> - **Small teams paid the platform-fee tax.** A 10-person Growth team effectively paid $6.90/employee, not the $1 list price, because the flat fee dominated the bill.
> - **Current customers have until April 2027 to migrate.** Start evaluating replacements now; the product is already flagged as "Freshteam (Legacy)" on Gartner Peer Insights.
> - **Strong alternatives exist at similar price points.** BambooHR, Workable, JazzHR, Zoho Recruit, and Breezy HR each handle the small-to-mid-market ATS use case Freshteam served.

## What Did Freshteam Cost Before the Shutdown?

Pricing was structured as two separate charges: a per-employee fee billed monthly plus a flat platform fee that stayed the same regardless of team size. Annual billing rates across the three paid tiers ran $1, $2, and $4 per employee per month, according to [eLearning Industry](https://elearningindustry.com/directory/elearning-software/freshteam/pricing) (2025) and [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/166825/Freshteam/pricing/) (2026). Monthly billing ran about 20% higher at $1.20, $2.40, and $4.80 per employee.

Annual billing rates broke down across all four tiers as follows:

| Plan | Per Employee/Month | Platform Fee/Month | Job Posting Limit | 50-Employee Monthly Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <strong>Free</strong> | $0 | $0 | 3 | $0 |
| <strong>Growth</strong> | $1.00 | $59 | 20 | $109 |
| <strong>Pro</strong> | $2.00 | $99 | 100 | $199 |
| <strong>Enterprise</strong> | $4.00 | $169 | Unlimited | $369 |

Small teams were often caught off guard by the platform fee structure. At the 10-person scale, Growth plan costs broke down to $10 in employee fees and $59 in platform fees - meaning the platform fee accounted for 85% of the total bill. That $69/month works out to $6.90 per employee, not the $1/employee listed on the pricing page.

Larger teams saw more favorable math. 200 employees on Growth paid $200 in employee fees plus $59 in platform fees, bringing the effective per-employee cost down to $1.30/month.

As headcount grew, the platform fee's impact shrank - making the product genuinely affordable for mid-size organizations but misleadingly cheap-looking for small ones.

**Pin's take:** When the Freshteam shutdown was announced in early 2026, the teams reaching out weren't primarily concerned about pricing continuity. They were asking about sourcing. Freshteam's ATS tracked candidates who applied - it never helped teams find candidates who didn't know to apply. In a sample of 52,000,000+ U.S. profiles drawn from Pin's index, just 0.2% show any active job-seeking signal in their headline. The other 99.8% are passive candidates who would never land in an applicant queue, however good the ATS. That's the difference between reactive and proactive recruiting, and it's the capability gap most small teams discover only after they've paid for a year or two of platform fees. Based on conversations with HR teams in early 2026, the biggest regret among Freshteam users wasn't the cost structure. It was spending two or three years managing inbound applications while open roles went unfilled because no one was actively sourcing. If you're evaluating replacements, treat the migration as a chance to close that gap rather than just swap one applicant tracker for another.

### Freshteam Pricing in India (INR)

Freshteam pricing for Indian customers was billed in INR through Freshworks' India entity, following the same four-tier structure (Free, Growth, Pro, Enterprise). Per the Indian SaaS directory [360Quadrants](https://www.360quadrants.com/software/applicant-tracking-systems-ats/freshworks) (2022), Growth cost ₹84 per user per month plus a ₹5,199 monthly platform fee. Pro ran ₹168 per user per month, and Enterprise ₹336 - each with its own platform fee. Those rates mapped almost exactly to the USD pricing at the prevailing ~₹84/USD exchange rate.

| Plan | INR Per User/Month | INR Platform Fee/Month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <strong>Free</strong> | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| <strong>Growth</strong> | ₹84 | ₹5,199 |
| <strong>Pro</strong> | ₹168 | ₹8,799 |
| <strong>Enterprise</strong> | ₹336 | ₹14,999 |

Founded in Chennai, Freshworks maintains significant India operations across its product suite, including Freshteam.

With renewals halted in March 2026, Freshteam pricing in India is no longer available to new or renewing customers. Indian teams on legacy plans face the same April 2027 shutdown deadline as global users. Zoho People and Zoho Recruit are widely recommended INR-priced replacements in the Indian market. Both are developed by Zoho Corporation, another India-headquartered SaaS company, and neither charges platform fees on top of per-user rates.

## What Did Each Freshteam Plan Include?

Freshteam offered four plans across a $0-$4/employee/month range, gating key features like sourcing tools and onboarding workflows behind the Pro and Enterprise tiers, according to [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/166825/Freshteam/) (2026). Basic hiring was functional on the Free tier, but meaningful recruiting automation didn't appear until Pro or higher. Each plan delivered:

**Free (up to 50 employees)**

-   Basic career site with limited branding
-   Employee directory and org chart
-   Time-off management (basic)
-   Candidate email applications
-   3 published job postings at any time
-   Mobile apps for iOS and Android

**Growth ($1/employee + $59/month platform fee)**

-   Everything in Free
-   Recruiting automations (called "Autopilot")
-   Custom hiring pipelines
-   Interview scorecards
-   Customizable career site
-   Up to 20 active job postings
-   2 holiday calendars and time-off policies

**Pro ($2/employee + $99/month platform fee)**

-   Everything in Growth
-   Candidate sourcing Chrome extension
-   Job requisition management
-   Offer management tools
-   Onboarding checklists
-   Talent pool management
-   Up to 100 published jobs
-   Unlimited time-off policies

**Enterprise ($4/employee + $169/month platform fee)**

-   Everything in Pro
-   IP-based access restrictions
-   Dedicated account manager
-   Custom URL with SSL certificate
-   Unlimited onboarding and offboarding checklists
-   Advanced leave reports
-   Custom user roles

One consistent gap across every tier: no plan included payroll. Adding Gusto, Rippling, or another payroll provider was required for any team that needed payroll. Missing payroll was a significant gap for an HR platform marketed to small businesses.

## What Were Freshteam's Hidden Costs and Limitations?

Sticker prices told only part of the story. Several costs and limitations didn't show up on the pricing page but consistently appeared in user reviews on [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/166825/Freshteam/reviews/) and TrustRadius.

**Platform fees dominated small-team bills.** A 10-person startup on the Growth plan paid $69/month total ($10 in employee fees + $59 platform fee) - that's $6.90 per employee, nearly 7x the advertised $1/employee rate. Pro was even worse at small scale: $119/month for 10 people works out to $11.90/employee, not $2. Structurally, the platform fees penalized the very small businesses Freshteam was designed to attract.

**Payroll absent from every tier.** Unlike Gusto or Rippling, Freshteam never included payroll processing, tax filing, or benefits administration. Teams that needed a complete HR stack had to pair Freshteam with a separate payroll provider, adding $6-$22/employee/month on top of Freshteam's costs. A 50-person company saw its $199/month Freshteam Pro bill grow to $499-$1,299/month once payroll was added.

**60-day cancellation notice.** Cancellation required 60 days' written notice before ending a subscription. Missing that window meant another billing cycle. Several Capterra reviewers flagged this as unexpected, especially compared to month-to-month competitors that let you cancel immediately.

**Integrations cost extra.** Beyond Freshworks' own ecosystem, most third-party integrations required paid add-ons. Teams using tools like Slack, Calendly, or external job boards often discovered they needed a higher plan tier or separate integration fees to connect their existing workflows.

**Job posting caps were strict.** Just 3 active postings on the Free plan, bumping to 20 on Growth. Growing companies hiring across multiple departments hit those limits faster than headcount growth alone would have dictated. Only Enterprise offered unlimited postings, at $169/month in platform fees before any per-employee charges.

**Phone support restricted to Enterprise.** Free and Growth customers were limited to email and chat support. Dedicated account management only appeared at the Enterprise level. HR teams dealing with time-sensitive hiring issues consistently flagged the lack of phone support as a pain point on lower tiers.

## Why Did Freshworks Discontinue Freshteam?

Back in 2022, Freshworks quietly moved Freshteam under its Freshservice division and redeployed the engineering team to other products, according to [PeopleMatters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/business/freshworks-to-end-freshteam-hr-product-stop-renewals-from-march-2026-47939) (2025). After that point, meaningful product updates stopped entirely. That "no material business impact" statement from Freshworks tells you how small Freshteam's revenue contribution had become.

Key dates for existing customers:

-   **2022:** Freshteam folded into Freshservice. Development effectively stopped.
-   **March 7, 2026:** All subscription renewals halted. No new customers accepted.
-   **April 2027:** Final cutoff. Remaining customers must migrate to Freshservice for Business Teams or a competitor.

Existing Freshteam users are being pushed toward Freshservice for Business Teams, Freshworks' enterprise service management platform. Freshservice pricing in 2026 runs substantially higher than what Freshteam users were paying - and the product is designed for IT service management first, HR second. Teams that chose Freshteam for its simplicity and low cost are likely to find Freshservice more complex and expensive than what they originally signed up for.

When the discontinuation became public, Freshteam dropped off several top-ATS rankings. [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/166825/Freshteam/) and TechnologyAdvice both removed it from their top-5 ATS lists in 2024, replacing it with actively developed platforms. On [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/freshteam/pricing), the product carries a 3.3 out of 10 rating across 24 reviews - reflecting the frustration of users stuck on a product that stopped improving years ago.
