RecFest USA 2026 runs September 23-24 at Fair Park, The Fairgrounds Nashville, drawing an expected 3,000+ talent acquisition professionals to a two-day outdoor festival across four stage tents. Early bird tickets are $99 per person ([RecFest USA](https://recfest.com/usa/tickets/), 2026). Founded in 2014 outside London by Jamie Leonard, the U.S. spinoff has roughly tripled in size since its inaugural 2023 Nashville year. Audience is described by organizers as in-house TA and HR professionals, though agency recruiters regularly attend.

Weighing RecFest against SHRM Annual, HR Tech Conference, or Transform? Three numbers matter most: a $99 early bird (vs $2,595+ at SHRM), a strict TA-only focus, and a festival format instead of a convention-center feel. Below: the venue, agenda, speakers, ticket logistics, and the sessions a recruiter using AI sourcing tools should put on their schedule.

## When and Where Is RecFest USA 2026?

The 2026 edition is held on **Wednesday, September 23 and Thursday, September 24, 2026** at **Fair Park, The Fairgrounds Nashville, 300 Craighead Street, Nashville, TN 37211** ([RecFest USA](https://recfest.com/usa/), 2026). Day 1 site access opens 8:30am with content running 9:30am to 5:45pm. Day 2 opens 8:30am with content from 9:30am to 3:45pm. On-site parking is $10 cash per day. Expect grass underfoot and tented stages: the venue is an outdoor festival ground, not a convention center.

Nashville has hosted RecFest USA since the inaugural 2023 edition. In 2025, the event moved from Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park to The Fairgrounds, and Fair Park is confirmed for 2026 as well.

## What Is RecFest USA, and How Did It Get Here?

RecFest is the talent acquisition industry's outdoor festival format: 5,000+ attendees at Knebworth Park in the UK and 2,834 in Nashville in 2025, up from roughly 100 at the inaugural East London event in 2014 ([RecFest](https://recfest.com/our-story/), 2024). Stateside, the edition debuted in Nashville in 2023 and has scaled fast. Growth has been steep: ~1,000 attendees per day at the inaugural year, ~2,000 total in 2024, and ~2,834 at the 2025 edition per third-party event database Vendelux. Organizers project 3,000+ for 2026.

Unlike most HR conventions, the format is deliberately unstructured. Tented stages on grass, after-hours networking that has included Nashville Zoo and rooftop parties in past years, and a stated mission to "celebrate as much as it educates" (Jamie Leonard, founder). Past attendees have called it "the Coachella of recruitment" and "ReunionFest" because the same recruiters come back year after year.

To get broader context on what TA leaders are walking into Nashville talking about, watch recurring RecFest host Hung Lee (curator of Recruiting Brainfood). His year-end take covers TA event trends, AI hype, and where leading companies are getting implementation right.

## What's the RecFest USA 2026 Agenda?

Four themed stages run simultaneously across both days, with full speaker and session details typically published in waves between May and August. The official stage breakdown ([RecFest USA Stages](https://recfest.com/usa/stages/), 2026) confirms the structure below. Individual session titles for 2026 have not been published yet, so the descriptions reflect the published stage themes plus what 2025 attendees can expect to recur.

### The four stages

| Stage | Day 1 focus | Day 2 focus | Best fit for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inspire** | High-caliber keynotes; global TA leaders | Multi-year strategy and purpose-driven sessions | TA directors, VPs, CHROs |
| **Disrupt** | TA Tech That Delivers (tools, platforms) | Tech in Practice (client case studies, ROI) | Recruiters evaluating [AI hiring tools](/blog/ai-hiring-tools/) |
| **Unplugged** | Unscripted Leadership (candid panels) | The Talent Engine (internal mobility, retention) | TA managers, talent partners |
| **Innovate** | The New Blueprint (reimagined recruiting) | The Art of Attraction (employer brand, marketing) | Recruitment marketers, EVP leads |

Of the four stages, Disrupt is the most directly relevant for groups shopping for AI sourcing platforms, automated outreach, or interview scheduling tooling. In 2025, roughly 35% of talks covered AI topics including AI agents, AI bias litigation, and skills-based hiring ([Ongig](https://blog.ongig.com/conferences/recfest-nashville-2025/), 2025), and 2026 is on track to push that share higher given the industry trend.

### After-hours and networking

Past editions have run an after-hours track on the Wednesday night, including a Nashville Zoo reception in 2025 and rooftop networking. Vendor activations like the Shaker Green Room (hosted by The Chad and Cheese Podcast) recur each year. Sponsor and exhibitor lists for 2026 had not been published as of April 26, 2026; the 2025 edition listed 60+ technology and service providers.

## Who Should Attend RecFest USA 2026?

Officially, RecFest describes its audience as "in-house TA and HR professionals" ([RecFest USA FAQ](https://recfest.com/usa/faq/), 2026), and the agenda is built around problems in-house teams face: hiring volume, internal mobility, employer brand, and tech evaluation. In practice, agency recruiters do attend (often quietly), but talks are not optimized for staffing-firm economics or commission-structure questions.

Best fit:

- **In-house TA leaders and managers** at companies hiring 50+ roles a year who want a peer-network reset.
- **Recruiters evaluating AI sourcing or recruiting tooling.** The Disrupt stage and exhibitor floor are the pitch.
- **Recruitment marketing and employer brand teams.** The Innovate stage is essentially built for this audience.
- **Solo TA pros at startups** who don't have a team to learn from internally.

Less suited:

- **Agency partners** focused on staffing or contract placement; you can attend but most sessions assume an in-house frame.
- **HR generalists** who need PDC credits or coverage of comp, benefits, and compliance breadth. SHRM Annual fits better.

## How to Prep for RecFest USA 2026

The Fairgrounds Nashville is an open-field venue, not a hotel ballroom, so the prep list looks more like a music festival checklist than a corporate travel itinerary. The official attendee guide ([RecFest](https://recfest.com/our-guide-ultimate-guide-to-be-recfest-ready/), 2025) flags the key items.

A 6-step pre-event checklist:

1. **Book a Nashville hotel by July.** Organizers publish a recommended-hotel list and rates; central Nashville books up around music-festival weekends (which RecFest week often is).
2. **Download the RecFest app.** It's the only way to reserve seats, book vendor meetings, and lock in 1:1 networking before the lineup gets crowded.
3. **Build a per-stage shortlist before you arrive.** Four simultaneous stages means real choices. Pick 2 must-attend sessions per day and treat the rest as discretionary.
4. **Wear comfortable shoes and bring a phone charger.** No cloakrooms; a small cross-body or bum bag is the move.
5. **Block 90 minutes for the exhibitor floor on Day 2.** Vendor demos land harder when you've already seen the case-study sessions on Day 1.
6. **Plan after-hours.** Past years had Nashville Zoo and rooftop receptions. They are part of the value, not a side activity.

If your team has Pin or another AI sourcing platform on the eval shortlist for 2027 budgets, pre-book demos with vendors at the Disrupt stage rather than walking the floor cold. Slots fill up fast.

## How RecFest USA Compares to SHRM, HR Tech, and Transform

RecFest USA costs $99 (early bird), runs in September, and targets practitioner TA teams. That is a different profile from the three other major 2026 U.S. TA events, and the right choice depends on what a team needs. Below: how RecFest stacks up against the other three.

**RecFest USA vs SHRM Annual 2026.** As the largest HR event in the country (25,000+ attendees, broad HR scope, PDC credits), SHRM Annual plays a different role than RecFest, which is one-tenth the size, all-TA, has no PDC credits, and costs less than 4% as much. Choose SHRM for certification credits or compensation breadth. Choose RecFest for a peer network built entirely around recruiting practitioners.

**RecFest USA vs HR Tech Conference.** HR Tech is the vendor-evaluation event of the year (7,200+ attendees, 400+ exhibitors, deep analyst presentations) and runs Oct 20-22 in Las Vegas, just four weeks after RecFest. For procurement cycles and analyst frameworks, HR Tech is the better call. For practitioner sessions and direct peer conversations about what is actually working, RecFest wins.

**RecFest USA vs Transform.** Held March 23-25, 2026 in Las Vegas, Transform targets senior people leaders, CPOs, and the people-tech investor community. That is the strategy event, not the operator event. RecFest is its opposite: optimized for the recruiter doing the daily work.

Several teams attend two of these per year. A common 2026 mix: SHRM Talent in April for skills frameworks, then RecFest in September for platform insights and peer learning.

### When is RecFest USA 2026?

### How much does RecFest USA 2026 cost?

### Who should attend RecFest USA 2026?

Targeting in-house talent acquisition leaders, recruiters, recruitment marketers, and TA managers at companies with active hiring, RecFest builds programming around in-house economics rather than agency commissions. Agency recruiters can attend, but most talks assume an in-house frame. HR generalists who need PDC credits should pick SHRM instead.

Yes, for TA-focused groups under tight conference budgets, RecFest is the most cost-effective national gathering of 2026 at $99 early bird. ### Is RecFest in the UK or USA?

RecFest runs in both. The UK edition is the flagship, held each July at Knebworth Park in Hertfordshire with 5,000+ attendees. The USA edition launched in Nashville in 2023 and runs each September at The Fairgrounds Nashville. The two events share a brand and format (outdoor festival, tented stages, TA-only audience) but have separate lineups, sponsors, and ticket pages. This guide covers the USA edition only.

### What kind of companies attend RecFest?

RecFest USA draws primarily in-house TA teams from mid-to-large enterprises with active hiring pipelines, alongside fast-growing startups that are scaling recruiting for the first time. Past attendee company types have included technology firms, healthcare systems, financial services, retail, and logistics. Staffing agencies and HR generalists attend in smaller numbers; the programming is built for in-house recruiting challenges, not agency or generalist use cases.
