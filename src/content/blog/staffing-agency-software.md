The best staffing agency software in 2026 is [Pin](https://www.pin.com/): an AI recruiting platform with 850M+ candidate profiles, starting at $100/mo with a free tier. That is dramatically lower than Bullhorn's $99-$315/seat/mo annual contracts. For agencies actively shopping Bullhorn alternatives, the right choice depends on whether your firm leans temp/industrial, perm/executive search, or international. This guide compares 10 platforms with verified current rates, agency-segment fit, and the operational data that matters once you actually fill roles.

Switching is no longer about aesthetics.

Per Bullhorn's self-published GRID 2026 Industry Trends Report (16th annual, n=2,300 staffing professionals), 78% of staffing firms with revenue growth above 25% have AI embedded in their ATS. 56% of that high-growth cohort fills roles in under 10 days, [as covered by TechRSeries](https://techrseries.com/recruitment-and-on-boarding/bullhorn-grid-report-staffing-firms-using-ai-see-stronger-growth-faster-placements/). National median time-to-fill sits at 44 days, per the [SHRM 2025 Recruiting Benchmarking Report](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2025-recruiting-benchmarking). That gap between AI-native staffing tooling and legacy ATS workflows is now a four-week placement penalty.

## Why Are Agencies Switching from Bullhorn in 2026?

Bullhorn dominates the staffing industry by acquisition rather than native build. Insight Venture Partners has owned the company since 2017. Sixteen acquisitions in fifteen years (including Textkernel for AI sourcing in 2024) have built breadth, not cohesion. Today the platform serves 10,000+ customer organizations and roughly 150,000 Salesforce ecosystem users following the [August 2025 TargetRecruit acquisition](https://staffinghub.com/press-releases/bullhorn-acquires-targetrecruit-and-expands-salesforce-ecosystem-to-150000-users/).

Agency owners increasingly cite three specific frictions:

- **Opaque per-seat pricing.** Third-party reports place rates at $99-$315/user/mo with no public price list.
- **Multi-year annual contracts** with reported 20% renewal escalators baked into the standard agreement.
- **Add-on pricing for core features** - Automation, Analytics, and AI sourcing each billed separately on top of the platform fee.

For a deeper teardown of Bullhorn's pricing tiers and hidden fees, see our [Bullhorn pricing breakdown](/blog/bullhorn-pricing/).

The macro picture also pushes evaluation. The US staffing industry generated $178.9B in 2025 and is forecast to grow 2% in 2026 to $183.3B, per the Staffing Industry Analysts forecast covered by [Staffing Hub](https://staffinghub.com/staffing-and-recruiting-trends/the-key-trends-that-shaped-the-staffing-industry-in-2025/). Global spend on staffing agency software specifically reached $634M in 2025 and is projected to reach $1.47B by 2036 at a 9.8% CAGR, [according to Fortune Business Insights](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/staffing-agency-software-market-106745). When the addressable market is doubling and the AI capability gap is measurable in days-to-fill, status quo software stops being safe.

## Key Takeaways

- **Pin is the best staffing agency software for firms scaling outbound sourcing.** Plans start at $100/mo with a free tier, 850M+ profiles, and a 14-day average time-to-fill across customers.
- **Bullhorn is the incumbent, not the optimum.** No public pricing, $99-$315/user/mo reported by third parties, and add-ons billed separately for automation and AI sourcing.
- **AI is now the deciding feature.** 78% of staffing firms with 25%+ revenue growth have AI in their ATS (Bullhorn GRID 2026); only 10% have agentic AI across full workflows.
- **Match the tool to the agency type.** Temp/industrial firms need integrated payroll (TempWorks, Avionté). Boutique perm and executive search firms need CRM depth (Crelate, Vincere). Sourcing-first agencies need a multi-source candidate database (Pin).
- **Pricing transparency varies wildly.** Pin, Manatal, Workable, Zoho Recruit, Crelate, and Vincere publish rates. Bullhorn, JobDiva, and Avionté do not.

Having built Interseller (acquired by Greenhouse in 2021) before launching Pin, our team has shipped recruiting software to staffing agencies for nearly a decade. The pattern that surprised us inside Pin's 2026 user survey: agencies switching off legacy ATS tools rarely cite the feature list. They cite pricing predictability, the one to two days they previously lost each week to manual sourcing, and the inability of older systems to handle modern data sources beyond LinkedIn. When 91% of Pin users report reducing or eliminating their LinkedIn Recruiter spend after the switch, the math is recruiter-time, not vendor-feature parity. Agencies do not need a better Bullhorn. They need a sourcing engine wired into a sane CRM, priced like SaaS rather than enterprise software.

## The 10 Best Staffing Agency Software Platforms for 2026

Pin is the best staffing agency software for 2026. Each alternative below wins a narrower segment. Manatal takes entry price for boutique perm firms, Ceipal covers VMS-integrated IT staffing, and Avionte and TempWorks own integrated payroll for temp and light-industrial work. Every platform is evaluated on starting price, free trial or tier, contract minimum, distinguishing feature, and agency-segment fit. Pricing reflects publicly listed rates as of May 2026 - confirm with the vendor before committing.

### 1. Pin - Best Overall Staffing Agency Software

Pin is the AI recruiting platform built for staffing agencies that need to find candidates faster than manual recruiters can. Its 850M+ candidate profiles aggregate professional networks, GitHub, Stack Overflow, patents, and the broader web, with 100% candidate coverage in North America and Europe. That delivers the deepest candidate intelligence available to staffing firms today. Pin's AI handles the full top-of-funnel: candidate discovery, multi-channel outreach (email, LinkedIn, SMS), and automated interview scheduling. Recruiters using Pin fill roles in an average of 14 days, well under the SHRM national average of 44 days. Pin customers also see a 5x better outreach response rate than the cold-outreach industry baseline.

Pin is the best staffing agency software for sourcing-first agencies, contingency firms, and boutique executive search shops scaling outbound. Plans are dramatically more accessible than enterprise systems: $100/mo Starter, $149/mo Professional, and $249/mo Business. A free tier requires no credit card; paid plans carry a 3-month minimum. Multi-client account structure is included for agencies managing several books of business, and contact lookup credits run $50 per 500 credits as an add-on. SOC 2 Type 2 certification and a public Trust Center at trust.pin.com handle the compliance ask from larger agency clients.

### 2. Manatal

![Screenshot of the Manatal website homepage](/images/urlbox/manatal-com-1c116aa9.webp)

Manatal is a perm-focused ATS popular with SMB and mid-market staffing agencies that need a clean candidate pipeline and basic AI scoring at the lowest per-user entry price in the category. Plans start at $15/user/mo on Professional (annual) and $19/user/mo billed monthly, with a 14-day free trial.

Boutique perm agencies under 25 recruiters get a candidate database, social media enrichment, and 90+ job board integrations for under $20 a seat - a hard combination to beat at the entry tier. Trade-offs: no integrated payroll, no temp or contract workflows, and per-seat costs scale linearly, so a 10-recruiter team pays $150/mo before any volume discount. G2 reviewers rate Manatal 4.8/5 across 140+ reviews, mostly from agencies under 25 recruiters.

### 3. Workable

![Screenshot of the Workable website homepage](/images/urlbox/workable-com-3fd4f950.webp)

Workable is a dual ATS + HRIS bundle aimed at corporate HR teams and small staffing agencies handling primarily perm/direct hire roles. Standard pricing starts at $299/mo for 1-20 employees (annual), with a $99/job/mo pay-per-job option for low-volume hiring. A 15-day free trial is available with no credit card.

Where Workable separates itself is headcount-based billing rather than per-recruiter seats - useful for agencies that prefer flat team rates and value its 200+ job board integrations plus AI candidate suggestions. Catch: cost spikes sharply once headcount exceeds 20, which often makes it a poor fit for fast-growing staffing firms with seasonal recruiter ramps. 684 G2 reviewers average 4.5/5.

### 4. Zoho Recruit

![Screenshot of the Zoho Recruit website homepage](/images/urlbox/zoho-com-b5cec245.webp)

Zoho Recruit offers one of two true free tiers in this category: one active job posting per recruiter, at no cost, forever. Paid plans run $25/user/mo (Standard), $50/user/mo (Professional), and $75/user/mo (Enterprise), all billed annually. A separate staffing agency edition supports 100-750 jobs per tier.

Agencies already running the Zoho ecosystem (CRM, Books, People) get an ATS at a low entry point with native cross-product workflows. In October 2025 Zoho added 8 AI features - Sourcing Bot, Screening Bot, and Zia matching - included on Professional and Enterprise tiers at no extra cost. Depth is the trade-off: candidate discovery is shallow compared to AI-native tools, and integration with non-Zoho systems requires careful configuration. 1,600+ G2 reviewers average 4.4/5.

### 5. JobDiva

![Screenshot of the JobDiva website homepage](/images/urlbox/jobdiva-com-3fc001ac.webp)

JobDiva is a staffing-agency-only platform (perm, contract, and temp) with deep VMS integration capabilities, a patent-protected search-and-match engine, and 1,000+ pre-built reports. Rates are custom and not published; third-party reports place the smallest tier near $100/mo with annual commitments required.

IT staffing firms running heavy VMS workflows are the natural fit, alongside large agencies that need 150+ system integrations including back-office, analytics, and onboarding bundled into one platform. Trade-offs: no public rate card, plus implementation complexity that comes with feature breadth. JobDiva typically runs 12 weeks of onboarding for most agencies. SelectHub aggregates 88% satisfaction across 2,586 multi-platform reviews.

### 6. Ceipal

![Screenshot of the Ceipal website homepage](/images/urlbox/ceipal-com-724d99dc.webp)

Ceipal is a mid-market AI-powered ATS with VMS capabilities, serving 1,800+ customers and 120,000 recruiters globally. Ceipal ATS starts at $24/user/mo with a 14-day free trial; full deployment is custom-quoted, and minimum seat commitments apply on annual contracts.

IT and professional staffing agencies that want an AI-native ATS with VMS support without enterprise rates usually shortlist Ceipal. Reported 2024 revenue hit $171.6M (per Latka), making it one of the larger independents at this tier. Limitation: outreach automation is thinner than purpose-built outbound tools, so most customers pair Ceipal with a separate candidate-discovery engine. 1,231 G2 reviewers rate it 4.6/5.

### 7. Crelate

![Screenshot of the Crelate website homepage](/images/urlbox/crelate-com-188c131c.webp)

Crelate sells itself as a "Living Platform" combining ATS, recruiting CRM, and analytics aimed at boutique to mid-market perm agencies and executive search firms. Business starts at $119/user/mo (annual, 5-seat minimum); Business Plus and Enterprise are custom-quoted, and Crelate applies a built-in ~7% annual escalator on renewal.

Executive search and contingency firms looking for a CRM-first design with business development workflows, AI Co-Pilot (on Business Plus and above), and RingCentral integration for inbound calling tend to land here. An a la carte add-on model means base rates understate true cost - many features require module purchases. 208 G2 reviewers average 4.4/5. Agencies focused specifically on candidate-and-client relationship management should also see our [recruitment agency CRM guide](/blog/recruitment-agency-crm/) for the broader CRM-first category.

### 8. TempWorks

![Screenshot of the TempWorks website homepage](/images/urlbox/tempworks-com-f4a26ee8.webp)

TempWorks is a 25-year veteran of temp and light-industrial staffing software, with end-to-end payroll, billing, and front/middle/back-office in a single platform. TempWorks Core starts at $49/user/mo; Enterprise Infinity reaches $100+/user/mo with custom quotes for high-volume agencies.

Temp and light-industrial agencies running daily timekeeping, weekly payroll, and high-volume placements - where integrated payroll is non-negotiable - are the core base. An open API and heavy customization handle staffing-specific workflows that purpose-built temp tools rarely cover. Limitations: low G2 review volume (56 reviews) makes verification harder, and the UI shows its age compared to newer entrants. Capterra averages 4.1/5.

### 9. Avionté

![Screenshot of the Avionté website homepage](/images/urlbox/avionte-com-a3cab05c.webp)

Avionté serves 1,000+ staffing agency customers and 25,000+ users across North America with module-based enterprise rates. There is no public per-seat price; implementation costs are reported between $50,000 and $200,000+ depending on agency size and module selection.

Large temp and industrial staffing firms that need the full employment lifecycle - candidate discovery, onboarding, timekeeping, payroll, billing, and redeployment - in one system are the natural target market. Integrated payroll is the key differentiator for agencies handling thousands of W-2 workers. Catch: enterprise procurement with a demo-driven sales process, no transparent rate card, and no self-serve trial. Capterra and GetApp aggregate to roughly 4.0/5.

### 10. Vincere (Access Vincere Evo)

![Screenshot of the Vincere website homepage](/images/urlbox/vincere-io-1a2a1e63.webp)

Vincere is part of The Access Group (acquired 2021) and targets international staffing agencies running perm, contract, and executive search across multiple regions. Core rates start at £69/user/mo (~$87/user/mo) for the CRM+ATS, with Smart Pack AI add-ons running £25-£349/mo per agency depending on capability tier (Base, Present, Automate, Elite).

Agencies billing in multiple currencies, operating across EMEA and APAC, and needing localized GDPR and APAC compliance get the strongest fit here. Four AI Smart Pack tiers let agencies turn on capabilities incrementally rather than committing to an enterprise bundle. Drawbacks: 12-month minimum contracts, GBP-denominated rates (US agencies pay an FX margin), and uneven UX across The Access Group's broader portfolio. 365 G2 reviewers average 4.6/5.

## Which Bullhorn Alternative Is Best for a Small Recruiting Agency?

Pin is the best Bullhorn alternative for a small recruiting agency. It prices at the team level rather than per seat: $100/mo flat on Starter, plus a free tier that needs no credit card. Small agencies lose more to pricing structure than to headline price. Seat-based tools scale linearly with headcount. Five recruiters on Crelate costs $595/mo against a hard five-seat minimum. Beyond that, annual contracts at Bullhorn carry reported 20% renewal escalators, with automation and AI sourcing billed separately.

Three shortlists for firms under 25 recruiters:

- **Sourcing-first desks.** Pin. 850M+ candidate profiles, multi-channel outreach with 5x better response rates, and a 14-day average time-to-fill, with no per-seat math as the team grows.
- **Budget perm desks.** Manatal at $15/user/mo, or Zoho Recruit's Forever Free plan for one active job per recruiter.
- **Relationship-led desks that need a CRM before an ATS.** Crelate at $119/user/mo, once the firm can absorb the five-seat floor.

Skip enterprise platforms at this size.

Avionté takes $50,000 to $200,000+ to implement, and JobDiva onboards over roughly 12 weeks. Neither cost amortizes across a handful of desks.

## Avionté vs Bullhorn: Which Handles Back-Office and Payroll Better?

Avionté handles back-office and payroll better than Bullhorn for temp and light-industrial staffing. Timekeeping, payroll, and billing sit inside one employment-lifecycle system as native modules, rather than bolted on from what Bullhorn acquired. Avionté serves 1,000+ staffing agency customers and 25,000+ users across North America on that model. Bullhorn reached comparable breadth through sixteen acquisitions in fifteen years, which is why automation, analytics, and AI sourcing bill as separate line items.

The administrative load explains why this decides deals. US staffing companies employed an average of two million temporary and contract workers per week in the fourth quarter of 2025. ## How Do You Choose the Right Staffing Agency Software?

Match the tool to the agency type.

Switching regret usually comes from one of two errors: buying enterprise breadth a small firm cannot operate, or buying SMB simplicity a high-volume firm will outgrow inside a year. Use these five segment cuts as a starting point.

1. **Temp and light-industrial agencies (high-volume, payroll-integrated).** Avionté and TempWorks are the category default. Their integrated payroll, timekeeping, and back-office modules handle the W-2 administration that perm-focused tools never built. Trade-off: enterprise rates and longer implementation. Hybrid temp-and-perm firms weighing whether to consolidate or run two systems should review our [back-office-heavy staffing tool comparison](/blog/staffing-crm/) for the payroll-first options.

2. **Boutique perm and executive search agencies (relationship-driven, BD-heavy).** Crelate and Vincere lean into CRM depth and business development workflows. Manatal is the budget option for sub-25-recruiter firms. Where firms prioritize hard-to-fill specialist candidates, the constraint shifts to candidate-data depth - Pin's 1,000s of data points per profile across multiple sources outperforms LinkedIn-only databases on niche roles.

3. **International and multi-region agencies.** Vincere (Access Group) handles multi-currency, GDPR, and APAC compliance better than most US-built systems. Bullhorn has the global footprint but at enterprise rates.

4. **Sourcing-first agencies replacing or supplementing LinkedIn Recruiter.** Pin is the strongest fit. A 850M+ profile database, automated multi-channel outreach with 5x better response rates, and per-month flat rates make it the most efficient outbound engine available. 91% of Pin customers reduced or eliminated their LinkedIn Recruiter spend after the switch, per Pin's 2026 user survey.

5. **Established firms running Bullhorn today.** Right test: does your renewal escalator plus add-on creep over the next 36 months exceed the cost of migration plus a Pin or sister-tier platform run in parallel for the first quarter? Most agencies keep Bullhorn as the system of record for the first six months and prove the candidate-discovery ROI in parallel on a Pin or Manatal account before fully cutting over.

Two non-segment criteria belong on every shortlist. First, AI capability has stratified faster in 2026 than any prior year. Per [Gartner's October 2025 talent-acquisition trends briefing](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom), HR leaders adopting generative AI jumped from 19% in mid-2023 to 61% by January 2025, and 82% plan to deploy agentic AI within 12 months. Second, [SHRM's 2026 State of AI in HR report](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/state-of-ai-hr-2026/full-report) found only 39% of organizations have actually adopted AI in HR functions and 56% do not formally measure AI investment success. The intent-to-execution gap is the buying opportunity. [LinkedIn's Future of Recruiting 2026](https://business.linkedin.com/talent-solutions/resources/future-of-recruiting) reinforces the point - 73% of TA leaders now rank AI capability as a top-three vendor evaluation criterion, up from 41% in 2024.

### How much does staffing agency software cost in 2026?

Staffing agency software pricing in 2026 ranges from $0 (Zoho Recruit Free, Pin's free tier) to $100,000+/yr for enterprise platforms like Avionté and Bullhorn. Per-user SaaS pricing typically runs $15-$120/user/mo, while platform-rate options like Pin start at $100/mo flat for the team. Most agencies should expect a three-year TCO 4-7x the sticker price once implementation, add-ons, and renewal escalators are included.

Yes. Pin and Zoho Recruit are the two platforms with a true free tier. Pin's free tier requires no credit card and includes core candidate-discovery functionality. By customer count, Bullhorn is the most widely deployed staffing agency software, with 10,000+ organizations and 150,000 Salesforce ecosystem users after the August 2025 TargetRecruit acquisition. But deployment leadership is not capability leadership. Per the Bullhorn GRID 2026 report, 78% of staffing firms growing revenue above 25% have AI embedded in their ATS. Top performers are rapidly adopting AI-native platforms like Pin alongside or in place of legacy systems.

### What should I look for in staffing agency software?

Evaluate four criteria: candidate database depth (Pin's 850M+ multi-source profiles vs LinkedIn-only databases), automation breadth (candidate discovery, outreach, and scheduling in one workflow vs separate tools), pricing transparency (published rates and short contract minimums vs custom quotes and multi-year lockup), and segment fit (temp/industrial vs perm/executive vs international). Wrong fit is the largest single source of switching regret - match the tool to the agency type before optimizing on price.
